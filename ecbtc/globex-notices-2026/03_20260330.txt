## Critical Updates

### iLink SBE Schema Version 8 Decommission - April 24

On Sunday, February 22, CME Group completed the launch of iLink SBE Schema version 9 on all futures and options on futures market segments. CME Globex is now only sending version 9 messages for all futures and options on futures markets.

Client systems can send iLink messages using version 8 or 9 until the end of day **Friday, April 24**, after which only version 9 messages will be accepted.

### Cryptocurrency Futures and Options Expansion to 24/7 Trading - May 29

Starting at 4:00 p.m. Central Time on**Friday, May 29,** CME Group will expand [Cryptocurrency futures and options](https://www.cmegroup.com/markets/cryptocurrencies.html) to 24/7 trading.

Cryptocurrency futures and options will trade continuously on CME Globex with at least a two-hour weekly maintenance period over the weekend. All holiday or weekend trading from Friday evening through Sunday evening will have a trade date of the following business day, with clearing, settlement and regulatory reporting processed the following business day as well.

A [Cryptocurrency Futures and Options Expansion to 24/7 Overview](https://www.cmegroup.com/markets/cryptocurrencies/files/client-facing-cryptocurrency-futures-and-options-expansion-to-24-7-trading.pdf) is now available for high-level information on the initiative. Please review the 24/7 cryptocurrency [client impact assessment](https://cmegroupclientsite.atlassian.net/wiki/spaces/EPICSANDBOX/pages/1283194884/Cryptocurrency+Futures+and+Options+Migration+to+24-7+Trading) for full technical analysis and comprehensive details.

### Globex Trade Date Behavior Change - June 19

Effective **Friday,June 19**, CME Group is implementing the following change to align CME Globex and CME Clearing trade dates when the Globex trade date falls on a Friday U.S. holiday.

All CME Globex futures and options trades executed on a Friday U.S. holiday will now reflect a Globex trade date to align with the next valid Clearing House trade date.

### iLink Mass Quote Protection Session Linking - Q2 2026

In**Q2 2026**,CME Group will introduce the new Mass Quote Protection (MQP) Session Linking functionality for all CME Globex options on futures.

This enhancement provides comprehensive risk mitigation by allowing firms to link multiple iLink sessions under the same CME Globex Firm ID (GFID). When an MQP event is triggered,[protection actions](https://cmegroupclientsite.atlassian.net/wiki/spaces/EPICSANDBOX/pages/457606136/Mass+Quote+Protections)are automatically extended across all associated sessions within the linked group.

Please review the[client impact assessment](https://cmegroupclientsite.atlassian.net/wiki/display/EPICSANDBOX/Mass+Quote+Protection+Session+Linking)for full technical details, configuration rules, and launch schedule.

The new MQP Session Linking will be available in New Release for customer testing on this**Thursday, April 2**.

Certification is not required to use the new MQP Session Linking functionality.

### Position Reporting Under CFTC Part 17

On April 30, 2024, the Commodity Futures Trading Commission ("CFTC") approved final rules to amend its [Large Trading Reporting Regulations for Futures and Options](https://www.cftc.gov/PressRoom/PressReleases/8902-24). The amendments were made to modernize CFTC's Large Trader Reporting process and align it with other reporting structures outlined in CFTC regulations.

Since October 2024, CME Group has offered methods to retrieve its instrument listing containing the Unique Instrument Code (UIC) and other instrument data elements in compliance with the amended rule.

Starting March 10, 2025, CME Group began accepting test files in the new Position Message FIXML format via SFTP. For details regarding submitting the new FIXML Position Message, file management, field validations, and example scenarios, please refer to the information in the[CME Group Client Systems Wiki](https://cmegroupclientsite.atlassian.net/wiki/spaces/EPICSANDBOX/pages/457216877/Position+Reporting+Under+CFTC+Part+17).

The Market Regulation Department will be publishing further guidance regarding Large Trader Reporting in subsequent Special Executive Reports as solutions become available.

CFTC's final rules became effective upon publication in the Federal Register on June 3, 2024. Reporting Firms must comply with the CFTC's final rules regarding Large Trader Reporting Requirements two years after publication in the Federal Register.

### CME Globex Firm ID Excessive Messaging Threshold Modifications Per Second Addition - This Week

On**Tuesday, March 31** (trade date Wednesday, April 1), in response to customer behavior specific to excessive order modification within product group SS (SOFR futures), the CME Globex Messaging Efficiency Program for CME Globex Firm IDs Excessive Messaging Threshold (EMT) was revised to include a Modifications Per Second (MPS) parameter of more than 2,000.

EMT MPS now tracks customer order modifications per second, by Globex Firm ID (GFID), within product group SS (SOFR futures). Additionally, CME Group also reserves the right at its discretion to aggregate CME Globex firms for purposes of determining whether product group SS-SOFR futures EMT GFID benchmark as part of the EMT MPS has been exceeded in circumstances where a single entity is sending in messages via more than one execution firm number. In such cases, CME Group will advise the participant accordingly.

Each breach of this new MPS parameter (more than 2,000) will be eligible for a potential $5,000 surcharge.

Please refer to the [CME Globex Messaging Efficiency Program](https://www.cmegroup.com/globex/files/2024-revised-mep.pdf) for additional information.

Contact the [CME Global Command Center](mailto:gcc@cmegroup.com) in the U.S. at +1 800 438 8616, in Europe at +44 20 7623 4747, in Japan: +81 3 6733 7559, and in Asia at +65 6532 5010, with any questions.

### CME Globex Connectivity Upgrades and Changes for Listed Derivative Markets - June 27

In response to client feedback and to provide firms with additional time to prepare, CME Group extended the deadline for the previously announced network equipment upgrades.

Starting **Saturday**, **June 27**, CME Group plans to replace End-of-Life network equipment that supports listed derivatives connectivity at [CME Globex Hubs](https://cmegroup.com/solutions/market-access/globex/connectivity-options.html#overview). Customers will be notified in advance if they are impacted by an equipment upgrade.

CME Globex market data for listed derivatives now regularly exceeds 1Gbps, increasing the risk of packet loss, performance concerns, and quality of service degradations on 1Gbps connections.

**Impact on 1Gbps Listed Derivatives Connections**

*   Multicast Market Data: Effective **June 27**, 1Gbps services will no longer support MDP 3.0 multicast market data.
*   Existing 1Gbps Customers with Market Data: Customers with 1Gbps service currently listening to multicast market data are encouraged to upgrade connections to 10Gbps as soon as possible.
*   Existing 1Gbps Customers without Market Data: Unicast services such as iLink, Drop Copy, STP, MQ and others will continue to be supported on 1Gbps connections. Customers with 1Gbps connectivity who are not receiving Market Data will have their service migrated to the new equipment.
*   New 1Gbps Connections: New connections for 1Gbps service do not support access to listed derivatives multicast market data.

**Copper Handoffs**

*   When the CME Globex Connectivity upgrades are rolled out in 2026, CME Group will no longer support copper handoffs.

Additional details about the CME Globex Connectivity upgrades will be announced in future CME Globex Notices.
