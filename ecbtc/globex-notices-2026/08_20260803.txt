## Critical Updates

### MBP and MBOFD Market Recovery - This Week

Starting this **Sunday, August 9** (trade date Monday, August 10), CME Globex will enhance [MBP and MBOFD Market Recovery](https://cmegroupclientsite.atlassian.net/wiki/spaces/EPICSANDBOX/pages/457672425/MDP+3.0+-+MBP+and+MBOFD+Market+Recovery) functionality.

To better support client recovery, MDP and MDOFD market recovery will no longer reset [daily statistics](https://cmegroupclientsite.atlassian.net/wiki/spaces/EPICSANDBOX/pages/457325864/MDP+3.0+-+Daily+Statistics) after a [channel reset message](https://cmegroupclientsite.atlassian.net/wiki/spaces/EPICSANDBOX/pages/457422951/MDP+3.0+-+Market+Data+Incremental+Refresh+-+Channel+Reset) is sent. Consequently, client systems should not reset the last received daily statistics with a channel reset.

Daily statistics values will be available in the market recovery feed after the channel reset is processed for instruments that have working orders or have traded during the current session. Refer to the [Channel Reset Guide](https://www.cmegroup.com/globex/files/mbp-mbofd-market-recovery-update.pdf) for a list of affected channels and timelines.

These changes are currently available for customer testing in New Release.

### New New Inter-Commodity Ratio Spread and Spread Type Change - September 13

Effective **Sunday, September 13** (trade date Monday, September 14), additional exchange-defined Inter-Commodity Ratio spreads will be made available for trading on CME Globex. The spreads will utilize a new strategy type (**EA**).

The EA spread is the simultaneous purchase/sale of an inter-commodity spread between two products, namely between Nikkei/USD (NKD) and Nikkei/Yen (NIY)) with different pre-determined leg ratios. The Exchange currently avails inter-commodity spreads for the Contracts on CME Globex with a ratio of 1:1. Effective **Sunday, September 13**, additional inter-commodity spread ratio functionality will be available per the[client impact assessment](https://cmegroupclientsite.atlassian.net/wiki/display/EPICSANDBOX/New+Inter-Commodity+Ratio+Spread).

### Position Reporting Under CFTC Part 17

On April 30, 2024, the Commodity Futures Trading Commission ("CFTC") approved final rules to amend its [Large Trading Reporting Regulations for Futures and Options](https://www.cftc.gov/PressRoom/PressReleases/8902-24). The amendments were made to modernize CFTC's Large Trader Reporting process and align it with other reporting structures outlined in CFTC regulations.

Since October 2024, CME Group has offered methods to retrieve its instrument listing containing the Unique Instrument Code (UIC) and other instrument data elements in compliance with the amended rule.

Starting March 10, 2025, CME Group began accepting test files in the new Position Message FIXML format via SFTP. For details regarding submitting the new FIXML Position Message, file management, field validations, and example scenarios, please refer to the information in the[CME Group Client Systems Wiki](https://cmegroupclientsite.atlassian.net/wiki/spaces/EPICSANDBOX/pages/457216877/Position+Reporting+Under+CFTC+Part+17).

CFTC's final rules became effective upon publication in the Federal Register on June 3, 2024. Subject to the conditions of the January 27, 2026, no-action letter, reporting firms must comply with these CFTC regulations by **July 26, 2027.**

A test environment for Large Trader Reporting is now available within the Firm Regulatory Portal (FRP UT). Users can access the testing portal at: [https://frp.uat.cmegroup.com](https://frp.uat.cmegroup.com/).

A brief [video demonstration](https://www.cmegroup.com/articles/2026/large-trader-position-reporting-in-firm-regulatory-portal.html) is available to highlight the new look and functionality of the FRP UT.

The Market Regulation Department will be publishing further guidance regarding Large Trader Reporting in subsequent Special Executive Reports as solutions become available.

### RepoFunds Rate Select20 - Now Available

On**Monday**, **August 3**, CME Group Benchmark Administration Limited (CBA) launched RepoFunds Rate Select20 Indices (RFR Select20) on the CME Benchmark Administration Premium market data channel.

The RFR Select20 are a series of one-day repo indices designed to measure the on-day cost of secured funding based on sovereign debt of selected countries. The RFR Select20 indices leverage an enhanced filtering method relative to the primary RepoFunds Rate (RFR) benchmark indices to create a rate more representative of the general collateral rate.

RFR France, Germany and Italy Select20 will be published by 5:00 p.m. Greenwich Mean Time (GMT)/British Summer Time (BST) on BrokerTec EU business days, and RFR Sterling Select20 will be published by 5:00 p.m. GMT/BST on BrokerTec UK Gilt business days.

### Messaging Efficiency Program Changes - This Week

Effective on trade date this **Monday, August 3,** the Messaging Efficiency Program (MEP) futures and options program benchmarks for product groups Gold Futures-GC and Silver Futures-SI will be increased to 45:1 and 60:1, respectively. This change will encourage responsible messaging while recognizing broader changes to market conditions.

The Excessive Messaging Threshold (EMT) for both CME Globex firms and iLink session IDs remains in place for all futures and options on futures products that trade on CME Globex. Refer to pages 12-14 in the [CME Globex Messaging Efficiency Program](https://www.cmegroup.com/globex/files/2024-revised-mep.pdf) for EMT-related information.

The [CME Globex Messaging Efficiency Program](https://www.cmegroup.com/globex/files/2024-revised-mep.pdf) creates fair business guidelines by which customers are billed a surcharge for overly high message rates.

CME Globex firms who exceed the benchmark ratios in applicable product groups and are enrolled accordingly, receive email notifications of any potential surcharges. CME Globex firms who have access to the Firm Administrator Dashboard have the ability to view their messaging statistics on a T+1 basis.
