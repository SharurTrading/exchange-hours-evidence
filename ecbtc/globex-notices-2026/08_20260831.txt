## Critical Updates

### 100-Ounce Silver Futures Migration and Expansion to 24/7 Trading - September 11

Effective **Friday, September 11**, CME Group will expand 100-Ounce Silver futures to [24/7 Trading](https://cmegroupclientsite.atlassian.net/wiki/spaces/EPICSANDBOX/pages/1553563649/24-7+Technical+Market+Functionality).Production 100-Ounce Silver futures weekend trading will begin at 4:30 p.m. Central Time.

The summary of changes are as follows:

*   New Security Group code (tag 1151-Security Group): 4S
*   Migration of 100-Ounce Silver futures from CME MDP 3.0 channel 360 to existing channel 329.
*   Migration of 100-Ounce Silver futures Market segment (tag 1300-MarketSegmentID) from 78 to 74.
*   Block trades will no longer be supported for 100-Ounce Silver futures.

100-Ounce Silver futures will trade continuously on CME Globex with at least a two-hour weekly maintenance period over the weekend. All holiday or weekend trading from Friday evening through Sunday evening will have a trade date of the following business day, with clearing, settlement and regulatory reporting processed the following business day as well.

**Please Note:**For the first day of 24/7 100-Ounce Silver futures weekend trading on **Friday, September 11**, the standard daily maintenance will be extended from two minutes to 30 minutes, from 4:00 p.m. to 4:30 p.m. CT. All subsequent Friday trading sessions will open at their regular time of 4:02 p.m. CT.

Please review the 100-Ounce Silver futures [client impact assessment](https://cmegroupclientsite.atlassian.net/wiki/display/EPICSANDBOX/100-Ounce+Silver+Futures+Expansion+to+24-7+Trading) for full technical analysis and comprehensive migration details.

These futures are currently available for customer testing in New Release.

These contracts are listed with, and subject to, the rules and regulations of COMEX.

### New Inter-Commodity Ratio Spread and Asset Tag Change - September 13

Effective **Sunday, September 13** (trade date Monday, September 14), additional exchange-defined Inter-Commodity Ratio spreads will be made available for trading on CME Globex. The spreads will utilize a new strategy type (**EA**).

The EA spread is the simultaneous purchase/sale of an inter-commodity spread between two products, namely between Nikkei/USD (NKD) and Nikkei/Yen (NIY)) with different pre-determined leg ratios. The Exchange currently avails inter-commodity spreads for the Contracts on CME Globex with a ratio of 1:1. Effective **Sunday, September 13**, additional inter-commodity spread ratio functionality will be available per the[client impact assessment](https://cmegroupclientsite.atlassian.net/wiki/display/EPICSANDBOX/New+Inter-Commodity+Ratio+Spread).

Please review the [client impact assessment](https://cmegroupclientsite.atlassian.net/wiki/display/EPICSANDBOX/New+Inter-Commodity+Ratio+Spread) for detailed spread construction and pricing examples.

In conjunction with this new spread, the existing Nikkei/USD vs. Nikkei/Yen spread will be changing as follows:

| Product Name | Current MDP 3.0 TAG 6937-Asset | New MDP 3.0 TAG 6937 -Asset | iLink/MDP 3.0 TAG 1151-SecurityGroup | Leg Ratio | MDP 3.0 Market Data Channel |
| Nikkei/USD (NKD) vs. Nikkei/Yen (NIY) Ratio | NKD | NK1N11 | N1 | 1:1 | 318 |

### Resting Order Elimination: Livestock Futures and Options Migration to New Market Segments and MDP Channels - September 13

Effective **Sunday, September 13** (trade date Monday, September 14), CME Group is launching new, dedicated Market Segments and Market Data Platform (MDP) Channels on-premise to segregate Livestock markets from the rest of the Agricultural complex.

This migration is part of CME Group preparation for Livestock markets migration to Google Cloud in Q4 2026, launching on-premise to segregate Livestock markets from the rest of the Agricultural complex.

Clients will need to connect to new multicast channels for market data, and the new Market Segment Gateways (MSGW) and Drop Copy for MSGW instances for access to Livestock listed derivative markets.

After the Livestock markets close on the **Friday** before migration, all resting Good Till Cancel (GTC) and Good Till Date (GTD) orders on User-Defined Spreads (UDS) will be eliminated and all UDS will be allowed to expire. GTC and GTD orders on exchange defined instruments and spreads will be persisted and will migrate to the new Segments along with the instruments.

These changes are currently available for customer testing in New Release.

These new market segments will be available in the Sandbox-Dallas environment before the Production-Dallas launch. The launch date will be announced in future CME Globex Notices.

Further details on these changes, including new networking information, are now available in the [Client Systems Wiki](https://cmegroupclientsite.atlassian.net/wiki/display/EPICSANDBOX/Livestock+Futures+and+Options+Migration+to+Production-Dallas).

### Treasury Link Spreads on CME Globex - Q4 2026

In **Q4 2026**, pending regulatory review, CME Group will introduce Treasury Link (cash versus futures) spreads for trading on CME Globex.

This offering represents a price differential between BrokerTec U.S. Treasury Actives offered on BrokerTec Chicago and CBOT U.S. Treasury futures, facilitating the simultaneous and atomic execution of both legs within a single transaction, eliminating legging risk.

Treasury Link spreads will be listed with 1 to 1 Ratio and DV01 Weighted (X:Y) Ratio. For more information, view the [Treasury Link Spreads - Product Specifications](https://cmegroupclientsite.atlassian.net/wiki/display/EPICSANDBOX/Treasury+Link+Spreads+on+CME+Globex#Treasury-Link-Spreads---Product-Specification).

Please review the [client impact assessment](https://cmegroupclientsite.atlassian.net/wiki/display/EPICSANDBOX/Treasury+Link+Spreads+on+CME+Globex) for connectivity and configuration information, technical details and launch schedules.

Use your CME Group Login to view market data multicast IP and port details on the [Connectivity and Configuration Details](https://www.cmegroup.com/solutions/market-access/globex/globex-resources.html#tlmdcd) page.

These products will be available for customer testing in New Release on **Monday, October 5**.

### Position Reporting Under CFTC Part 17

On April 30, 2024, the Commodity Futures Trading Commission ("CFTC") approved final rules to amend its [Large Trading Reporting Regulations for Futures and Options](https://www.cftc.gov/PressRoom/PressReleases/8902-24). The amendments were made to modernize CFTC's Large Trader Reporting process and align it with other reporting structures outlined in CFTC regulations.

Since October 2024, CME Group has offered methods to retrieve its instrument listing containing the Unique Instrument Code (UIC) and other instrument data elements in compliance with the amended rule.

Starting March 10, 2025, CME Group began accepting test files in the new Position Message FIXML format via SFTP. For details regarding submitting the new FIXML Position Message, file management, field validations, and example scenarios, please refer to the information in the[CME Group Client Systems Wiki](https://cmegroupclientsite.atlassian.net/wiki/spaces/EPICSANDBOX/pages/457216877/Position+Reporting+Under+CFTC+Part+17).

CFTC's final rules became effective upon publication in the Federal Register on June 3, 2024. Subject to the conditions of the January 27, 2026, no-action letter, reporting firms must comply with these CFTC regulations by **July 26, 2027.**

A test environment for Large Trader Reporting is now available within the Firm Regulatory Portal (FRP UT). Users can access the testing portal at: [https://frp.uat.cmegroup.com](https://frp.uat.cmegroup.com/).

A brief [video demonstration](https://www.cmegroup.com/articles/2026/large-trader-position-reporting-in-firm-regulatory-portal.html) is available to highlight the new look and functionality of the FRP UT.

The Market Regulation Department will be publishing further guidance regarding Large Trader Reporting in subsequent Special Executive Reports as solutions become available.

### CME CF Cryptocurrency Pricing Data: CME CF Crypto Market Index and Emerging Crypto Index - Now Available

The pricing data is offered via streamlined [CME CF Cryptocurrency Pricing Market Data feed on Channels 213 and 214](https://www.cmegroup.com/confluence/display/EPICSANDBOX/SBE+-+Streamlined+Market+Data+for+CME+CF+Cryptocurrency+Indices).

The feed publishes the following:

*   **Real-Time Indices**to be published daily approximately once every second:
    *   CME CF Crypto Market Index - Real Time (CRYPTORT)
    *   CME CF Emerging Crypto Index - Real Time (ECIRT)

*   Settlements to be published shortly after **4 p.m. London time** each day, including weekends and bank holidays:
    *   CME CF Crypto Market Index - London Settlement (CRYPTORR)
    *   CME CF Emerging Crypto Index - London Settlement (ECIRR)

*   Settlements to be published shortly after **4 p.m. Eastern Time** each day, including weekends and bank holidays:
    *   CME CF Crypto Market Index - New York Settlement (CRYPTONY)
    *   CME CF Emerging Crypto Index - New York Settlement (ECINY)

*   Settlements to be published shortly after **4 p.m. Hong Kong Time/Singapore Time** each day, including weekends and bank holidays:
    *   CME CF Crypto Market Index - APAC Settlement (CRYPTOAP)
    *   CME CF Emerging Crypto Index - APAC Settlement (ECIAP)

**Please Note:**These indices will not be used in the settlement of any contracts.

The new cryptocurrency indices are currently available for testing in New Release. Certification is not required.
