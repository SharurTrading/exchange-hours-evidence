## what_it_settles

It does NOT settle the ECBTC weekday window, and I will not claim it does. What it does settle, and what I think changes the shape of the question:

1. There IS a second CME document, published after 2026-05-29, that names ECBTC and states "Monday-Friday 4:00p.m. to 4:02 p.m. CT": CME rule filing 26-266, filed 06/04/2026 and signed by CME's Chief Regulatory Counsel. But it is a verbatim reproduction of SER-9740R as Exhibit A, not an independent restatement, so it cannot adjudicate a conflict with the wiki. Counting it as a "second statement" would be double-counting one document.

2. SER-9740R's hours table is demonstrably not tailored to ECBTC, and both halves of that proof are verbatim and public. SER-9740R (and 26-266) give ECBTC's "Current CME Globex Hours" as "Sunday 5:00 p.m. -Friday - 4:00 p.m. CT with a daily maintenance period from 4:00 p.m. - 5:00 p.m. CT". ECBTC's actual pre-cutover cell, per its own listing SER-9092, was "Sunday 5:00 p.m.- Friday 3:00 p.m. - Next day's Event Contract will list at 5:00 p.m." — a 3:00 p.m. end and a 5:00 p.m. relist, not a 4:00–5:00 p.m. maintenance period. The SER itself explains why: ECBTC was inserted into Table 1 in blackline and "No other changes have been made to the original SER", i.e. Table 2 remained the cryptocurrency-complex cell. So SER-9740R's 16:00 is the crypto-complex figure carried over, and it misdescribes ECBTC's own prior schedule. That materially weakens SER-9740R as the ECBTC-specific authority and is consistent with the wiki's 15:00 being deliberate (15:00 CT is ECBTC's inherited termination instant under SER-9092).

3. The Saturday side and the reopen side are independently confirmed post-cutover; only the close start is unresolved. Globex Notices of 2026-08-24 and 2026-08-31 place "Event-Based Contracts" channel 329 (ECBTC's channel and market segment 74 per CME's own client impact assessment) inside the "24/7 markets" list whose "standard schedule" Saturday window is 2:00–4:00 a.m. CT. SER-9740R/26-266 and the wiki already agree on the 16:01→16:02 queue and the 16:02 open. Nothing published after 2026-05-29 through any CME channel restates ECBTC's weekday close instant.

4. Strong same-channel circumstantial evidence that the wiki's "3:00:00 p.m." may be a template error — but it is evidence about other products, not a statement about ECBTC, so it must not be written up as one. CME's public "24/7 Trading for 1-Ounce Gold and 10-Barrel Crude" deck (July 8, 2026) migrates 1-Ounce Gold onto "329 (shared with events contracts)" and market segment 74 with "No impact to event contracts", and prints the identical row template as the ECBTC wiki — same Saturday row (2:00 a.m. to 3:45 a.m.), same pre-open (4:01:00–4:01:30), same no-cancel (4:01:30–4:02:00), same open (4:02:00) — differing only in "Close: 4:00:00 p.m. to 4:01:00 p.m. CT" where the ECBTC row reads "3:00:00 p.m.". Two products cannot have different daily maintenance windows on one Globex channel, which is the practical argument for 16:00; but I retrieved no CME sentence saying that about ECBTC, so under the governing law this stays an argument, not a source.

Bottom line for the crate: the 15:00-vs-16:00 conflict is unresolved by CME's own channels. If a value must ship, SER-9740R/26-266's 16:00 is the one carried in a rulebook-level, CFTC-certified document, but it must ship with the note that the same table misstates ECBTC's pre-cutover hours; and the 15:00 reading must be recorded as the ECBTC-specific client impact assessment's figure, not discarded.
