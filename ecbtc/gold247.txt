## Good Till (GT) Order Support 

Globex will support Good Till (GT) orders during weekend trading ● Key question: How can 5-day and 7-day clients manage resting GT orders? 

Solution 

● CME Globex Credit Controls (GC2) supports automatic GT elimination functionality via a distinct weekend GC2 limit at the execution firm level. ● Accounts default to a 5-day support schedule (zero weekend limit). ● The GFID weekend credit setting determines GT order eliminations, not the type of session 

Weekend GFIDs NOT Enabled Weekend GFIDs Enabled 

Cancellation Time: GT orders for these products will be canceled every Friday at 4:00 p.m. CT .

Scope: This cancellation applies only to products designated as "7-Day Products" and is enforced at the GFID level. 

iLink 7-day session: GT Orders submitted through 7-day GFIDs will remain active and manageable in the market. 

iLink 5-day CGW Session: GT Orders submitted through a 5-day session will remain working, although not accessible via an iLink session. © 2026 CME Group. All rights reserved. CME GROUP PUBLIC
