UpdateChanges to iLink On-Demand Party Details for Order Mass Cancel - October 4

† Denotes update to the article

Starting †Sunday, October 4 (trade date Monday, October 5), CME Group will implement iLink messaging changes to on-demand administrative information for the iLink Order Mass Action Report (tag 35-MsgType=BZ) to consistently return customer account information.

On-Demand Party Detail Definition Request Acknowledgment

Population of tag 1691-PartyDetailID for the Customer Account Party Detail Role (tag 1693-PartyDetailRole=24) on the Party Details Definition Request Acknowledgment (PDDRA, tag 35-MsgType=CY) will always reflect the value from the Party Details Definition Request (PDDR, tag 35-MsgType=CX).

Currently, the tag 1691-PartyDetailID on the PDDRA is populated only when tag 6115-MassCancelRequestType=101 (Account) is sent in the Order Mass Action Request (tag 35-MsgType=CA).

ABCDE = 101 ABCDE ABCDE

This change is only applicable to on-demand administrative information. Pre-Registered Party Detail Definition Request Acknowledgment messages sent from the Order Entry Service Gateway remain unchanged.

TransactTime Precision Update

With this change, tag 60-TransactTime on the iLink Order Mass Action Report (tag 35-MsgType=BZ) will be sent with nanosecond precision. This timestamp is currently sent in milliseconds.

UpdateTreasury Link Spreads on CME Globex - Q4 2026

† Denotes update to the article

In Q4 2026, pending regulatory review, CME Group will introduce Treasury Link (cash versus futures) spreads for trading on CME Globex. 

This offering represents a price differential between BrokerTec U.S. Treasury Actives offered on BrokerTec Chicago and CBOT U.S. Treasury futures, facilitating the simultaneous and atomic execution of both legs within a single transaction, eliminating legging risk. 

Treasury Link spreads will be listed with 1 to 1 Ratio and DV01 Weighted (X:Y) Ratio. For more information, view the Treasury Link Spreads - Product Specifications. 

For technical specifications, launch timelines, connectivity instructions, and revision history of changes since initial publication, review the †client impact assessment.

Use your CME Group Login to view market data multicast IP and port details on the Connectivity and Configuration Details page.

These products will be available for customer testing in New Release on Monday, October 5.

CME Globex Migration to Google Cloud - Q4 2026

Listed Derivative markets on the CME Globex platform are migrating to Google Cloud’s Ultra Low Latency (ULL) Solution to deliver a more flexible, scalable, and resilient trading ecosystem for clients. By combining Google Cloud’s ULL infrastructure with industry-leading Globex execution capabilities, this transition will unlock expanded co-location options, real-time data analytics, and enhanced operational efficiency to better support your needs. Maintaining market integrity and operational continuity remains our highest priority; we are dedicated to supporting you throughout every phase of this migration with robust testing sandboxes, transparent roadmaps, and dedicated support to deliver a reliable transition.

In response to client interest, CME Group is extending the Sandbox-Dallas Pilot Program for clients who achieve specific technical milestones in the Sandbox-Dallas environment. The first 50 participants to complete each obligation by Wednesday, September 30, and submit proof will earn a stipend. Details on this program are available in the CME Globex Sandbox-Dallas Pilot Program Agreement.

To provide clients with a real-world production environment on Google Cloud prior to the Chicago region launch, Livestock futures and options will migrate to a new Production-Dallas environment in the private zones of the Dallas region in Q4 2026. In the second half of 2028, Livestock futures and options will migrate to the Production-Chicago environment, in the private Google Cloud Chicago region.

To support market access and the Dual Zones Solution, CME Group offers new discovery services via REST API for easing client network connectivity management. CME Group now offers the CME Globex Discovery Service - Configuration API. Clients can use this REST API endpoint to programmatically retrieve IP address configurations for MDP channels, MSGW, and Drop Copy gateways in the Sandbox environment. The CME Globex Discovery Service - Zone Schedule API is also available, to determine which zone is currently Active and when the next zone swap is scheduled to occur.

Information on compute pricing for U4C Bare Metal and U4S Virtual Machine instances is available now from Google Cloud’s Account Teams. To support the migration and the testing of U4 machines specifically, Google Cloud customers may opt in to a Google Cloud incentive program that provides up to $30,000 in credits for U4 compute usage. More information on this program is available from your Account Team or contact exchanges@google.com.

Position Reporting Under CFTC Part 17

On April 30, 2024, the Commodity Futures Trading Commission ("CFTC") approved final rules to amend its Large Trading Reporting Regulations for Futures and Options. The amendments were made to modernize CFTC's Large Trader Reporting process and align it with other reporting structures outlined in CFTC regulations.

Since October 2024, CME Group has offered methods to retrieve its instrument listing containing the Unique Instrument Code (UIC) and other instrument data elements in compliance with the amended rule.

Starting March 10, 2025, CME Group began accepting test files in the new Position Message FIXML format via SFTP. For details regarding submitting the new FIXML Position Message, file management, field validations, and example scenarios, please refer to the information in the CME Group Client Systems Wiki.

CFTC's final rules became effective upon publication in the Federal Register on June 3, 2024. Subject to the conditions of the January 27, 2026, no-action letter, reporting firms must comply with these CFTC regulations by July 26, 2027.

A test environment for Large Trader Reporting is now available within the Firm Regulatory Portal (FRP UT). Users can access the testing portal at: https://frp.uat.cmegroup.com.

A brief video demonstration is available to highlight the new look and functionality of the FRP UT.

The Market Regulation Department will be publishing further guidance regarding Large Trader Reporting in subsequent Special Executive Reports as solutions become available.

Support Contacts

  • To request access to FRP UT, please contact the Enterprise Application & System Entitlements (EASE) team.
  • For questions or comments regarding FRP UT, please contact LTR_Part17_Alerts@cmegroup.com.
  • For questions about trade practice rules, please contact MarketReg.Outreach@cmegroup.com.

Listing of Five CME and One CBOT E-mini Factor Futures Contracts - This Week

Effective this Sunday, September 20 (trade date Monday, September 21), pending completion of all regulatory review periods, five CME and one CBOT E-mini Factor futures contracts will be listed for trading on CME Globex and for submission for clearing via CME ClearPort.

The corresponding fixing prices are sent on CME Data Insights - Settlements & Valuations channels via Market Data Platform (MDP) 3.0 Market Data Incremental Refresh (tag 35-MsgType=X) message types with the following attributes:

  • Tag 279-MDUpdateAction will be set to 0 = New
  • Tag 269-MDEntryType will be set to 6 = Settlement Price OR 269-MDEntryType will be set to W = Fixing Price
  • Tag 5796-Trading Reference will display the date for which the marker price was calculated
