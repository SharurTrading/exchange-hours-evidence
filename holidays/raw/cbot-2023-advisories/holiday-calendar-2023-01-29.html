CME Group Holiday Calendar

CME Group observes U.S.-recognized holidays. On these holidays, trading hours may vary depending on markets traded. Opening and closing times may be affected on the holiday, as well as the business days prior to or after a holiday.

    2023 CME Globex Trading Schedule

    Please note that with the 2023 calendar year the CME Group will be transitioning from downloadable holiday calendars for Globex and incorporating the holiday hours into our trading hours page. To view the trading hours for a specific holiday please select the appropriate date in the “DATE” field and the data will automatically update in the table below the date field. You can then filter for the various products that you need to view. To view the trading hours for the next upcoming holiday you can click on the “View trading hours for (holiday name)” link that appears to the right of the date field. Please note that near the top of the page there is a “DOWNLOAD” button that will allow you download the 3-day calendar view as displayed on the page. This will download all filtered products. The link can be found highlighted below and also by clicking hyperlink on each holiday..
