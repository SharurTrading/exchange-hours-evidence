Futures Europe or the stock exchange on which the underlying security is listed (“the Relevant Stock
Exchange”) is closed because of a public holiday.

Please note that on days in which the Relevant Stock Exchange observes a half day, contracts will
close for trading at 14:00 LLT (London Local Time) on ICE Futures Europe.
