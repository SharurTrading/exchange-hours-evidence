TRADING DAYS IN 2010
Executive Summary
This Notice announces the trading days for the London market in 2010.
1.

The London market will be open on Monday to Friday throughout 2010 except on the following
days:
Friday 1 January 2010 (New Year’s Day)
Friday 2 April 2010 (Good Friday)
Monday 5 April 2010 (Easter Monday)

2.

On the following UK Bank Holidays, NYSE Liffe’s sterling based products and UK-based
commodity products will not be made available, but other products will be available:
Monday 3 May 2010
Monday 31 May 2010
Monday 30 August 2010
Monday 27 December 2010
Tuesday 28 December 2010

3.

On Friday 24 December 2010 and Friday 31 December 2010, trading hours will be aligned with
those of underlying and, where appropriate, related markets. On these days the Exchange will
commence the closing of its derivatives contracts from midday GMT (1.00 pm CET).

4.

Options shall not be capable of being exercised on days on which such options are not
available for trading/reporting. Any Exercise Notice given on such days in respect of such
options shall be invalid.

5.

Precise details of the trading hours and associated information in relation to all London
derivative contracts will be published in due course on the NYSE Liffe website
(www.nyx.com/liffeholidays). Moreover, that web page will be updated as further information
becomes available.

For further information in relation to this Notice, Members should contact their Account Manager.
