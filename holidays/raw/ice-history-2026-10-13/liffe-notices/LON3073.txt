TRADING DAYS IN 2009

Executive Summary
This Notice announces the trading days for the London market in 2009.

1.

The London market will be open on Monday to Friday throughout 2009 except on the following
days:
Thursday 1 January 2009 (New Year’s Day)
Friday 10 April 2009 (Good Friday)
Monday 13 April 2009 (Easter Monday)
Friday 1 May 20091
Friday 25 December 2009 (Christmas Day)

2.

Certain contracts will be available for trading on the following UK public holidays in 2009:
Monday 4 May 2009
Monday 25 May 2009
Monday 31 August 2009
Monday 28 December 2009

3.

On Thursday 24 December 2009 and Thursday 31 December 2009, trading hours will be aligned
with those of underlying and, where appropriate, related markets. On these days the Exchange
will commence the closing of its derivatives contracts from midday GMT (1.00 pm CET).

4.

Options shall not be capable of being exercised on days on which such options are not
available for trading/reporting. Any Exercise Notice given on such days in respect of such
options shall be invalid.
