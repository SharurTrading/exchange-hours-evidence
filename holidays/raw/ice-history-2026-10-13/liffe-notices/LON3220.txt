Executive Summary
This Notice provides information regarding the trading arrangements for the period 24 December
2009 to 1 January 2010.

1.

Summary of Trading Hours

1.1

The trading hours of the London market during the period of 24 December 2009 to 1 January
2010 inclusive can be summarised as follows:
Thursday 24 December 2009

Half day trading, with Contracts closing by 13:00
hours (GMT) (14:00 hours CET) 1, 2.

Friday 25 December 2009

Closed.

Monday 28 December 2009

Sterling-based products and UK-based commodity
products will not be made available for trading on
this day. In addition, flexible Individual Equity
Options and Universal Stock Futures based on Irish
Stocks will not be available. All other Contracts will
be available for trading as usual (there will be no
evening trading for Euribor and Eurodollar
Contracts, both of which will close at 18:00 hours
GMT (19:00 CET)) 1.

Tuesday 29 December and
Wednesday 30 December 2009

1

Normal trading day for all Contracts.1

Thursday 31 December 2009

Half day trading, with Contracts closing by 13:00
hours (GMT) (14:00 hours CET) 1,2.

Friday 1 January 2010

Closed.

For the avoidance of doubt, Members should note that Euribor Futures will open at 01:00 hours (GMT) as usual.

2.

Japanese Bank Holidays

2.1

As the Tokyo Stock Exchange will be closed for trading on 23 December and 31 December
2009, JGB trades executed on the LIFFE market on the business day immediately preceding
each of these days, will be held by NYSE Liffe Clearing overnight and will be subject to
initial margin calculation and subsequent calls/payments under the NYSE Liffe Clearing
arrangements.

2.2

The Tokyo Financial Exchange (“TFX”) will also be closed for trading on 23 and 31
December 2009. Notwithstanding this, Euroyen (TIBOR) trades executed on the LIFFE
market on the business day immediately preceding each of these days will be transferred to
TFX as normal.

3.

Expiry, Option Exercise and Tender Deadlines

3.1

The December 2009 delivery month of the Long Gilt Contract shall cease trading at 11:00
hours GMT on 29 December 2009. Delivery Notices on 29 December 2009 must be
submitted by 11:00 hours GMT, with settlement taking place on 31 December 2009. Delivery
Notices on 30 December 2009 (Last Notice Day) must be submitted by 10:00 hours GMT,
with settlement also taking place on 31 December 2009.

3.2

Deadlines for the submission of Exercise Notices to NYSE Liffe Clearing on 24 December
and 31 December 2009 are shown in the Attachments to this Notice.

For further information in relation to this Notice, Members should contact their Account Manager.
