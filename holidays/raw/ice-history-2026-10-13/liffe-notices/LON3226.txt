TRADING DAYS IN 2010

Executive Summary
This Notice informs Members that the precise details of the trading hours, and associated information
in relation to Trading Days in 2010 for all London derivatives, have now been published on the NYSE
Euronext website.

1.

London Notice No. 3203, issued on 13 October 2009, announced the trading days for the
London market in 2010.

2.

This Notice confirms that the details of the trading hours, and associated information in
relation to all London derivative contracts, have now been published on the NYSE Euronext
website (www.nyx.com/liffeholidays).

3.

That document contains all available information as at 30 November 2009. When further
information is available, the document will be updated.

For further information in relation to this Notice, Members should contact their Account Manager.
