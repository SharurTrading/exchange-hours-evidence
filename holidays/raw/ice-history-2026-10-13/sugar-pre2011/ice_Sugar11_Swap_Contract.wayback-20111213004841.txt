Cleared-Only Sugar No.11 Swap Contract
This contract is not available for trading, it is a cleared-only product. Eligible
over-the-counter participants can submit bothsides of an over-the-counter
trade to the exchange to be substituted into a Sugar Swap Contract.
Trade Submission Hours
  Cleared Swaps Contracts must be submitted for clearing via the ICEBlock system, and can be submitted only




                                                                                                                     Cleared-Only Sugar No. 11 Swap Contract
  during trading hours for the related, traded futures contract.		


Symbol:
  SBC


Contract Size:
  $112,000 X Final Settlement Value


Price Quotation:
  Cents and hundredths of a cent per pound to two decimal places


Contract Listings:
  January, March, May, July and October; contracts are available up to3 years forward

Minimum Price Movement:
  1/100 of a cent per pound ($11.20/contract)




                                                                                                                     v 2.4 February 2009
Settlement:
  Cash settlement at expiry based upon the the daily settlement price of the corresponding Sugar 11 traded
  future contract on the Last Trading Day of the swap contract.


Daily Price Limit:
  None


Last Trading Day:
  The Last Trading Day of the regular option on the corresponding Sugar No. 11 traded future.
