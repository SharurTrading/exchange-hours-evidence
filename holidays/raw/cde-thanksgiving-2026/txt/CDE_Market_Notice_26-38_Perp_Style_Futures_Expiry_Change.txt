Subject:​           Coinbase Derivatives Equity Index Perp Style Futures Expiry Change
------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Coinbase Derivatives, LLC (CDE) is announcing a change to its Perpetual Style Futures
contracts in alignment with this CFTC Letter. Effective Monday, October 26, 2026 the contracts
listed below, currently structured as five-year long dated futures, will have their expiration dates
removed and will become true perpetual futures contracts with no expiration.

Although CDE is not changing contract size, minimum tick size or value, funding rate
methodology, daily settlement procedures, margining, or clearing, changes to contract terms can
affect prices and trading conditions where open positions exist. Holders of open positions could
experience gains or losses, and impacts on pricing, liquidity, basis, volatility, or hedging
performance may be difficult to predict in advance. Market participants, including customers
trading through intermediaries, should evaluate this change based on their own trading
objectives, risk limits, hedging needs, and operational setup, including any systems or
processes that reference a contract expiration date.

    1.​ US500 Index Perp Style Futures
    2.​ Tech100 Index Perp Style Futures
    3.​ Defense10 Index Perp Style Futures
    4.​ AI10 Index Perp Style Futures
    5.​ China10 Index Perp Style Futures
    6.​ Coin50 Perp Style Futures

CDE will continue to publish a reference expiration date for these contracts to consume and
reference, in order to simplify the operational transition to perpetual futures. CDE will
communicate its longer-term approach to the system handling of expiration dates in a separate
market notice.
