On Sunday, December 13, 2026 at 18:50 CT, Coinbase Derivatives, LLC (CDE) will launch fifty
seven (57) Single Stock Perpetual Futures (listed below) for Trade Date December 14, 20261.
All products are perpetual futures on individual equities listed in the United States, settled in
cash and denominated in USD, eligible for trading from Sunday 19:00 CT to Friday 16:00 CT.

Single Stock Perpetual Futures will be available for testing in CDE's integration environment
ahead of launch, and AAPL and QQQ are available now. CDE will add all remaining products on
November 23rd, 2026.

For access to the Integration environment, please contact derivatives@coinbase.com.

Product highlights

      1.​ Trading hours. Single Stock Perpetual Futures trade continuously from Sunday 19:00
          CT through Friday 16:00 CT each week.
      2.​ No expiration. Each contract is assigned a reference expiration set far in the future,
          which exists for systems that require a date. No final settlement is scheduled against it,
          and the reference date will be adjusted as required.
      3.​ Hourly funding. A funding rate is calculated each hour, smoothed against the prior
          hour's rate and capped at plus or minus 10 basis points per hour. Accrued funding is
          applied through a clearing cash adjustment at the exchange's regular margin runs, which
          occur midday and at the end of the day. No funding rate is published for any hour the
          market is closed, paused, or halted.
      4.​ Corporate actions. Depending on the event, CDE will either adjust the affected position
          and reference price, or let the market price in the economic impact before the event
          takes effect. CDE may halt trading in an affected contract to complete the required
          adjustment, and certain events may result in early cash settlement followed by a new
          listing. CDE will publish a market notice ahead of each event.

Corporate action handling

CDE will either adjust the affected position and reference price, or allow the market to price in
the economic impact before the event takes effect. CDE may halt trading in an affected contract
to complete the required adjustment. Treatment by event type is below.

 Stock split (forward, reverse, or fractional)                The Clearing House adjusts the number of
                                                              contracts held and reference price by the split
                                                              ratio. If, after the adjustment, the number of
                                                              contracts is not a whole number, it is rounded
                                                              down to the nearest whole number. Such
                                                              adjustments are intended solely to preserve,
                                                              as nearly as practicable, the holder's existing
                                                              economic exposure and constitute a
                                                              continuation of the holder's existing position
                                                              rather than the establishment of a new
                                                              position, liquidation of an existing position, or
                                                              replacement of a contract. Same treatment
                                                              for forward, reverse, and fractional splits, only
                                                              the ratio differs.

 Stock dividend                                               Handled like a stock split, using the stock
                                                              dividend ratio. The adjustment is intended
                                                              solely to preserve the holder’s pre-event
                                                              economic exposure.

 Cash dividend (ordinary)                                     No adjustment to positions or prices.

 Special dividend                                             Positions are adjusted by the special dividend
                                                              amount such that their price is the pre-special
                                                              dividend price ex-special dividend.

 Spin off                                                     For a spin-off, positions are early-settled at
