## Interpretive steps (recorded so a reader can re-verify)

1. **CFE timezone.** The CFE holiday table sits inside the page's CFE section, whose header reads
   "(all times are Central Time)". The table's own cells carry no zone. The values corroborate CT:
   `08:30:00 - 12:15:00` matches the CFE 8:30 a.m. CT regular open, and `5:00 PM` matches the CFE
   5:00 p.m. CT extended open. Treated as Central Time.
2. **CFE "Regular Trading Hours: None" + non-empty "Extended Trading Hours".** On these dates CFE prints
   no regular (day) session but an extended (overnight) session that ends at a stated instant on the
   holiday date — e.g. MLK: "5:00 PM (Sun) to 10:30 AM (Mon) and 5:00 PM (Mon) to 8:30 AM (Tue)".
   Recorded as an **early close at the stated instant on the holiday date**, with the verbatim row kept so
   the reader can re-derive it. Only 2026-12-25 has "None / None" and is a full closure.
3. **ICE "open¹".** The 2026 and 2027 IFUS calendars mark many holidays `open1`, footnoted:
   "Trading Hours for these contracts will be announced in advance of the respective holiday via Exchange
   Notices". Recorded as **modified** (the day trades, but not on the regular grid), and resolved to an
   instant only where the per-holiday Exchange Notice was retrieved.
4. **Eurex 2027.** Only indicative. Not admissible as an unconditional dated future under
   LAW-NO-FABRICATED-DATES; carried here with the caveat in the verbatim and listed under `missing`.
