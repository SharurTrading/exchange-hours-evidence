Friday, November 11, 2011

Equity Trader Alert #2011 - 54
NASDAQ OMX U.S. Markets will be Closed on Thursday, November 24, 2011, and will Close Early on Friday, November 25, 2011

Markets Impacted:

  • The NASDAQ Stock Market
  • NASDAQ OMX PSX
  • NASDAQ OMX BX
  • The NASDAQ Options Market
  • NASDAQ OMX PHLX
  • NASDAQ OMX Futures Exchange

Contact Information:

  • Transaction Services U.S. Market Sales at +1 800 846 0477

What you need to know:

  • The NASDAQ Stock Market® (NASDAQ®), NASDAQ OMX BXSM (BXSM), NASDAQ OMX PSXSM (PSXSM), The NASDAQ Options MarketSM (NOM), NASDAQ OMX PHLXSM (PHLX®) and The NASDAQ OMX Futures ExchangeSM (NFXSM) will be closed on Thursday, November 24, 2011, and will close early on Friday, November 25, 2011, in observation of the Thanksgiving holiday.
  • NASDAQ OMX® will operate on normal operating schedule for its European markets on November 24th and November 25th.

What will be closing?

NASDAQ, BX, PSX, NOM, PHLX and NFX will be closed Thursday, November 24, 2011, and will close early on Friday, November 25, 2011, in observation of the Thanksgiving holiday.

NASDAQ OMX will operate on normal operating schedule for its European markets on November 24th and 25th.
