Thursday, January 2, 2025

Equity Trader Alert #2025 - 1
Trade Settlement and EX dates for Thursday, January 9, 2025

Category:

  • Industry Announcement

Markets Impacted:

  • The Nasdaq Stock Market
  • Nasdaq BX
  • Nasdaq PSX
  • FINRA/Nasdaq TRF

Contact Information:

  • Nasdaq Corporate Data Operations at +1 877 308 0523
  • Nasdaq Trading Services at +1 212 231 5100

Resources

  • ETA #2024-86
  • ECA #2024-632

As announced in Equity Trader Alert #2024 -86, Nasdaq U.S. equities markets, options markets, and the FINRA/Nasdaq TRFs™. will be closed on Thursday, January 9, 2025, in observance of the passing of President Jimmy Carter.

As oulined in Equity Corporate Actions Alert #2024 - 632, Thursday, January 9, 2025 is not a valid trade date. It will be considered a valid settlement date and all trades executed on Wednesday, January 8, 2025 will settle in the usual manner on Thursday, January 9, 2025.

All trades on Friday, January 10, 2025 will settle on the normal settlement date of Monday, January 13, 2025.

As a result of the market closure on Thursday, January 9, 2025 all stocks which would normally have been ex-dividend on Thursday, January 9, 2025 (with a record date of Thursday, January 9, 2025), shall be ex-dividend on Friday, January 10, 2025. All stocks with a record date of Friday, January 10, 2025, will be ex-dividend on January 10, 2025.

Settlement questions can be directed to Nasdaq Market Operations at +1.212.231.5180, Option 5 and Corporate Action questions to Corporate Data Operations at +1.877.308.0523.
