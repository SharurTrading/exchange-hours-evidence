Summary
Coinbase Derivatives, LLC ("CDE" or "Exchange") is completing its move to continuous (24x7)
operation, after which the weekly 1-hour Friday maintenance window will be retired and rolling
gateway restarts will occur during market hours.

The revised weekly Friday maintenance windows (16:00–16:50 CT, removal effective 17:00 CT)
are:
    ●​ Integration (UAT): Friday, July 24, 2026
    ●​ Production: Friday, September 11, 2026

After each environment's final Friday window, TRUE_24X7 products will remain OPEN and
trade continuously, subject only to the scheduled weekend maintenance windows.

At the final Friday window in each environment, all products that currently support weekend
trading (the weekend trading set, comprising all crypto futures plus Gold and Silver futures)
transition to a TRUE_24X7 trading schedule and trade continuously thereafter, subject only to
scheduled weekend maintenance windows (see market notices). Products that do not trade on
weekends currently are unaffected. Transitioning products are listed at the end of this Market
Notice.

This Market Notice supersedes 26-13 and revises the Production date in 26-25. Sequence
Reset on Logon (141=Y) is recommended but not required (see Connectivity
Recommendations).

Client Runbook & Impacted Services
Unless noted, guidance applies to both Internet and Cross Connect (data center) sessions, for
whichever gateway side (A or B) a session is subscribed to. These recommendations apply to
the rolling deployments above and to any unplanned service event on an A or B gateway.

    ●​ Sequence Reset on Logon: If your FIX engine reconnects to the opposite side during a
       restart window, the session is established as a new sequence-number epoch. We
       recommend sending ResetSeqNumFlag=Y (Tag 141) on all logons to handle this cleanly.
       Applies to FIX/SBE Order Entry and FIX Market Data. Recommended, not required.
    ●​ Order Entry (FIX and SBE): Clients on a single gateway side experience a momentary
       disconnect then automatic reconnect. Your engine should handle this via standard
       session logon recovery.
    ●​ Market Data – FIX: Feeds may briefly interrupt during the restart of upstream sequencer
       and replication services. Reconnect and re-send a MarketDataRequest to receive the full
       snapshot. No data from the gap window is retransmitted automatically.
    ●​ Market Data – UDP: Expect a brief delay in sequence numbers as gateways restart.
       UDP sequence-number gapping is not expected.
    ●​ Drop Copy: Beginning with Event 3.0 (Aug 7, 2026), Drop Copy sessions are included
       in the rolling restart. On reconnect, use EventResendRequest to recover missed
       ExecutionReports and dedupe via ExecID and PossResend. Before Event 3.0, Drop
       Copy is not impacted.
    ●​ REST API: Momentary outages are possible. If a request times out, resend it.
    ●​ No client-facing impact: Post-Trade and Reporting pipelines resume automatically
       from their last checkpoint with no data loss. The CDE Portal is unaffected.


Connectivity Recommendations
    ●​ Sequence Reset on Logon is optional but strongly recommended, particularly for
       clients on Internet gateways that rely on aggregate-port failover. Sending 141=Y on
       every logon ensures a clean epoch when your session lands on the opposite side after a
       restart.
    ●​ Monotonic sequence numbers and long-lived sessions: Under continuous operation,
       sessions are expected to live indefinitely. A session whose outbound sequence number
       approaches the FIX protocol maximum (the signed 32-bit limit, roughly 2.1 billion) will be
       disconnected by the exchange to force a reset. Clients who never reset (141=N) should
       perform a periodic sequence reset to avoid an unexpected disconnect. Clients sending
       141=Y on logon are not affected.
    ●​ Internet connectivity: If you connect to an A-side or B-side feed-specific port, switch to
       the aggregate ports for automatic load-balanced failover.
    ●​ Failover: A session that does not fail over to the opposite side remains disconnected
       until the impacted gateway completes its restart, then reconnects automatically.
       Configure your engine to initiate logon immediately on TCP reconnect.

    ●​ Order Entry recovery (FIX): Persist the last processed ExecID, and after reconnect use
       DCM's replay flow: LastExecIdRequest then EventResendRequest. Client processing
       should be idempotent and dedupe via ExecID and PossResend.
    ●​ Order Entry recovery (SBE): Same as FIX, except treat all events within the replay
       window as possibly duplicated and dedupe via the unique ExecID.


Seamless Deploys (Upcoming Enhancement)
CDE is rolling out seamless gateway deployments. During a seamless takeover, the incoming
gateway instance assumes ownership of the outgoing instance's active orders, so the takeover
does not trigger cancel-on-disconnect for those orders. This is expected to reduce per-gateway
client-visible downtime from approximately 60 seconds today to approximately 3 seconds.

Seamless deployments do not change the Connectivity Recommendations above. Clients
should still reconnect and, where applicable, reset sequence numbers on logon. Availability will
be announced by Market Notice per environment.


Open Orders and Positions
    ●​ If CancelOnDisconnect is enabled (session or user level), resting orders are canceled on
       disconnect. If not enabled, they remain on the book during and after the rolling deploy.
       With seamless deploys, resting orders are preserved across the takeover regardless.
    ●​ Positions and balances are fully consistent before and after the restart. No reconciliation
       action is required.
