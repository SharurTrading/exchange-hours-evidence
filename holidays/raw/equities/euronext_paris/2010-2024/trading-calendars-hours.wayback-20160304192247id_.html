Calendar of business days 2016

Euronext's cash and derivatives markets will be open Monday to Friday throughout 2016 except on the following days:

2016
Friday 1 January 2016 New Year's Day
Friday 25 March 2016 Good Friday
Monday 28 March 2016 Easter Monday
Monday 26 December 2016 Boxing Day

 

On Friday 23 December 2016 and on Friday 30 December 2016, trading on the Cash and Derivatives markets will close at the usual times.
