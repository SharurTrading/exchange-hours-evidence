TMX Group Holiday Operating Schedule

Date 30/11/2016

Toronto Stock Exchange, TSX Venture Exchange, TSX Alpha Exchange and Montréal Exchange will be closed on Monday, December 26, 2016 in lieu of Christmas Day on Sunday, December 25, 2016, Tuesday, December 27, 2016 in lieu of Boxing Day on Monday, December 26, 2016 and Monday, January 2, 2017 in lieu of New Year's Day on Sunday, January 1, 2017.

NGX will remain open on those dates but will operate on a reduced trading schedule.
