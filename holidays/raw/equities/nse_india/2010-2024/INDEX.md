# NSE India raw artifacts, 2010-2024

Retrieved 2026-09-29 UTC. Every artifact is a Wayback `id_` replay of the
operator's own published holiday material: the annual trading-holiday pages on
`nseindia.com` (2011, 2014-2022; T1), the operator's annual circulars
(`HOLIDAYS.zip` inner `eq_holidays.pdf` for 2010 and the 2011 circular PDF for
corroboration, the 2013 circular PDF; T1), and the operator's own
`holiday-master?type=trading` machine channel (2022 corroboration, 2023 and
2024; T2). The 2010 `HOLIDAYS.zip` holds one PDF per segment; the capital-market
`eq_holidays.pdf` inside it is circular 061, "Trading holidays for the calendar
year 2010".

No 2012 artifact and no 2018 artifact exists in the archive: the CM
trading-holiday page has no capture in either calendar year (the 2012-era
`marketinfo/holiday_master` pages are a query form, not a list; the old page
302-redirects from 2017-07 and the new pages begin 2020-01; the `holiday-master`
API begins 2021-03), and the annual circular PDFs are not captured for those
years. 2012 and 2018 stay outside every audited window; closing conditions are
in the evidence file.
