Trading Calendar 2013
At FWB® Frankfurter Wertpapierbörse (Frankfurt Stock Exchange) there will be trading Mondays to Fridays in 2013, with the exception of 1 January, 29 March, 1 April, 1 May, 24 December, 25 December, 26 December and 31 December. 24 and 31 December are settlement days.

Trading calendar for Xetra International Market
The trading calendar for Xetra International Market is based on the trading calendar of the Frankfurt Stock Exchange and also takes into account the bank holidays of the respective country.
