## Reading notes

- Each year's rows key on the per-year calendar's closure sentence
  ("there will be trading Mondays to Fridays in <year>, with the exception
  of ..."), quoted in full per year section in `docs/evidence/xetra.md`.
  The PDFs print the sentence under the month grids; the 2010-2014 grid
  editions are the `DB_HK_<year>` documents, the 2015-2024 editions the
  `xetra-trading-calendar-<year>` PDFs.
- "Settlement day" rows (24/31 December, Whit Monday 2015-2021, 3 October
  2014/2016-2019, 2017-10-31) are in the sentence's exception list — no
  trading — and ship `Closed`.
- The sentences name no 3 October in 2011 (Monday) and 2013 (Thursday), and
  name no Whit Monday or 3 October from 2022 on: those days traded.
