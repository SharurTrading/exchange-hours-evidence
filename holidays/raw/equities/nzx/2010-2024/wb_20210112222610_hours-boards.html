NZX Dairy Derivatives Trading Hours

NZX Dairy Derivatives provide global and local dairy processors, manufacturers, firms and farmers, with tools to manage their price risk from growing volatility in the sector. NZX Dairy Derivatives are the only dairy derivatives based on globally traded prices.

You can learn more about NZX Dairy Derivatives here.
