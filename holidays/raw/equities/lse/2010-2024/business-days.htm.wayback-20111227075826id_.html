Business days


The Exchange operates its Trading Services each weekday excluding Public and Bank Holidays in England & Wales.  The Public and Bank Holidays recognised by the Exchange are set out below.
 
The days indicated below will also be non-settlement days for Exchange executions settling in Euroclear UK & Ireland.  Please note this includes all SETS executions.  Whilst the International Order Book observes the same trading calendar as the rest of our Trading Services it operates on it's own Settlement Calendar.  Full details can be found as a download here. 

Please note that on days indicated by * the Exchange will still provide the following:
  • OTC (off-Exchange) trade reporting for European Quoting Service (trading segment EQS) and European Trade Reporting (trading segment ITR) securities; and
  • Dissemination of the FTSE indices supported by the Millennium Exchange platform
     
The Exchange will keep its days of operation under review
