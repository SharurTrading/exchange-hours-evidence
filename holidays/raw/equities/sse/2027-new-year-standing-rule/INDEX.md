# Shanghai Stock Exchange 2027 New Year's Day

Trading Rules 2.4.1 establishes Monday through Friday trading and closure
on statutory holidays and exchange-announced closure dates. State Council
Order 795 establishes January 1 as the statutory New Year's Day. The
exchange's closure index records the state of the annual publication check.
