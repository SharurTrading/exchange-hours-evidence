- The Chinese notice states the seven statutory windows: 元旦 1月1日（周五）至3日（周日）; 春节 2月5日（除夕、周五）至13日（周六），1月31日（周日）、2月14日（周日）上班; 清明节 4月3日（周六）至5日（周一）; 劳动节 5月1日（周六）至5日（周三），5月8日（周六）上班; 端午节 6月9日（周三）; 中秋节 9月15日（周三）; 国庆节 10月1日（周五）至7日（周四），9月26日（周日）、10月9日（周六）上班.
- The English page's table corroborates the same seven windows (New Year's Day three days; Spring Festival Feb 5 — Feb 13, nine days; Qingming April 3 — April 5; International Workers' Day May 1 — May 5; Dragon Boat Festival June 9; Mid-Autumn Festival Sept 15; National Day Oct 1 — Oct 7).
- The `上班` clauses are the national working-weekend swaps (调休): for the stock market those Saturdays and Sundays stay ordinary weekend closures, exactly as the SSE notices' `另外，X为周末休市` clauses have been treated in every keyed year, so no session exists to encode for them.
- The notice states holiday arrangements in national-civil language (放假/放假调休); it does not state exchange closures in session language. The SSE rows that will implement these windows ship only from the operator's own notice, per the annual pattern (the 2025 notice 上证公告〔2024〕38号 followed the 2024 State Council arrangement; the 2026 notice 上证公告〔2025〕45号 followed the 2025 one).
- Closing condition unchanged: publication of 上交所's own 关于2027年部分节假日休市安排的通知, which extends the audited window to 2027-12-31 (re-checked monthly per LAW-WATCH; see `../forward-2027/SEARCH-recheck-2026-11-18.md`).
