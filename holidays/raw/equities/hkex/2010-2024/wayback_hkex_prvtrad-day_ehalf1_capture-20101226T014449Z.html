Trading News

Updated: 24/12/2010 
Half Trading Day Today


Please be reminded that today, 24/12/2010 being eve of the coming
Christmas is a half trading day from 9:30am to 12:30pm where there will be
no afternoon trading session.

In addition, download of closing prices and trade details via File
Transfer System (FTS) will be available from 1:00pm to 3:00pm and Central
Trade Feed service (CTF) from 12:30pm to 2:00pm (instead of the normal
service time).

For details, please refer to Circulars CMD/TMO/057/10 of 1 December 2010
and CMD/TMO/059/10 of 15 December 2010.
