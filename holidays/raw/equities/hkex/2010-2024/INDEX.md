## Reading notes

- 2010-2017 rows key on the per-year `Trading Calendar` PDFs (the
  `eng/market/sec_tradinfo/tradcal/` documents); 2018-2024 rows key on the
  `Trading Calendar and Holiday Schedule` page edition that prints each year.
- The 2010-2011 eves close at the era's printed half-day edge 12:30
  (`HKEX-TN-2010`); the CAS-era eves close at 12:10 (`HKEX-HOURS-SEC`); the ten
  2012-2015 eves ship `Unsourced` (issue #208) because no era artifact states
  that half-day shape.
- The two captures marked "retrieval extra, not keyed" are alternates pulled
  during CDX retrieval and kept for provenance; no row keys on them.
- The per-year text extractions `hkex-tradcal-2010.txt` .. `hkex-tradcal-2017.txt`
  are working extractions of the PDFs above, not independent evidence.
