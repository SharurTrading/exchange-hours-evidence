Settlement and Trading Calendar

20102009

Always up to date with the settlement calendar

The SIX Swiss Exchange settlement calendar shows the days on which national banks are closed for a public holiday. The settlement calendar provides this information for all currencies in which products are traded on SIX Swiss Exchange. There is no settlement on these days.

The trading calendar[pdf] shows on which days there is no trading on SIX Swiss Exchange.

Any dates not included in the settlement calendar are considered normal trading days with settlement in the relevant currency.
