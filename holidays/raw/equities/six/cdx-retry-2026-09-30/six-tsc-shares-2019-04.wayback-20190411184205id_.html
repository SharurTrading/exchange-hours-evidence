Trading and Currency Holiday Calendar

 

The trading calendar 2019[pdf] shows on which days there is no trading on SIX Swiss Exchange.

The SIX Swiss Exchange Currency Holiday Calendar 2019[pdf] shows the days on which national banks are closed for a public holiday. The Currency Holiday Calendar provides this information for all currencies in which products are traded on SIX Swiss Exchange. There is no settlement on these days.
Any weekdays not included in the Currency Holiday Calendar are considered normal trading days with settlement in the relevant currency.

For the settlement holiday calendar (showing the settlement holidays at each CSD) please refer to the website of SIX Securities Services
