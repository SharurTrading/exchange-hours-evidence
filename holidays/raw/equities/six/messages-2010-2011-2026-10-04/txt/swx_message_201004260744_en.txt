The purpose of this message is to inform you about changes concerning the SIX Swiss Exchange
trading segments Exchange Traded Funds (ETF) and Exchange Traded Structured Funds (ETSF).

Multi-currency trading
Starting on 1 June 2010, providers of ETFs and ETSFs will have the possibility to list their products
in multiple trading currencies on SIX Swiss Exchange.
Interested providers can specify additional trading currencies in their listing application to SIX
Exchange Regulation for products to be newly listed.
A written request must be submitted to SIX Exchange Regulation for approval to trade products that
are already listed in their primary currency in additional currencies. The request must include
evidence that the relevant governing body has expressly agreed to the start of trading in an
additional currency. Furthermore, an official notice and an advertisement must be published, and
market making for the additional trading currency must be assured.
The usual listing deadlines apply to the listing of products in additional trading currencies.

During the morning «snapshot» on 1 June 2010, the Exchange will delete all good-till-date orders
that do not indicate a valid price step. It will be in the responsibility of the participant to re-enter the
corresponding orders prior to the start of trading on 1 June 2010.
Wherever necessary, the Exchange will adjust the reference prices to reflect the newly valid price
step. As a part of this, the reference prices will be rounded to the closest valid price step.
All information on the valid trading parameters can be found in Annex G to Directive 3: Trading and
in the Product Guide – Fund Market, which will be amended accordingly as of 1 June 2010.
