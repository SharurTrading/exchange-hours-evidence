SIX Swiss Exchange Message no. 45/2011

Subject         Change in trading segments for Schindler Holding AG
                and Weatherford International Ltd due to the ordinary index review
Category        Index Adjustments
Authorised by   Adrian Isler; Head Market Operations
                Björn Schaub; Head Exchange Operations
Pages           2
Date            04.08.2011                                                                    Information


SIX Swiss Exchange informed you in SIX Swiss Exchange Message No. 36/2011 on 8 July 2011 of the
index adjustments made on the occasion of the ordinary index review. This message contains key
information regarding the consequences of this ordinary index adjustment.

The inclusion of Schindler Holding AG in the SLI® and the resulting elimination of Weatherford
International Ltd from the SLI® mean that both stocks will be moved to different trading segments as of
19 September 2011.

Schindler Holding AG will be moved from the Mid-/Small-Cap segment (market code XSWX) to the Blue
Chip segment (market code XVTX), while Weatherford International Ltd will be moved from the Blue Chip
segment to the Mid-/Small-Cap segment. As a result of this adjustment, the market code for Schindler
Holding AG’s separate trading line will also change from XSWX to XVTX.
All good-till-date orders relating to these stocks will be deleted from the order books after the close of
trading at 5.30 p.m. CET on 16 September 2011 (the last trading day before the change in segments).
Members will receive confirmation that their orders have been deleted in the form of a Standard Trading
Interface (STI) Execution Report Message (OrdStatus “Canceled” and Text “0: delisted”). We will leave it
to the participants to enter these orders into the new order books by the start of trading on 19 September
2011.
