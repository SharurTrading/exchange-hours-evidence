Subject         Changes to Directive 7 – Access Fee
Category        Regulation

Pages           1
Date            06.07.2011                                                                Information


Dear Sir or Madam

We would like to inform you about forthcoming changes to Annex J of Directive 7 (Fees and Costs)
regarding access fees. The changes will take effect on 1 August 2011.
SIX Swiss Exchange will be making significantly more capacity available to participants during the course
of this year, which will lead to an increase in the minimum bandwidth requirements going forward. We
have decided to offset this increase by reducing the access fees.
Use the link below to see the amended Directive 7:
