Changes to Directive 7 – Access Fee


Dear Sir or Madam
With this Message, we would like to inform you about the impending changes to Annex J of Directive
7 regarding access fees. The changes will take effect as of 1 January 2011.
In conjunction with the harmonization of SIX Swiss Exchange's fee structure, the access fee for
participants, as well as for Independent Software Vendors (ISV) and Application Service Provider
(ASP) with direct connectivity to the SCAP network, will no longer be charged on an annual basis but
instead monthly starting on 1 January 2011.
Moreover, in future these fees will no longer include but rather exclude value-added tax. The new monthly
fee rates correspond to the rounded monthly proportion of the previous annual fee, reduced by the
amount attributable to VAT.

The corresponding paragraphs of Annex J to Directive 7 (Fees and Costs) have been amended and can
be accessed via the following link:
       http://www.six-exchange-regulation.com/regulation/participants_en.html
