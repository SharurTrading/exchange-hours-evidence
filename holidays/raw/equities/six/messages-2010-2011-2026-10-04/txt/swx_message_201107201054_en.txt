Subject         Changes in the registration procedure for traders of new participants

                Nadine Bürgis / Head of Member Services
Pages           1
Date            20.07.2011                                                                         Information


Dear Sir/Madam

On 1 August 2011, a change regarding trader registrations of new participants will enter into force. This
change affects section 3.2.3 of Directive 1 (Admission of Participants; Registration procedure).

SIX Swiss Exchange and Scoach Schweiz can grant traders of new participants a provisional registration
during a three-month period beginning at the point when the new participant begins trading. An individual
trader must apply for the trader examination before commencing trading, otherwise the provisional
registration cannot take place.

The trader with the provisional registration must pass the trader examination within this three-month
period.

The updated version of Directive 1 can be accessed via the following link:

SIX Swiss Exchange: http://www.six-swiss-exchange.com/rule_book/02-REV-DIR01_en.pdf
Scoach Schweiz: http://www.scoach.ch/MediaLibrary/Document/Directive01_en_20110801.pdf

In this respect, please note that the regulations for traders of existing participants that are being
registered for the first time are unchanged, i.e. it is the responsibility of the participant to acquire this
license before commencing trading
