Adjustments to the equity indices on 20 September 2010




Dear Sir or Madam
Based on the criteria set out in the index regulations of the SIX Swiss Exchange (rules governing the
   ®     ®      ®         ®
SMI , SLI , SPI and SXI indices), the scheduled index adjustments will be conducted on 17 September
2010 after the market close and take effect as of 20 September 2010.
A provisional list with newly valid outstanding share totals and free float values is accessible at the
SIX Swiss Exchange website:
http://www.six-swiss-exchange.com/indices/adjustments_en.html
The final list will be published on 13 September 2010 prior to the opening. This allows any last-minute
updates to be taken into account.
