Subject             Definite index adjustment effective from 19 September 2011
Category            Index Adjustments
Authorised by       Werner Bürki, Member of the Management Committee
Pages               1
Date                12.09.2011                                                           Information


On 22 August 2011, the Index Department published a provisional list of newly valid outstanding share
totals and free float values (see SIX Swiss Exchange message 47/2011).
In the meantime, some last-minute changes have been taken into account in order to ensure that the new
index basket weights correspond to the current status.
The definite list of all changes effective from 19 September 2011 is now accessible at the SIX Swiss
Exchange website:
http://www.six-swiss-exchange.com/indices/adjustments_en.html
