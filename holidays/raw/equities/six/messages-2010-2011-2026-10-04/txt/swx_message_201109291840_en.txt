SIX Swiss Exchange Message no. 57/2011

Subject          SWXess Maintenance Release 1.2 launch postponed
Category         SWXess Trading Platform
Authorised by    Christoph Landis; Head Operations
                 Adrian Isler; Head Market Operations
Pages            1
Date             29.09.2011                                                                Information


In Message no. 43/2011 of August 3, 2011, we announced the launch of SWXess Maintenance Release
1.2 (SMR 1.2) into production effective October 3.
Due to only recently-identified errors in post-trade processing on the SWXess platform, SIX Swiss
Exchange has decided to postpone the launch of SMR 1.2 for a few weeks.
As a result, none of the adjustments described in Message 43/2011 will be active as of October 3. They
will not become effective until the new launch date, which will be determined and publicized shortly.
We regret the inconvenience associated with such a short-term announcement, and thank you for your
understanding.
