Subject         Extension of Fee Holiday for SLS
Category        General Information

Date            31.08.2011                                                                 Information


SIX Swiss Exchange Liquidnet Service (SLS), which launched on 8.7.2011, has shown a total turnover for
CH, DE, FR, NL and UK markets of 138 million CHF in July and 227 million CHF in August.
We would like to thank you for this success by extending the fee holiday for SLS transactions until
30.9.2011.
