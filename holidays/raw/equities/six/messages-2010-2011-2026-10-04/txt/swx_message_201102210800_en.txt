Adjustments to the equity indices on 21 March 2011


Dear Sir or Madam
                                                                                                                                         ®
Based on the criteria set out in the index regulations of SIX Swiss Exchange (rules governing the SMI ,
   ®     ®         ®
SLI , SPI and SXI indices), the scheduled index adjustments will be conducted on 18 March 2011 after
the market close and take effect as of 21 March 2011.
A provisional list with newly valid outstanding share totals and free float values is accessible at SIX
Swiss Exchange website:
http://www.six-swiss-exchange.com/indices/adjustments_en.html
The final list will be published on 14 March 2011 prior to the opening. This allows any last-minute updates
to be taken into account.
