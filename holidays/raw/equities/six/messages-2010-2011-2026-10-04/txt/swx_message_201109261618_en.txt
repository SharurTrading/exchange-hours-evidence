Subject          SIX Swiss Exchange Liquidnet Service (SLS) – Fees
Category         General Information
Authorised by    Daniel Risold; Head Business Engineering
                 Martin Schönenberger; Head Member Relations
Pages            1
Date             26.9.2011                                                              Information


We are delighted to announce that as from 1 October 2011, transactions carried out through SIX Swiss
Exchange’s Liquidnet Service (SLS) will be priced at an attractive flat rate of 1 bp.
This fee will be applicable for SLS transactions in the following five markets:
          Swiss Equities
          UK Equities
          German Equities
          French Equities
          Dutch Equities
Directive 7 “Fees and Costs” has been changed accordingly and can be found under the following link:
http://www.six-exchange-regulation.com/regulation/participants_en.html
