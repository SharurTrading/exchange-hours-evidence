Changes to Directive 7
Reduction of trading fees for mid- & small caps as well as shares with a secondary listing

Dear Sir or Madam

We are pleased to inform you that SIX Swiss Exchange has decided to reduce significantly the trading
fees for the Swiss Mid- & Small Cap Segment as well as for shares with a secondary listing. The following
tariff adjustments will take effect as of 1 January:

           The transaction fee for trades executed via the Capacity Trading Interface (CTI) during
            continuous trading will be lowered from CHF 1.00 to CHF 0.50.
            This represents a halving of the CTI transaction fee for 75% of all shares listed on SIX Swiss
            Exchange.
           For stocks with a secondary listing, the Balanced Tariff will be reduced in line with the tariff model
            for blue chip shares, namely to 0.32 basis points (previously 0.54bp) with a per-trade maximum of
            CHF 32 (previously CHF 54).

The corresponding passages of Directive 7 (Fees and Costs) in Annexes B and C have been amended
accordingly. The revised Directive 7 can be accessed via the following link:

                  http://www.six-swiss-exchange.com/participants/regulation/directives_en.html
