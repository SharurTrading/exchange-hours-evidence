SIX Swiss Exchange Member Services and SIX Exfeed would like to inform you that new versions of
Directive 6 – Market Information for SIX Swiss Exchange Ltd and Scoach Schweiz AG will take effect on
1 January 2012. Details about the use of price data and the related changes can be found in sections 4
and 5 – Authorization required for further use.
