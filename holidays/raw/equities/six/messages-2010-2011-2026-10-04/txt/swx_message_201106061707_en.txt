SIX Swiss Exchange further extends range of services for participants



Dear Sir or Madam

We are pleased to announce the immediate introduction of our new Participant Report. It contains a
graphical and tabulated overview of trading activities at participant level (Member Organization ID).

The new Participant Report is provided free of charge in the Member Section under the heading
“Business Reports”.

The launch of the Participant Report follows a successful three-month pilot phase during which we
received feedback from several participants on the report’s information content. We were then able to
optimize the report and incorporate most of the suggested changes.

We are delighted to be adding value for you with this extension of our range of services.

Please do not hesitate to contact your Account Manager if you have any questions or feedback.


Yours sincerely

SIX Swiss Exchange Ltd.
