Change in trading segments of Transocean Ltd and GAM Holding due to extraordinary inclusion
of Transocean Ltd in SMI and SLI


Dear Sir or Madam
SIX Swiss Exchange informed you on 3 June 2010 of the extraordinary inclusion of Transocean Ltd in the
SMI and SLI (SIX Swiss Exchange Message No. 46/2010).
This message contains the key information regarding the consequences of this extraordinary index
composition change.
The inclusion of Transocean Ltd in the SMI and SLI and the resulting elimination of GAM Holding from the
SLI mean that both stocks will be moved to different trading segments as of 21 June 2010.
Transocean Ltd will be moved from the Small and Mid Cap segment (market code XSWX) to the Blue
Chips segment (market code XVTX), while GAM Holding will be moved from the Blue Chips segment to
the Small and Mid Cap segment. The elimination of Swiss Life Holding from the SMI does not result in a
change of trading segment because the stock remains in the SLI.
All good-till-date orders relating to the stocks listed below will be deleted from the order books after the
close of trading at 5.30 p.m. CET on 18 June 2010 (the last trading day before the change of segment).
Members will receive confirmation that their orders have been deleted in the form of an STI Execution
Report Message with OrdStatus “Canceled” and Text “0 : delisted”.
Participants are responsible for entering these orders into the new order books by the start of trading on
21 June 2010 at the latest.
