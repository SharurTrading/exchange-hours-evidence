Change in trading segments for Weatherford International Ltd and Petroplus Holdings Ltd due to
extraordinary inclusion of Weatherford International in SLI®


Dear Sir/Madam

SIX Swiss Exchange informed you in a media release on 7 March 2011 of the extraordinary inclusion of
Weatherford International Ltd in the Swiss Leader Index (SLI®). This message contains the key
information regarding the consequences of this extraordinary index composition change.

The inclusion of Weatherford International Ltd in the SLI® and the resulting elimination of Petroplus
Holdings Ltd from the index mean that both stocks will be moved to different trading segments as of
21 March 2011. Weatherford International Ltd will be moved from the Mid and Small Cap segment
(market code XSWX) to the Blue Chip segment (market code XVTX), while Petroplus Holdings Ltd will be
moved from the Blue Chip segment to the Mid and Small Cap segment.

All good-till-date orders relating to the two stocks will be deleted from the order books after the close of
trading at 5.30 p.m. CET on 18 March 2011 (the last trading day before the change in segments).
Members will receive confirmation that their orders have been deleted in the form of an STI Execution
Report Message with OrdStatus “Canceled” and Text “0 : delisted”.
We will leave it to them to enter these orders into the new order books by the start of trading on 21 March
2011.
