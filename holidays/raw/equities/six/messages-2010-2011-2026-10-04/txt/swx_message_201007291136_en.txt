Deactivation of Commerzbank AG – ex DKBGB

Dear Sir or Madam
SIX Swiss Exchange message no. 27/2009 of 22 May 2009 communicated the legal integration of the
former Dresdner Bank AG London Branch, as a result of the takeover by Commerzbank AG, Frankfurt.
This legal integration was implemented on 11 May 2009. Because the technical readiness was not given
at the time, the former Dresdner Bank AG London Branch continued to appear on the market under the
new name of Commerzbank AG, ex DKBGB.
The technical migration has now been completed and Commerzbank AG, ex DKBGB, Member Org ID
193, will no longer appear on the market as of Monday, 2nd August 2010.

Member Organisation to be deactivated as of 2nd August 2010:
