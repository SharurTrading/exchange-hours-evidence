New sector indices have been added to the index universe of the Swiss Bond Index SBI®
as at 3 January 2011


Dear Sir or Madam
We are happy to announce that SIX Swiss Exchange has added 180 new indices to the SBI Index family
as at 3 January 2011. A number of SBI sector indices have been launched based on the ICB Fixed
Income classification benchmark, which classifies issuers according to their economic activity, and a
model for guarantee types and collateral; this model was developed by SIX Swiss Exchange in
partnership with the Swiss Bond Commission (SBC) and allows individual issues to be allocated to sub-
indices based on bond-specific guarantees and collateral.
