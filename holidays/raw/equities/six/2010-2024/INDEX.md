## Reading notes

- Each year's rows key on the dark `Market Holiday — Market Closed` cells of
  the year's own Trading Calendar PDF. The 2012-2016 editions print the cells
  red (fill rgb ≈ (0.84, 0.17, 0.12)); the 2017-2027 editions dark blue
  (rgb ≈ (0.0, 0.17, 0.37)). Adjacent holidays print as one merged rectangle.
- Extraction was cell-by-cell from the PDF vector fills with the day number
  read inside the cell rect, cross-checked against the weekday column of each
  resolved date, then re-read cell by cell from page renderings; the 2025 PDF
  was decoded with the same tooling and reproduces the shipped 2025 rows
  exactly.
- 2010-2011 and 2018-2019 are unaudited spans (issue #212): the era's grid
  documents are settlement/currency calendars and state in their own prose
  that the trading calendar is a different document.
