Summary
Coinbase Derivatives, LLC ("CDE" or "Exchange") is deferring the rolling gateway deploys
during market hours and the removal of the weekly 1-hour Friday maintenance window
described in Market Notice 26-33.2 (published 09/10/2026).

This notice amends Market Notice 26-33.2 and Market Notice 26-25 (published 05/20/2026).



Customer Impact
    ●​ The weekly 1-hour Friday maintenance window (16:00 to 17:00 CT) remains in effect.
       CDE deployments will take place during this window.
    ●​ The Friday 14:30 CT rolling deploy events listed in Market Notice 26-33.2 are canceled,
       including Friday, Sept 25, 2026 and all weekly Friday events through GoLive.
    ●​ Products in the weekend trading set will keep their current trading hours, including the
       Friday maintenance window. The TradingSessionID (336) value in FIX Market Data,
       introduced in Market Notice 26-32, will not change to TRUE_24X7 until the transition is
       rescheduled.
    ●​ The Connectivity Recommendations in Market Notice 26-33.2 (aggregate ports for
       Internet sessions, ResetSeqNumFlag=Y (Tag 141) on logon, and ExecID-based
       recovery) remain recommended as safeguards for unplanned service events.
    ●​ https://status.cde.coinbase.com/ is updated before each deployment, during any
       unexpected issues, and at the conclusion of the deployment.
