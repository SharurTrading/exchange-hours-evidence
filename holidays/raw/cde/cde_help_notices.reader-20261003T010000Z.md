[Derivatives](https://help.coinbase.com/derivatives)
Market Notices are official announcements or notifications issued by the Exchange to inform market participants about various matters affecting trading activities or market operations. These notices are important for traders, investors, and other stakeholders to stay informed and compliant with Exchange Rules.

If you wish to receive Market Notices please reach out to [derivatives@coinbase.com](mailto:derivatives@coinbase.com).

Market Notices are also posted to our [website](https://www.coinbase.com/derivatives/market-notices).
