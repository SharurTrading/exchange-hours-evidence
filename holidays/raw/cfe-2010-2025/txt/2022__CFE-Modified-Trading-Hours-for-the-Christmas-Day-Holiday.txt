CFE Modified Trading Hours for the Christmas Day Holiday
Notice Date: December 12, 2022


Overview

Cboe Futures Exchange, LLC (CFE) will have modified trading hours for the Christmas Day holiday, which will be observed on Monday, December 26, 2022.
