CFE Modified Trading Hours for the Presidents' Day Holiday
Notice Date: February 6, 2023


Overview

Cboe Futures Exchange, LLC (CFE) will have modified trading hours for the Presidents' Day holiday, which will be observed on Monday, February 20, 2023.

Holiday Trading Schedule

The following holiday trading schedule will apply for CFE products. All extended trading hours (ETH) and regular trading hours (RTH) times referenced in this notice are Central
Time.
