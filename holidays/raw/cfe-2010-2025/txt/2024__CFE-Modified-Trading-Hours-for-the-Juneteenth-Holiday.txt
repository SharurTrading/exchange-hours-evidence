CFE Modified Trading Hours for the Juneteenth Holiday
Notice Date: May 29, 2024


Overview

Cboe Futures Exchange, LLC (CFE) will have modified trading hours for the Juneteenth holiday, which will be observed on Wednesday, June 19, 2024.

Holiday Trading Schedule

The following holiday trading schedule will apply for CFE products. All extended trading hours (ETH) and regular trading hours (RTH) times referenced in this notice are Central Time.

Holiday Testing Opportunity

To provide additional opportunities for holiday testing, the CFE certification environment will be configured to observe a holiday trading session beginning on Wednesday, June 5,
2024. The modified trading hours above will apply in the CFE certification environment on the same days of the week as above commencing on the testing start date.
