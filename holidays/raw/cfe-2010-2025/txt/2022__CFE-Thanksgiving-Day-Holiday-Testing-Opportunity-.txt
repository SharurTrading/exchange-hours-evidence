CFE Thanksgiving Day Testing Opportunity
Notice Date: November 10, 2022


Overview

To provide additional opportunities for holiday testing, the Cboe Futures Exchange, LLC
(CFE) certification environment will be configured to observe a holiday trading session
beginning Wednesday, November 16, 2022. The test is designed to mimic the modified
trading hours associated with the 2022 Thanksgiving Day holiday. After the start of the
CFE certification trading environment on the afternoon of November 16, 2022, the
simulated holiday trading session will commence for the business date of November
18, 2022.
