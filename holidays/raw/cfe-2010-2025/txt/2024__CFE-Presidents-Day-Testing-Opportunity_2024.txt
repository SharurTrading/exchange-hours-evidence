CFE Presidents’ Day Testing Opportunity
Reference ID: C2024012903


OVERVIEW
Applicable Cboe Exchanges: CFE
To provide additional opportunities for holiday testing, the Cboe Futures Exchange, LLC (CFE) certification
environment will be configured to observe a holiday trading session beginning Sunday, February 4, 2024.
The test is designed to mimic the modified trading hours associated with the 2024 Presidents’ Day holiday.
