Cboe Trading Schedule Update ─ Juneteenth National
Independence Day
Reference ID: C2021061701


Overview
Applicable Cboe Exchanges: BYX Equities, BZX Equities, EDGA Equities, EDGX Equities, BZX Options, Cboe
Options, C2 Options, EDGX Options, Cboe Future Exchange (“CFE”)

In anticipation of President Joe Biden signing the bill to make Juneteenth a federal holiday, please be
advised that in the interest of maintaining fair and orderly markets Cboe will not be adjusting trading
hours for BYX Exchange, BZX Exchange, EDGA Exchange, EDGX Exchange, BZX Options Exchange, Cboe
Options Exchange, C2 Options Exchange, EDGX Options Exchange, and CFE for Friday, June 18, 2021 or
Monday, June 21, 2021. Cboe is fully supportive of this bill and looks forward to working with the industry
to consider how best to handle trading schedules for our markets in support of Juneteenth in the years to
come.


Additional Information

Please contact the Cboe Trade Desk or your Director of Sales with any questions.
