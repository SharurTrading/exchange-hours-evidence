CFE Presidents’ Day Holiday Testing Opportunity
Notice Date: February 7, 2023


Overview

To provide additional opportunities for holiday testing, the Cboe Futures Exchange, LLC
(CFE) certification environment will be configured to observe a holiday trading session
beginning Sunday, February 12, 2023. The test is designed to mimic the modified
trading hours associated with the 2023 Presidents’ Day holiday. After the start of the
CFE certification trading environment on the morning of February 12, 2023, the
simulated holiday trading session will commence for the business date of February 14,
2023.
