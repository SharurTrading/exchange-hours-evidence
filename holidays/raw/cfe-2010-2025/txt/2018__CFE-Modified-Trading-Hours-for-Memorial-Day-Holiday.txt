CFE Modified Trading Hours for Memorial Day Holiday
Reference ID: C2018051700


Overview
This notice sets forth the modified trading hours on Cboe Futures Exchange, LLC (CFE) for Cboe
Volatility Index (VX) and Cboe Bitcoin (USD) (XBT) futures and all other CFE products for the Memorial
Day holiday. All times referenced in this circular are Chicago time.


Holiday Trading Schedule for Monday, May 28, 2018
VX and XBT Futures
The schedule below will apply for the Monday, May 28, 2018 holiday trading session in VX and XBT
futures, including Trade at Settlement (TAS) transactions in VX futures (Ticker: VXT):

  Type of Trading Hours            Monday, May 28, 2018                 Tuesday, May 29,2018
        Extended               5:00 p.m. (Sunday) to 10:30 a.m.     5:00 p.m. (Monday) to 8:30 a.m.
         Regular                            None                         8:30 a.m. to 3:15 p.m.

Trades in VX futures (including TAS transactions in VX futures) during the May 28, 2018 holiday trading
session will be submitted for clearing on the Business Day of Tuesday, May 29, 2018.

A holiday trading session includes extended trading hours on the calendar day of the holiday and any
extended trading hours for the holiday on the previous calendar day. Holiday trading sessions are not
separate Business Days and are part of the next Business Day. Trading in VX and XBT futures is
suspended between sessions of extended trading hours on the calendar day of a holiday.

ECRP Transactions and Block Trades in VX Futures
Exchange of Contract for Related Position (ECRP) transactions and Block Trades in VX and XBT futures
may be consummated during the May 28, 2018 trading session if they satisfy all of the following three
requirements: The ECRP or Block Trade:

       (i)     must be agreed to during a queuing period or trading hours during the May 28, 2018
               holiday trading session;
       (ii)    must be fully reported to CFE by no later than 30 minutes after the transaction is agreed
               upon for an ECRP transaction and by no later than 10 minutes after the transaction is
               agreed upon for a Block Trade; and

       (iii)   must be fully reported to CFE within a queuing period or trading hours during the May
               28, 2018 holiday trading session.

An ECRP transaction or Block Trade may not be reported during the suspension period between 10:30
a.m. to 4:45 p.m. on Monday, May 28, 2018.

Additionally, ECRP transactions and Block Trades must be reported through the CFE Block Trade/ECRP
Reporting Tool as is the case on regular trading days.

All Other Products
Trading will be closed for all CFE products other than VX and XBT futures on Monday, May 28, 2018.
Additionally, no ECRP transactions or Block Trades may occur in these other products on Monday, May
28, 2018.
