CFE Modified Trading Hours for the Independence Day Holiday
Notice Date: June 11, 2026


Overview

Cboe Futures Exchange, LLC (CFE) will have modified trading hours for the Independence Day holiday, which will be observed on Friday, July 3, 2026.

Holiday Trading Schedule

The following holiday trading schedule will apply for CFE products. All extended trading hours (ETH) and regular trading hours (RTH) times referenced in this notice are Central Time.

Holiday Testing Opportunity

To provide additional opportunities for holiday testing, the CFE certification environment will be configured to observe a holiday trading session beginning on Friday, June 26, 2026. The
modified trading hours above will apply in the CFE certification environment on the same days of the week as above commencing on the testing start date.
