CFE Modified Trading Hours for the Good Friday Holiday
Overview
This notice sets forth the modified trading hours on Cboe Futures Exchange, LLC (CFE) for Cboe
Volatility Index (VX), Cboe Bitcoin (USD) (XBT) futures, and all other CFE products for the Good Friday
holiday. All times referenced in this circular are Chicago time.

Trading Schedule for Thursday, March 29, 2018

Trading in VX and XBT futures will close at 4:00 p.m. on Thursday, March 29, 2018. Trading in all other
CFE products will close at 3:15 p.m. on Thursday, March 29, 2018.

The trading hours for all Trade at Settlement (TAS) transactions in VX futures (VXT) will end at 3:13 p.m.
on March 29, 2018.

Block Trades must be fully reported by no later than 10 minutes after the transaction is agreed upon,
and Exchange of Contract for Related Position (ECRP) transactions must be fully reported by no later
than 30 minutes after the transaction is agreed upon.

Additionally, TAS Block Trades and TAS ECRP transactions must be fully reported by no later than 3:13
p.m. on March 29, 2018, and Non-TAS Block Trades and Non-TAS ECRP transactions must be reported
to CFE by no later than 4:00 p.m. on March 29, 2018.

There will be no extended trading hours for VX and XBT futures on Thursday, March 29, 2018 after the
close of trading at 4:00 p.m.

Good Friday Holiday Schedule for Friday, March 30, 2018

Trading will be closed for all CFE products on Friday, March 30, 2018.

No Block Trades or ECRP transactions may occur in CFE products on March 30, 2018.

Resumption of Normal Trading Schedule on Sunday, April 1, 2018
CFE will have a normal trading schedule on Sunday, April 1, 2018. CFE will reopen following the Good
Friday holiday at 5:00 p.m. on April 1, 2018 with the regularly scheduled start of extended trading hours
for VX and XBT futures.
