CFE Modified Trading Hours for the Christmas Day Holiday
Notice Date: December 4, 2024


Overview

Cboe Futures Exchange, LLC (CFE) will have modified trading hours for the Christmas Day holiday, which will be observed on Wednesday, December 25, 2024.

Holiday Testing Opportunity

To provide additional opportunities for holiday testing, the CFE certification environment will be configured to observe a holiday trading session beginning on Tuesday, December 10,
2024. The modified trading hours above will apply in the CFE certification environment on the same days of the week as above commencing on the testing start date.
