CFE Modified Trading Hours for the Good Friday Holiday
Reference ID: C2019032601


Overview
This notice sets forth the modified trading hours on Cboe Futures Exchange, LLC (CFE) for Cboe
Volatility Index (VX), Cboe Bitcoin (USD) (XBT) futures, and all other CFE products for the Good Friday
holiday. All times referenced in this circular are Chicago time.


Trading Schedule for Thursday, April 18, 2019
Trading in VX and XBT futures will close at 4:00 p.m. CT on Thursday, April 18, 2019. Trading in all other
CFE products will close at their regular closing times on Thursday, April 18, 2019.

The trading hours for all Trade at Settlement (TAS) transactions in VX futures (VXT) will end at 3:13 p.m.
CT on April 18, 2019.

Block Trades must be fully reported by no later than 10 minutes after the transaction is agreed upon,
and Exchange of Contract for Related Position (ECRP) transactions must be fully reported by no later
than 30 minutes after the transaction is agreed upon.

Additionally, TAS Block Trades and TAS ECRP transactions must be fully reported by no later than the
applicable close of trading hours for TAS transactions by product on April 18, 2019. Non-TAS Block Trades
and Non-TAS ECRP transactions must be reported to CFE by no later than the applicable close of trading
hours for Non-TAS transactions by product on April 18, 2019.

There will be no extended trading hours for VX and XBT futures on Thursday, April 18, 2019 after the
close of trading at 4:00 p.m. CT.


Good Friday Holiday Schedule for Friday, April 19, 2019
Trading will be closed for all CFE products on Friday, April 19, 2019.

No Block Trades or ECRP transactions may occur in CFE products on April 19, 2019.
Resumption of Normal Trading Schedule on Sunday, April 21, 2019
CFE will have a normal trading schedule on Sunday, April 21, 2019. CFE will reopen following the Good
Friday holiday at 5:00 p.m. CT on April 21, 2019 with the regularly scheduled start of extended trading hours
for VX and XBT futures.
