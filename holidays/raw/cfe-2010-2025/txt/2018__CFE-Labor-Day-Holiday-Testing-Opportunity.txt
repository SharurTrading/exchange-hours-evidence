CFE Labor Day Holiday Testing Opportunity
Reference ID: C2018081501


Overview
To provide additional opportunities for holiday testing, the CFE Certification environment will be
configured to observe a holiday trading session beginning Sunday, August 19, 2018. The test is designed
to mimic the modified trading hours associated with the 2018 Labor Day holiday. After the start of the
CFE Certification trading environment on the morning of August 19, 2018, the simulated holiday trading
session will commence for the business date of August 21, 2018.

A holiday trading session includes extended trading hours on the calendar day of the holiday and any
extended trading hours for the holiday on the previous calendar day. Holiday trading sessions are not
separate Business Days and are part of the next Business Day. Trading in VX and XBT futures is suspended
between sessions of extended trading hours on the calendar day of a holiday.
All Other Products

Simulated trading in the certification environment will be closed for all CFE products other than VX and
XBT futures on Monday, August 20, 2018.
