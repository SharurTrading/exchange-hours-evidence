CFE Modified Trading Hours for the Independence Day Holiday
Notice Date: June 20, 2023


Overview

Cboe Futures Exchange, LLC (CFE) will have modified trading hours for the Independence Day holiday, which will be observed on Tuesday, July 4, 2023.

Holiday Trading Schedule

The following holiday trading schedule will apply for CFE products. All extended trading hours (ETH) and regular trading hours (RTH) times referenced in this notice are Central
Time.
