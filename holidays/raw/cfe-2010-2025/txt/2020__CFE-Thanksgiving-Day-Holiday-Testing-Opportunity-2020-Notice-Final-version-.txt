CFE Thanksgiving Day Holiday Testing Opportunity
Reference ID: C2020100701


Overview
To provide additional opportunities for holiday testing, the Cboe Futures Exchange, LLC (CFE) certification
environment will be configured to observe a holiday trading session beginning Wednesday, October 14,
2020. The test is designed to mimic the modified trading hours associated with the 2020 Thanksgiving
Day holiday. After the start of the CFE certification trading environment on the afternoon of October 14,
2020, the simulated holiday trading session will commence for the business date of October 16, 2020.

A holiday trading session includes extended trading hours on the calendar day of the holiday and any
extended trading hours for the holiday on the previous calendar day. In this case, Thursday October 15,
2020 is simulating Thursday, November 26, 2020 as the date of the holiday, which is the date on which
the 2020 Thanksgiving Day holiday is being observed. Holiday trading sessions are not separate business
days and are part of the next business day.
All Other Products

Simulated trading in the certification environment will be closed for all CFE products other than VX, VXM,
VXT, VXMT and AMERIBOR futures on Thursday, October 15, 2020.
