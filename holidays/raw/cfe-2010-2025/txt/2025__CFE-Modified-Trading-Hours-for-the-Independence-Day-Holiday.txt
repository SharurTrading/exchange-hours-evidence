CFE Modified Trading Hours for the Independence Day Holiday
Notice Date: June 12, 2025


Overview

Cboe Futures Exchange, LLC (CFE) will have modified trading hours for the Independence Day holiday, which will be observed on Friday, July 4, 2025.
