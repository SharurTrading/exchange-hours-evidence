CFE Modified Trading Hours for Martin Luther King, Jr.
Holiday
Overview
This notice sets forth the modified trading hours on Cboe Futures Exchange, LLC (CFE) for Cboe
Volatility Index (VX) and Cboe Bitcoin (USD) (XBT) futures and all other CFE products for the Martin
Luther King, Jr. holiday. All times referenced in this circular are Chicago time.


Holiday Trading Schedule for Monday, January 15, 2018
VX and XBT Futures

The schedule below will apply for the Monday, January 15, 2018 holiday trading session in VX and XBT
futures, including Trade at Settlement (TAS) transactions in VX futures (Ticker: VXT).

Type of Trading Hours             Monday, January 15, 2018               Tuesday, January 16, 2018

       Extended                5:00 p.m. (Sunday) to 10:30 a.m.        5:00 p.m. (Monday) to 8:30 a.m.


        Regular                             None                            8:30 a.m. to 3:15 p.m.

Trades in VX and XBT futures (including TAS transactions in VX futures) during the January 15, 2018
holiday trading session will be submitted for clearing on the Business Day of Tuesday, January 16, 2018.

ECRP Transactions and Block Trades in VX Futures

Exchange of Contract for Related Position (ECRP) transactions and Block Trades in VX and XBT futures
may occur during the extended trading hours for VX and XBT futures on Monday, January 15, 2018, but
may not occur outside of those trading hours. ECRP transactions must be reported by no later than 30
minutes after the transaction is agreed upon and Block Trades must be reported by no later than 10
minutes after the transaction is agreed upon. ECRP transactions and Block Trades in XBT futures must
be reported via the Trade Reporting Tool (TRT).
All Other Products

Trading will be closed for all CFE products other than VX and XBT futures on Monday, January 15, 2018.
Additionally, no ECRP transactions or Block Trades may occur in these products on Monday, January
15, 2018.

A holiday trading session includes extended trading hours on the calendar day of the holiday and any
extended trading hours for the holiday on the previous calendar day. Holiday trading sessions are not
separate Business Days and are part of the next Business Day. Trading in VX and XBT futures is halted
between sessions of extended trading hours on the calendar day of a holiday. The CBOE System will
complete the processing of trades that are in the course of being processed by the CBOE System prior
to the start of such a halt period.
