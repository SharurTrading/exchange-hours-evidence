Reminder - Impact of Independence Day Holiday Closures on Weekly
VIX Derivatives
Reference ID: C 2 0 2 0 0 5 2 8 0 1


Overview
Applicable Cboe Exchange: Cboe Options and Cboe Futures
As a reminder, VIX options (symbol: VIXW) and VX futures (symbol: VX22/M0) that have an expiration date
of Tuesday, June 2, 2020, will also settle on Tuesday, June 2, 2020. The adjusted June 2, 2020 expiration and
settlement date is due to the constituent S&P 500 Index options (symbol: SPXW) being closed for trading on
July 3, 2020.
