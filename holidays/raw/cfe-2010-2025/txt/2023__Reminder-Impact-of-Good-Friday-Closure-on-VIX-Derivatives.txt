Reminder - Impact of Good Friday Closure on VIX Derivatives
Reference ID: C2023030200


Overview
Applicable Cboe Exchange: Cboe Options, CFE
As a reminder, certain VIX options (symbol: VIXW) as well as one VX future (symbol: VX10/H3) will have an
expiration date of Tuesday, March 7, 2023. The settlement process for weekly VIX options and the weekly
VX10/H3 future will occur on Tuesday instead of Wednesday next week due to the constituent S&P 500
Index options (symbol: SPXW) used to calculate the settlement value being closed for trading on Friday, April
7, 2023.

Additional Information
Questions regarding this notice can be directed to the Cboe Operations Support Center, Cboe Trade Desk,
or CFE Trade Desk.
