CFE Modified Trading Hours for the Martin Luther King, Jr. Day Holiday
Notice Date: December 29, 2023


Overview

Cboe Futures Exchange, LLC (CFE) will have modified trading hours for the Martin Luther King, Jr. Day holiday, which will be observed on Monday, January 15, 2024.
