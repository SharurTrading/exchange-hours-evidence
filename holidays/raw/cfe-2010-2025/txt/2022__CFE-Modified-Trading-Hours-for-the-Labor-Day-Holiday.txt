CFE Modified Trading Hours for the Labor Day Holiday
Notice Date: August 22, 2022


Overview

Cboe Futures Exchange, LLC (CFE) will have modified trading hours for the Labor Day
holiday, which will be observed on Monday, September 5, 2022.
