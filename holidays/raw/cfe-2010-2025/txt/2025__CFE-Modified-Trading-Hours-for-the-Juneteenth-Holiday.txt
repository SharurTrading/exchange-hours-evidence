CFE Modified Trading Hours for the Juneteenth Holiday
Notice Date: May 29, 2025


Overview

Cboe Futures Exchange, LLC (CFE) will have modified trading hours for the Juneteenth holiday, which will be observed on Thursday, June 19, 2025.
