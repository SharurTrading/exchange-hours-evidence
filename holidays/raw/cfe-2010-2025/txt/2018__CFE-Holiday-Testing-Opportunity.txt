CFE Holiday Testing Opportunity
Reference ID: C2018061802


Overview
To provide additional opportunities for holiday testing, the CFE Certification environment will be
configured to observe a half day trading session followed by a holiday trading session beginning on
Wednesday, June 27, 2018. The test is designed to mimic the modified trading hours associated with
the Independence Day holiday. The testing opportunity will begin with an early close for business date
June 27 at 12:15 pm CT. Simulated daily settlement price dissemination will occur shortly after the
close of trading. After the restart of the CFE trading environment on the afternoon of Wednesday, June
27, the holiday trading session will commence for business date of June 29, 2018.


Simulated Modified Trading Schedule for Wednesday, June 27, 2018
Trading in all futures products in the CFE Certification Environment will close at 12:15 p.m. on
Wednesday, June 27, 2018. Simulated daily settlements will be calculated shortly thereafter.

The trading hours for all Trade at Settlement (TAS) transactions in VX futures (VXT) in the CFE
Certification Environment will end at 12:13 p.m. on June 27, 2018.

A holiday trading session includes extended trading hours on the calendar day of the holiday and any
extended trading hours for the holiday on the previous calendar day. Holiday trading sessions are not
separate Business Days and are part of the next Business Day. Trading in VX and XBT futures is
suspended between sessions of extended trading hours on the calendar day of a holiday.

All Other Products

Trading will be closed for all CFE products in the CFE Certification Environment other than VX and XBT
futures after 12:15 p.m. on June 27, 2018 and on June 28, 2018.
