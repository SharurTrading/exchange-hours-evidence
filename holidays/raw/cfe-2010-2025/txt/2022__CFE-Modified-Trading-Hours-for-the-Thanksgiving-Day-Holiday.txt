CFE Modified Trading Hours for the Thanksgiving Day Holiday
Notice Date: November 10, 2022


Overview

Cboe Futures Exchange, LLC (CFE) will have modified trading hours for the Thanksgiving Day holiday, which will be observed on Thursday, November 24, 2022.

Holiday Trading Schedule

The following holiday trading schedule will apply for CFE products. All extended trading hours (ETH) and regular trading hours (RTH) times referenced in this notice are Central
Time.
