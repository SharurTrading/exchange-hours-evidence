CFE Thanksgiving Day Holiday Testing Opportunity
Reference ID: C2018102902


Overview
To provide additional opportunities for holiday testing, the Cboe Futures Exchange, LLC (CFE) Certification
environment will be configured to observe a holiday trading session beginning Wednesday, November 7,
2018. The test is designed to mimic the modified trading hours associated with the 2018 Thanksgiving
Day holiday. After the start of the CFE Certification trading environment on the afternoon of November
7, 2018, the simulated holiday trading session will commence for the business date of November 9, 2018.

A holiday trading session includes extended trading hours on the calendar day of the holiday and any
extended trading hours for the holiday on the previous calendar day. Holiday trading sessions are not
separate Business Days and are part of the next Business Day. Trading in VX and XBT futures is suspended
between sessions of extended trading hours on the calendar day of a holiday.

All Other Products
Simulated trading in the certification environment will be closed for all CFE products other than VX and
XBT futures on Thursday, November 8, 2018.

On Friday, November 9, 2018, all CFE products other than VX futures will open at their regularly
scheduled opening times and close at 12:15 p.m.
The queuing period for the Friday trading session for non-TAS single leg contract expirations and non-TAS
spreads in CFE products other than VX and XBT futures will commence at 5:00 p.m. on Thursday,
November 9, 2018 plus a randomized time period from zero to three seconds. The queuing period for the
Friday trading session for any TAS single leg contract expirations and TAS spreads in CFE products other
than VX and XBT futures will commence at 5:00 p.m. on Thursday, November 9, 2018 plus a randomized
time period from three to six seconds.

Session Disconnect from CFE Certification Environment on November 8, 2018
At approximately 4:10 p.m. on November 8, 2018, a session disconnect from the CFE Certification
Environment will occur during the simulated 10:30 a.m. to 4:45 p.m. suspended state.

This disconnect will not cause any orders to cancel due to Cancel on Disconnect. As a general rule, Cancel
on Disconnect is not in effect between the scheduled end of trading for a given futures product and the
next system restart. Trading Privilege Holders may refer to the FIX and BOE specifications for further
information on how to configure cancel on disconnect settings.
