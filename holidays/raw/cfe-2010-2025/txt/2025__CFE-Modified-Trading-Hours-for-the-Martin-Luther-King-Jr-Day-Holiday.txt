CFE Modified Trading Hours for the Martin Luther King, Jr. Day Holiday
Notice Date: December 26, 2025


Overview

Cboe Futures Exchange, LLC (CFE) will have modified trading hours for the Martin Luther King, Jr. Day holiday, which will be observed on Monday, January 19, 2026.

Holiday Testing Opportunity

To provide additional opportunities for holiday testing, the CFE certification environment will be configured to observe a holiday trading session beginning on Monday, January 12, 2026.
The modified trading hours above will apply in the CFE certification environment on the same days of the week as above commencing on the testing start date.
