Cboe Global Markets Observing Normal Trading Hours on
December 30, December 31, and January 3
Reference ID: C2021121601


Overview
Applicable Cboe Exchanges: BZX Options, Cboe Options, C2 Options, EDGX Options, BYZ Equities, BZX
Equities, EDGA Equities, EDGX Equities, CFE

All Cboe-affiliated Options Exchanges (Cboe Options, C2, BZX, and EDGX), all Cboe-affiliated Equities
Exchanges (BYZ, BZX, EDGA, and EDGX), and CFE will follow normal trading hours on the following dates.

   •   Thursday, December 30, 2021
   •   Friday, December 31, 2021 (New Year’s Eve)
   •   Monday, January 3, 2022


Additional Information
