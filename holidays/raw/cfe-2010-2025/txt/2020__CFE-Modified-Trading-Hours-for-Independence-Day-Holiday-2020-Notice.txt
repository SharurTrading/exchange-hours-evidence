CFE Modified Trading Hours for the Independence Day
Holiday
Reference ID: C2020061700


Overview
This notice sets forth the modified trading hours on Cboe Futures Exchange, LLC (CFE) for Cboe Volatility
Index (VX) futures, AMERIBOR (AMB3, AMB1, and AMW) futures, and all other CFE products for the
Independence Day Holiday.


Trading Schedule for Thursday, July 2, 2020
CFE will have normal trading hours for all products on Thursday, July 2, 2020.

Trades in VX (including VXT TAS transactions) and AMERIBOR futures during the July 3, 2020 holiday
trading session will be submitted for clearing on the Business Day of Monday, July 6, 2020.

A holiday trading session includes extended trading hours on the calendar day of the holiday and any
extended trading hours for the holiday on the previous calendar day. In this case, the Independence
Day holiday is being observed on July 3, 2020 and the calendar day of the holiday is July 3, 2020. Holiday
trading sessions are not separate Business Days and are part of the next Business Day.

The daily settlement prices for the combined Business Day that encompasses the holiday trading session
on Friday, July 3, 2020 and the extended and regular trading hours for Monday July 6, 2020 will be
determined on Monday, July 6, 2020.

All Other Products

Trading will be closed for all CFE products other than VX and AMERIBOR futures on Friday, July 3, 2020.


ECRP Transactions and Block Trades
VX and AMERIBOR Futures

Exchange of Contract for Related Position (ECRP) transactions and Block Trades in VX and AMERIBOR
futures (including VXT TAS transactions) may be consummated during the July 3, 2020 trading session if
they satisfy all of the following three requirements. The ECRP or Block Trade:

        (i)     must be agreed to during a queuing period or the applicable trading hours during the
                July 3, 2020 trading session;

        (ii)    must be fully reported to CFE by no later than 30 minutes after the transaction is agreed
                upon for an ECRP transaction and by no later than 10 minutes after the transaction is
                agreed upon for a Block Trade; and

        (iii)   must be fully reported to CFE within a queuing period or the applicable trading hours
                during the July 3, 2020 trading session.

An ECRP transaction or Block Trade may not be reported during the suspension period between 10:30
a.m. CT on Friday, July 3, 2020 and 4:00 p.m. CT on Sunday, July 5, 2020

Additionally, ECRP transactions and Block Trades must be reported through the CFE Block
Trade/ECRP Reporting Tool as is the case on regular trading days.

All Other Products

No ECRP transactions or Block Trades may occur in any CFE products other than VX and AMERIBOR
futures on July 3, 2020.
