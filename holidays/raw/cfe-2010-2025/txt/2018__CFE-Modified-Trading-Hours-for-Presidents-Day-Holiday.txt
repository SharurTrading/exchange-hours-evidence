CFE Modified Trading Hours for Presidents’ Day Holiday
Overview
This notice sets forth the modified trading hours on Cboe Futures Exchange, LLC (CFE) for Cboe
Volatility Index (VX) and Cboe bitcoin (USD) (XBT) futures and all other CFE products for the Presidents’
Day holiday. All times referenced in this circular are Chicago time.


Holiday Trading Schedule for Monday, February 19, 2018
VX and XBT Futures
The schedule below will apply for the Monday, February 19, 2018 holiday trading session in VX and XBT
futures, including Trade at Settlement (TAS) transactions in VX futures (Ticker: VXT):

  Type of Trading Hours          Monday, February 19, 2018             Tuesday, February 20, 2018
        Extended               5:00 p.m. (Sunday) to 10:30 a.m.       5:00 p.m. (Monday) to 8:30 a.m.
         Regular                            None                           8:30 a.m. to 3:15 p.m.

Trades in VX futures (including TAS transactions in VX futures) during the February 19, 2018 holiday
trading session will be submitted for clearing on the Business Day of Tuesday, February 20, 2018.

ECRP Transactions and Block Trades in VX Futures
Exchange of Contract for Related Position (ECRP) transactions and Block Trades in VX and XBT futures
may occur during the extended trading hours for VX and XBT futures on Monday, February 19, 2018, but
may not occur outside of those trading hours. ECRP transactions must be reported by no later than 30
minutes after the transaction is agreed upon and Block Trades must be reported by no later than 10
minutes after the transaction is agreed upon. ECRP transactions and Block Trades in XBT futures must
be reported via the Trade Reporting Tool (TRT).

All Other Products
Trading will be closed for all CFE products other than VX and XBT futures on Monday, February 19, 2018.
Additionally, no ECRP transactions or Block Trades may occur in these other products on Monday,
February 19, 2018.

A holiday trading session includes extended trading hours on the calendar day of the holiday and any
extended trading hours for the holiday on the previous calendar day. Holiday trading sessions are
not separate Business Days and are part of the next Business Day. Trading in VX and XBT futures is
halted between sessions of extended trading hours on the calendar day of a holiday. The Cboe System
will complete the processing of trades that are in the course of being processed by the Cboe System
prior to the start of such a halt period. Trades in VX and XBT futures (including TAS transactions in
VX futures) during the Presidents’ Day holiday trading session will be submitted for clearing on the
Business Day of Tuesday, February 20, 2018.
