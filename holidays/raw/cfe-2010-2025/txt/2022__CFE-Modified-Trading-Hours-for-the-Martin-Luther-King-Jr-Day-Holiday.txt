CFE Modified Trading Hours for the Martin Luther King, Jr. Day Holiday
Notice Date: December 30, 2022


Overview

Cboe Futures Exchange, LLC (CFE) will have modified trading hours for the Martin Luther King, Jr. Day holiday, which will be observed on Monday, January 16, 2023.

Holiday Trading Schedule

The following holiday trading schedule will apply for CFE products. All extended trading hours (ETH) and regular trading hours (RTH) times referenced in this notice are Central
Time.
