CFE Modified Trading Hours for the Juneteenth Holiday
Notice Date: June 5, 2023


Overview

Cboe Futures Exchange, LLC (CFE) will have modified trading hours for the Juneteenth holiday, which will be observed on Monday, June 19, 2023.

The following holiday trading schedule will apply for CFE products. All extended trading hours (ETH) and regular trading hours (RTH) times referenced in this notice are Central
