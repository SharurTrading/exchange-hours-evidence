CFE Memorial Day Holiday and BOEv3 Failover Testing
Opportunities
Reference ID: C2021051400


Overview

To provide additional opportunities for testing holiday schedule system behavior, the CFE certification trading
environment will be configured to observe a simulated Memorial Day holiday trading session beginning
Sunday, May 23, 2021. CFE will also conduct BOEv3 protocol failover testing in its CFE certification
environment on Wednesday, May 26, 2021 and Thursday, May 27, 2021.


Simulated Memorial Day Holiday Trading Schedule
The simulated Memorial Day Holiday Trading Schedule in the CFE certification trading environment will follow
the modified trading hours of the previously announced CFE Modified Trading Hours for the Memorial Day
Holiday notice. Please use the previous notice as a guide to our modified trading hours for the simulated
Memorial Day Holiday during this testing opportunity.

BOEv3 Protocol Failover Testing
CFE will conduct BOEv3 failover testing on Wednesday, May 26, 2021 and Thursday, May 27, 2021 at 10:00
a.m. C.T. on each of the respective days. The failover testing will simulate the scenarios described in our
BOEv3 Specification where the BOEv3 Secaucus Primary port (A) is unavailable and BOEv3 protocol users are
required to connect to Secaucus Secondary port (B). BOEv3 Unit 1 failover testing will occur on Wednesday,
May 26, 2021, and BOEv3 Unit 2 failover testing will occur on Thursday, May 27, 2021. The table below
describes the CFE products associated with each unit.

          Symbol Range Start                  BOEv3 Unit Used for Testing.
          VX, VXT, VXM, VXMT                            Unit 1
           All Other Products                           Unit 2
