The following details Trading Hours as they relate to the ASX 24 markets during domestic and international Public Holidays. The policy and process regarding Trading Hours and Public Holidays is detailed in the tables immediately below.

Public Holidays ASX 24 Australian, US and ASX 24 NZ Market Status
Australian Day Session Closed
Night Session Normal Trading Hours
New Zealand Day Session Closed
Night Session Normal Trading Hours
US Day Session Normal Trading Hours
Night Session to cease trading at midnight
International Closed Day and Night Sessions

Public Holidays refer to holidays observed by the respective Debt and/ or Equity financial markets.

Over Night Options (ONO) on Australian Dollar Products before a Day Session Holiday

The 3 and 10 Year Treasury Bond ONOs will not be available for trading on the Night Session on the dates detailed below due to the unavailability of the following Day Session to calculate a settlement price with integrity.

Intra Day Options (IDO) on Australian Dollar Debt Products

The 3 and 10 Year Treasury Bond IDOs will not be available for trading on dates where the day session for ASX 24 Australian products are not available or closes early.
