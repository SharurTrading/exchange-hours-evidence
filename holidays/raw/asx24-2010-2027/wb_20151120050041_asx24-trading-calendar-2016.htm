ASX 24 Trading calendar - 2016

The following details Trading Hours as they relate to the ASX 24 markets during domestic and international Public Holidays. The policy and process with regards to Trading Hours and Public Holidays is detailed in the tables immediately below.

Public Holidays ASX 24 Australian, US and ASX 24 NZ Dollar Market Status
Australian Day Session Closed
Night Session Normal Trading Hours
New Zealand Day Session Closed
Night Session Normal Trading Hours
US Day Session Normal Trading Hours
Night Session Normal Trading Hours
International Closed Day and Night Sessions

Public Holidays refer to holidays observed by the respective Debt and/ or Equity financial markets.

Australian Holidays NZ Holidays US Holidays International Holidays

Over Night Options (ONO) on Australian Dollar Products before a Day Session Holiday

 

The Three and Ten Year Commonwealth Government Bond ONOs will not be available for trading on the Night Session on the dates detailed below due to the unavailability of the following Day Session to calculate a settlement price with integrity.

 

Calendar Date Public Holiday - Trade Date
Monday 25 January, 2016 Australia Day – Tuesday 26 January, 2016
Friday 22 April, 2016 ANZAC Day – Monday 25 April, 2016
Friday 10 June, 2016 Queen’s Birthday – Monday 13 June, 2016

 

Intra Day Options (IDO) on Australian Dollar Debt Products

 

The Three and Ten Year Commonwealth Government Bond IDOs will not be available for trading on dates where the day session for ASX 24 Australian products are not available or closes early.
