ASX Index Futures Trading hours

The table below sets out the trading hours for the ASX Index futures market.  All times are Sydney local times.

ASX Index Futures Trading Hours
5.30am to 6.00am

Pre Open (only cancel, amend and required trade report permitted)
6.00am to 9.40am

Open (normal trading)
9.40am to 9.50am Pre Open (only cancel, amend and required trade report permitted)
9:50am to 5.00pm

Open (normal trading)
5.00pm to 5.30pm Pre Night-Trading (only cancel, amend and required trade report permitted)
5.30pm to 8.00pm Night-trading (normal automated trading)
8.00pm Close
