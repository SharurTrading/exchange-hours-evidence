CFE Information Circular IC17-022
Date:    June 16, 2017
To:      Trading Privilege Holders and Vendors
From: CFE Market Services Department
RE:      Modified Trading Hours for Independence Day Holiday

This circular sets forth the modified trading hours for CBOE Volatility Index (VX) futures and all other CFE
products for the Independence Day holiday. All times referenced in this circular are Chicago time.

Modified Trading Schedule for Monday, July 3, 2017:

Trading in all CFE products will close at 12:15 p.m. on Monday, July 3, 2017.

The trading hours for all Trade at Settlement (TAS) transactions in VX futures (VXT) will end at 12:13 p.m.
on July 3, 2017.

Block Trades must be fully reported by no later than 10 minutes after the transaction is agreed upon, and
Exchange of Contract for Related Position (ECRP) transactions must be fully reported by no later than 30
minutes after the transaction is agreed upon.

Additionally, TAS Block Trades and TAS ECRP transactions must be fully reported by no later than 12:13
p.m. on July 3, 2017 and Non-TAS Block Trades and Non-TAS ECRP transactions must be reported to
CFE by no later than 12:30 p.m. on July 3, 2017.

All Block Trades and ECRP transactions reported on July 3, 2017 will be submitted for clearing as July 3,
2017 transactions.

Holiday Trading Schedule for Tuesday, July 4, 2017:

VX Futures

The schedule below will apply for the Tuesday, July 4, 2017 holiday trading session 1 in VX futures, including
TAS transactions in VX futures (Ticker: VXT):

 Type of Trading Hours                   Tuesday, July 4, 2017                       Wednesday, July 5, 2017

        Extended                   3:30 p.m. (Monday) to 10:30 a.m.              5:00 p.m. (Tuesday) to 8:30 a.m.


         Regular                                   None                                8:30 a.m. to 3:15 p.m.



1 A holiday trading session includes extended trading hours on the calendar day of the holiday and any extended trading

hours for the holiday on the previous calendar day. Holiday trading sessions are not separate Business Days and are
part of the next Business Day. Trading in VX futures is halted between sessions of extended trading hours on the
calendar day of a holiday. The CBOE System will complete the processing of trades that are in the course of being
processed by the CBOE System prior to the start of such a halt period. Trades in VX futures (including TAS transactions
in VX futures) during the Independence Day holiday trading session will be submitted for clearing on the Business Day
of Wednesday, July 5, 2017.
Trades in VX futures (including TAS transactions in VX futures) during the July 4, 2017 holiday trading
session will be submitted for clearing on the Business Day of Wednesday, July 5, 2017.

ECRP Transactions and Block Trades in VX Futures

ECRP transactions and Block Trades in VX futures may occur during the extended trading hours for VX
futures on Tuesday, July 4, 2017, but may not occur outside of those trading hours. ECRP transactions
must be reported by no later than 30 minutes after the transaction is agreed upon and Block Trades must
be reported by no later than 10 minutes after the transaction is agreed upon. Notification to CFE by the
Authorized Reporter for each party to any ECRP transaction or Block Trade that occurs during extended
trading hours on Tuesday, July 4, 2017 may be made by e-mail to the CFE Help Desk or through the CFE
Trade Reporting Tool in accordance with the regularly applicable procedures for those notification methods.

All Other Products

Trading will be closed for all CFE products other than VX futures on Tuesday, July 4, 2017. Additionally,
no ECRP transactions or Block Trades may occur in these products on Tuesday, July 4, 2017.
