April 24, 2025

DELAYED OPEN TIME FOR COCOA, COFFEE “C”® AND SUGAR NO. 11®
CONTRACTS, AND NO TRADING OF DAILY GOLD AND SILVER FUTURES
CONTRACTS ON MAY 5, 2025


Due to a London bank holiday on Monday, May 5, 2025, ICE Futures U.S. will delay the start of trading in
Sugar No. 11, Coffee “C” and Cocoa futures and options contracts until 7:30 am NY time on that day.

This change to the start of trading time does not affect pre-open, end of trading or settlement times for these
products.

Daily Gold and Silver futures contracts are closed for trading on Monday, May 5, 2025, due to the London
Bank Holiday.

All other Exchange products will follow their regular trading schedules and are not affected by these changes
to these contracts.
