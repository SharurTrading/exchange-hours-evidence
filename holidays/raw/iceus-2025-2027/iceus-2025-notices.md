# ICE Futures U.S. — 2025 holiday notices, transcribed verbatim

Every quotation below is read off the artifact named in the heading (the `.pdf`, whose
`pdftotext -layout` twin sits beside it). Quotations are given as printed; only the
column whitespace of the `-layout` extraction is normalised to ` | `. The operator prints
all times as **“all times shown in NY time”** on every notice.

The crate's `iceus` venue table is the intersection of seven families, so the **product
group named beside each line is the operative fact**: a group header that does not name a
family leaves that family's row unstated.

## 4. Target 2 — late 2026 and 2027 (publication horizon, not a retrieval failure)

- **2026-11-26 (Thanksgiving), 2026-12-24 (Christmas Eve), 2026-12-28 (Boxing Day): not yet
  published.** The 2026 calendar marks the holiday cells `open1`, with footnote 1 verbatim:
  > 1Trading Hours for these contracts will be announced in advance of the respective holiday via Exchange
  > Notices that can be found at https://www.ice.com/futures-us/notices.

  Every 2026 notice actually issued runs from `…2026_GoodFridayHoliday_20260202.pdf` to
  `…2026_Columbus_Day_Thanksgiving_Canada_8.28.26.pdf` (the round-0 listing, preserved at
  `../cfe-eurex-ice-cde-smfe-2026-2027/ifus_notices.md`); nothing later than August 28, 2026 is
  issued as of this retrieval (2026-09-26), and neither archive holds one. Note also that
  2026-11-27 and 2026-12-24 are **not** holidays on the calendar, so their early closes exist
  only in notices that do not yet exist.
- **2027 per-holiday notices: not yet published, and by construction.** The 2027 calendar
  (`ICE_Futures_US_Exchange_Notice-2027_Holiday_Calendar_20260604.pdf`, held in
  `../cfe-eurex-ice-cde-smfe-2026-2027/`) carries the identical footnote 1, and the Wayback
  prefix query returns an **empty** result set for every `ICE_Futures_US_2027…` notice URL. The
  2027 `open1` cells are unstatable until the operator issues the individual notices, which it
  does “in advance of the respective holiday”.
- **Newly retrieved, bearing on the 2026 boundary:** `IFUS_2026_NewYearHoliday_20251107.pdf`
  (§2.9) confirms 2026-01-01 `Closed` for every group and states the 2025-12-31 instants.

## 5. Target 3 — LBMA Daily Gold and Silver

The channel is the “Delayed Opens” notice (§2.8); the 2025 instances are saved. The two 2026
instances are **not retrieved**: the early-May one is discoverable as
`https://www.ice.com/publicdocs/futures_us/exchange_notices/ICE_Futures_US_ExNotDelayedOpensMay4_20260319.pdf`
(not archived, and the live host was rate-limiting at retrieval time), and the late-August one
(UK Summer Bank Holiday, Monday 2026-08-31) has neither a known URL nor an archive capture.
No crate identity routes to Daily Gold and Silver, so this is outside the venue intersection.
