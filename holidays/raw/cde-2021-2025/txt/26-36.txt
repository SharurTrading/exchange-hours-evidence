   -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
   In observance of Labor Day, the Monday, September 7, 2026 trading session will be closed for
   select Coinbase Derivatives products.

   For 24x7 enabled firms, all crypto products and Gold (GOL) and Silver (SLR) will remain open
   throughout the holiday. The weekly 1-hour maintenance window will run at its standard time on
   Friday, September 4th from 16:00 to 17:00 CT. The Tuesday, September 8th trade date will
   commence on Friday, September 4th at 17:00 CT. For firms not 24x7-enabled, orders will be
   rejected from Friday, September 4th 16:00 CT to Monday, September 7th 17:00 CT.

   For Equity Index Perp Style Futures (AIP, CHN, DEF, TEK, US5), the Tuesday, September 8th
   trade date will commence on Monday, September 7th at 19:00 CT
