                           Market Notice

To: FairX Members and Participants

From: FairX Command Center

Notice: 21-03

Date: 06/30/2021

Subject: Independence Day Observed Monday, July 5th, 2021




FairX will observe Independence Day on Monday, July 5th, 2021. Markets will
be closed from 16:00 CT Friday, July 2nd – 17:00 CT Monday, July 5th. FairX
will re-open for normal trading hours on Monday, July 5th at 17:00 CT for
trade date Tuesday, July 6th, 2021.
