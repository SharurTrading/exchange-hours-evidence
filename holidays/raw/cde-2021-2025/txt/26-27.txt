       Subject:​           Coinbase Derivatives 2026 Juneteenth Schedule
       -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
       For select Coinbase Derivatives products, the June 19, 2026 trading session will be closed in
       observance of Juneteenth.

       For 24x7 enabled firms, all crypto products will remain open throughout the holiday. The weekly
       1-hour maintenance window will run at its standard time on Friday, June 19th from 16:00 to
       17:00 CT. The Monday, June 22nd trade date will commence on Friday, June 19th at 17:00 CT.
       For firms not 24x7-enabled, crypto product orders will be rejected from Friday, June 19th 16:00
       CT to Sunday, June 21st 17:00 CT. All firms have normal crypto access throughout the Friday,
       June 19th trade date — Thursday, June 18th 16:00 CT through Friday, June 19th 16:00 CT —
       the same schedule as any other week.

       For Equity Index Perp Style Futures (24x5 — AIP, CHN, DEF, TEK), the Monday, June 22nd
       trade date will commence on Sunday, June 21st at 19:00 CT.

       Please note: NOLN26 (nano Crude Oil July 2026 outright) will expire one trading day early on Thursday,
       June 18, 2026 at 13:30 CT.
