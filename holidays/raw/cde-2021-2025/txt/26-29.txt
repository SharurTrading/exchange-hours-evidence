     Subject:​           Coinbase Derivatives 2026 Independence Day Schedule
     -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
     In observance of Independence Day (observed), the Friday, July 3, 2026 trading session will be
     closed for select Coinbase Derivatives products.

     For 24x7 enabled firms, all crypto products and Gold (GOL) and Silver (SLR) will remain open
     throughout the holiday. Due to the Friday holiday, the weekly 1-hour maintenance window will be
     adjusted to Thursday, July 2nd from 16:00 to 17:00 CT. The Monday, July 6th trade date will
     commence on Thursday, July 2nd at 17:00 CT. For firms not 24x7-enabled, orders will be
     rejected from Thursday, July 2nd 16:00 CT to Sunday, July 5th 17:00 CT.

     For Equity Index Perp Style Futures (AIP, CHN, DEF, TEK), the Monday, July 6th trade date will
     commence on Sunday, July 5th at 19:00 CT.
