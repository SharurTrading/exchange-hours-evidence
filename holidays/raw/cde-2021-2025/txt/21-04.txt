                          Market Notice

To: FairX Members and Participants

From: FairX Command Center

Notice: 21-04

Date: 08/31/2021

Subject: Labor Day Observed Monday, September 6th, 2021




FairX will observe Labor Day on Monday, September 6th, 2021. Markets will
be closed from 16:00 CT Friday, September 2nd – 17:00 CT Monday,
September 6th. FairX will re-open for normal trading hours on Monday,
September 6th at 17:00 CT for trade date Tuesday, September 7th, 2021.
