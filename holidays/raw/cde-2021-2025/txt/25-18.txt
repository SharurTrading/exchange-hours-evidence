Subject:​           Coinbase Derivatives 2025 Memorial Day Schedule
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
For select Coinbase Derivatives products, the May 26, 2025 trading session will be closed in
observance of Memorial Day.

For enabled firms, 24x7 Products will remain open through holiday. Tuesday’s trade date will
begin Friday at 17:00 CT. As of May 13, 2025, 24x7 Products include Bitcoin Futures (BTI),
nano Bitcoin Futures (BIT), Ether Futures (ETI), and nano Ether Futures (ET).

Please see below for detailed hours of operations and settlement information around the holiday
weekend schedule. Additional detail may be found on CDE’s Cloud Docs Market Hours page.
