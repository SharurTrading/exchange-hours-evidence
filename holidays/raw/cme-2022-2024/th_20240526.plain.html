Globex Trading Hours and Holiday Schedules

Please note that with the 2024 calendar year the CME Group will be transitioning from downloadable holiday calendars for Globex and incorporating the holiday hours into our trading hours page. To view the trading hours for a specific holiday please select the appropriate date in the “DATE” field and the data will automatically update in the table below the date field. You can then filter for the various products that you need to view. To view the trading hours for the next upcoming holiday you can click on the “View trading hours for (holiday name)” link that appears to the right of the date field.

PREOPEN

Order Entry, modification, and cancel are allowed. No order matching.

OPEN

Start of continuous trading phase. Order matching begins.

Paused

Interruption of continuous trading. Only order cancellation is allowed. No order matching.

pcp (POST CLOSE - PREOPEN)

Allows GTC/GTD orders only placement, modification, and cancellation. No order matching.

closed

Final Close of the date. Day and GTD (current trade date) orders are eliminated.
