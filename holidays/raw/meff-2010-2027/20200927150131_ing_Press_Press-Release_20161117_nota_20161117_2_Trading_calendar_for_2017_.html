Trading calendar for 2017

17 November, 2016

BME, which comprises, among other entities, the four Spanish Stock Exchanges, the Spanish Futures and Options Market (MEFF) and the Corporate Debt Market (AIAF), informs that in 2017 the Spanish markets will be closed the following days:

 

  • Friday, April 14th
  • Monday, April 17th
  • Monday, May 1st     
  • Monday, December 25th
  • Tuesday, December 26th
