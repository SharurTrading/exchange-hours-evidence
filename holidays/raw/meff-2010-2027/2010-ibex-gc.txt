    1.    Introduction

          These General Conditions modify the prior version with the aim of (i) adjusting
          the calculation of the Settlement Price at Expiration in special circumstances
          when one or more stocks are not tradable during the period of calculation,
          referring to a Circular for the detailed calculation in those cases (ii) substituting
          the Annex of the Technical regulations for the composition and calculation of
          the IBEX 35, for the section number 2 where a link to Sociedad de Bolsas web
          is provided (iii) modifying the item Futures Price Quotation in section 3 to
          mention that the minimun price fluctuation will be set according to the quotation
          of the underlying asset or the Market practice, and it will be set by means of a
          Circular.

          These General Conditions describe the characteristics of the IBEX 35 Futures
          Contract as required by the Regulations of the Market for Equity Related
          Financial Derivatives in their article 3.1.

          The IBEX 35 Futures Contract has as its Underlying Security the IBEX 35 Index
          multiplied by 10 EURO.

          As a consequence of the trading of the abovementioned instrument, the trading
          parties (MEFF RV, the Members and the Clients) acquire the rights and
          obligations set out in articles 5, 6, 7 and 8 of the Market Regulations.

          These General Conditions form part of the regulations of the Market and
          develop those provisions where the Regulations refer to the General Conditions
          of each Contract. The terms used have the same sense as defined in article 2.1
          of the Regulations.

          The Members with access to trading and settlement shall be all those which are
          Market Members, with the rights and obligations as determined by the class of
          Member to which they belong.

          The limits of the Members' and Clients' open positions, if any, shall be
          determined by Circular under the terms set out by the Regulations.


    2.    Underlying asset
           The IBEX 35 Futures Contract has as Underlying Asset the IBEX 35 Index,
           managed and published by Sociedad de Bolsas.

           Rules regarding the calculation and composition of the IBEX 35 are described
           in the document Technical rules for the composition and calculation of the
           IBEX 35, which is available at http:/www.sbolsas.com

    3.    Technical specifications

      Underlying asset:     The IBEX 35 Index.

      Multiplier:           10 EURO

      Contract Size:        The IBEX 35 Index times the Multiplier.

      Expirations:          Every month. The three nearest expirations, at least,
                            shall be traded at all times.

      Expiration Date:      The third Friday of the expiration month.

      Contract Settlement
      Date:                 First Business Day following the Expiration Date.

      Last trading day:     The Expiration Date.

      Futures Price
      Quotation:            In whole Index points with a minimum fluctuation set
                            according to the quotation of the underlying asset and/or
                            the Market practice, which will be established by Circular.
                            The minumun fluctuation shall be different in pre-arranged
                            trades between Members.

      Maximum Price
      Fluctuation:          None.

      Daily Settlement of
      Gains and Losses:     Before the start of the session of the Business Day
                            following the date of the transaction, by means of
                            cash transfer of the difference with the Daily
                            Settlement Price.

      Settlement of
      Commissions:          First Business Day following the date of the
                            Transaction.

      Margins:              Variable in function to the portfolio of Options and
                            Futures (see Margin Calculation section). Margins
                            shall be supplied before the start of the session of the
                            Business Day following the date of the calculation.


      Daily Settlement
      Price:                Arithmetic Average between the best buy and sale
                            price for each expiration at the close of the Market
                            each day.

    4.    Timetable
           The trading timetable shall be determined by MEFF RV through the
           corresponding Circular.

           As established in the Market Regulations, the trading timetable shall always be
           in the period between 8:00 and 22:00.

    5.    Means of Contract Settlement
           Settlement at Expiration of Contracts shall be made by cash transfer of the
           difference with the Settlement Price at Expiration.

    6.    Settlement Price at Expiration
           The Settlement Price at the expiration of the Contracts shall be the arithmetic
           average of the IBEX 35 Index between 16:15 and 16:45 on the Expiration
           Date, with one value being taken per minute. The Settlement Price at
           Expiration shall be rounded to one decimal position.

           For the calculation of the average, the value per minute shall be taken as the
           new value published immediately following the start of each minute according
           to the clock of the Sociedad de Bolsas.

           If during any minute between 16:15 and 16:45 no new Index value is
           published, the last published value prior to the start of that minute shall be
           taken as the value for the calculation.

           The abovementioned arithmetic average shall be arrived at using the following
           formula:

               i = Value of the Index each minute.

               Adjustment to the general rule

               MEFF will set, by means of a Circular, the necessary adaptations to the
               general rule for the calculation of the Settlement price at Expiration when,
               during the period of calculation, one or several stocks are suspended or enter
               into a volatility auction, or SIBE technical failure occurs.

    7.    Margin Calculation
           Calculation shall be carried out on the basis of portfolio analysis, taking into
           account all Contracts (options and futures) in a portfolio and arriving at the
           positive or negative value of the portfolio under the simulated worst-case
           scenario.

           The valuation model to be used shall be specified by Circular, as shall be the
           model adjustments, the simulated scenarios and the variables necessary for
           the full calculation.

    8.    Daily Settlement of Gains and Losses
           Each day, prior to the calculation of Margins in combination with a portfolio's
           Options position, the calculation of the Daily Settlement of Gains and Losses
           corresponding to the Futures position for each expiration shall be carried out.

           The Settlement shall correspond to the net sum of the following operations on
           each account:

           a)   Futures bought: if the Price of the Future is lower than the Daily
                Settlement Price, the sum corresponding to the difference shall be
                credited; if the Price of the Future is higher than the Daily Settlement
                Price, the sum corresponding to the difference shall be charged.

           b)   Futures sold: if the Price of the Future is lower than the Daily Settlement
                Price, the sum corresponding to the difference shall be charged; if the
                Price of the Future is higher than the Daily Settlement Price the sum
                corresponding to the difference shall be credited.

           c)   Futures bought and sold on the day of the calculation: the same
                operations as in a) and b) shall be carried out with the applicable Price of
                the Future being the one agreed in each Transaction.
