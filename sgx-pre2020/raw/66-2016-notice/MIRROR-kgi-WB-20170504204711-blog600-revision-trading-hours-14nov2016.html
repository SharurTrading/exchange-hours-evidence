Revision of the trading hours for SGX derivatives contracts with effect from 14 November 2016

Kindly note that with the launch of SGX's Titan DT/DC1, the trading hours for SGX derivatives contracts’ will be revised with effect from 14 November 2016.


Click here for the list of contracts and their revised trading hours. 

SGX-DT contracts available for trading during onshore public holidays

With reference to SGX Circular no. DT/AM – 76 of 2016 dated 24 October 2016, selected SGX-DT contracts will be open for trading in the T and T+1 sessions on all onshore public holidays except for New Years’ Day holiday in lieu on 2 January 2017 (unless otherwise specified). Please note that this policy will only apply to SGX MSCI Taiwan Index Futures and Options with effect from 3 January 20172.
 

On 2 January 2017, all derivatives contracts will be closed for trading, except for the India suite of derivatives contracts which are SGX MSCI India Index Futures, SGX Nifty 50 Index Futures, SGX Nifty 50 Index Options, SGX Nifty Bank Index Futures, SGX Nifty CPSE Index Futures, SGX Nifty IT Index Futures and SGX Nifty Midcap 50 Index futures.
 

Trading Schedule for other contracts during onshore public holidays

Click here for the trading schedule of the other contracts during onshore public holidays.
 

Please note that SGX trading schedules outlined in this circular are subject to change in the event of further announced holidays, acts of government or other unforeseen circumstances leading to closure of the underlying markets. For more information, you may refer to SGX website at www.sgx.com.


1 With reference to SGX Circular no. DT/AM – 65 of 2016
2 As of 15 November 2016, there are no Taiwan public holidays from 15 November 2016 to 3 January 2017.
