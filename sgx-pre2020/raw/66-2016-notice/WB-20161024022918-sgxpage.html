Market Summary

21 Oct 2016

The Straits Times Index (STI) ended 11.56 points or 0.41% lower to 2831.06, taking the year-to-date performance to -1.79%. The top active stocks today were QAF, which gained 0.81%, Singtel, which declined 0.51%, DBS, which declined 0.46%, OCBC Bank, which declined 0.59% and UOB, with a 0.11% advance. The FTSE ST Mid Cap Index declined 0.19%, while the FTSE ST Small Cap Index declined 0.38%.
