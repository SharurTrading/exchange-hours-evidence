Temporary Change To Opening Times For 3 ICE Contracts From Monday, 31 October Until Friday, 4 November 2016

Wednesday, 26 October 2016

Due to the end Of Daylight Saving Time (DST) in Europe, the opening times for these ICE Futures and Options contracts will be temporarily changed, from Monday, 31 October to Friday, 4 November 2016:

New Opening Times:

Name of Contract

New Start Time

Sugar No. 11
  • 4:30 am NY time (8:30 am GMT)

Coffee “C”
  • 5:15 am NY time (9:15 am GMT)

Cocoa
  • 5:45 am NY time (9:45 am GMT)


The temporary opening times will be applicable till Friday, 4 November 2016.

In addition, for this same period, the daily settlement window for Cocoa futures and options contracts will be from 12:48 to 12:50 pm. At the end of this week, the window will revert to the normal 11:48 to 11:50 am time. Due to this temporary change to the settlement window, during this week Trade at Settlement (or “TAS”) trading for Cocoa will end at 12:50 pm each day instead of the normal 11:50 am time.

For more details, you may read ICE's notice here.
