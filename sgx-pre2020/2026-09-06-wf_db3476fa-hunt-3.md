## what_it_settles

It settles LESS than the ONE QUESTION asks. This is an SGX-authored, SGX-hosted, publicly reachable primary document (investorrelations.sgx.com; no authentication — note that a bare curl User-Agent gets HTTP 403, a full browser header set gets HTTP 200 application/pdf, 5,988,827 bytes). It states, in SGX's own voice, that (a) a derivatives trading-hours extension happened in NOVEMBER 2019, (b) its purpose was "to cover the New York cash market closing hours", and (c) the resulting envelope is "22.5 hours" — which is exactly the 06:45→05:15 span consistent with a 05:15 T+1 close. It therefore independently corroborates the existence, direction, month and rationale of the change that ends with the T+1 close at 05:15.

It does NOT state an effective DAY. Under LAW-NO-FABRICATED-DATES a month is not a cutover; "November 2019" cannot be narrowed to 2019-11-11 without an inference, and inference is exactly what the law forbids. So this document may be cited as corroboration and as a bound (the cutover lies inside November 2019), but it CANNOT key a revision row. The ONE QUESTION remains open on my angle.

Nothing at all was found on the second question (the Japan T-session extension left undated by PR #44). SGX Annual Report 2025 (FY July 2024 – June 2025), which covers November 2024, contains no mention of trading hours, a T session, or the Nikkei/FTSE Blossom hours change — its only "hours" hits are staff training and volunteering hours.
