The ultimate forex training platform

To become a successful forex trader, you need to train under real market conditions:

  • Experience real-time rates and real-world volatility.
  • Use a full-featured trading interface with the same benefits as the fxTrade trading account used by professional traders.
  • Trade more than 60 major and exotic currency pairs, as well as silver and gold crosses. Experiment with emerging market alternatives, such as a Chinese yuan cross (USD/CNY).
  • Test any trading strategy using completely flexible trade sizes.

OANDA's currency trading technology provides everyone with equal access to the same low OANDA Spreads™ and professional trading features. The demo account is a key tool our customers use to learn forex—risk-free.

Practical learning, flexible environment

  • Your practice account never expires. You get unlimited free access to the account, for as many or as few times as you want.
  • Reset your 100,000-unit starting balance or your profit/loss at any time, and change your leverage to test your comfort level.
  • Trade anywhere from any computer or from forex mobile apps.
  • We never hassle you to open a trading account with pressure, spam, or phone calls.
