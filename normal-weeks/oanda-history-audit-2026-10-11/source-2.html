Avoiding margin closeouts

Ensure that you maintain sufficient margin in your OANDA Trade account at all times to avoid a margin closeout. To reduce the risk of a margin closeout, you could reduce or close your overall positions or add more funds to your OANDA Trade account. To prevent unwanted order execution, consider widening your take-profits, stop-losses or trailing-stops prior to the market close.

Existing and new positions

You cannot close existing positions or open new positions when the markets are closed. You can, however, place or modify entry, stop-loss, take-profit and trailing stop orders.

Market prices

Market prices may significantly change or “gap” when the market resumes trading. This change could trigger orders, which would be executed at the prevailing market rate. For example, if a price moves against you, a stop-loss order may be triggered at a different price to the price specified on your order. This could result in additional losses.

Note: All forex instruments will be closed from 16:59-17:05 (New York time) each day the market is open.

Frequently asked questions

Smart answers to common questions

What are the standard operating hours for the forex market in the US?

The US forex market hours are 24 hours, Sunday to Friday according to New York time. OANDA’s forex market hours are

between 17:05, Sunday, and 16:59, Friday, according to New York time.

Please note: these times are subject to change during daylight saving time and certain public holidays.

What are the standard operating hours for the crypto market in the US?

Crypto market hours are 24 hours a day, 365 days a year.

Please note: Cryptocurrency trading hours (on OANDA offered through Paxos) may be impacted by regular scheduled maintenance. Users will be notified via email when maintenance will occur.

Can I place orders outside of regular trading hours?

When trading with OANDA, you can place or modify orders, including entry, stop-loss, take-profit, and trailing stop orders. However, you can’t open new positions or close existing ones.
