# Carried-normal-week no-changes sweeps — 2026-10-05 (UTC)

The maintainer's 2026-10-05 directive asked, for every family refusing dates as
`NormalWeekCarried`, whether the carry is a real gap or an artifact of the
horizon never having moved after its verification landed. Each family's own
change channels were swept over its carried interval; the per-family outcomes
are recorded in the owners' evidence files in the repository, and the artifacts
live beside this note. Every retrieval is 2026-10-05 (UTC); digests are in each
directory's `SHA256SUMS.txt`.
