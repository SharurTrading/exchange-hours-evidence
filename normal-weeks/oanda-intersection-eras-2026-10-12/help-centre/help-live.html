Hours of operation in OANDA Europe Limited

When can I trade?

Our hours of operation coincide with the global financial markets. The trading hours are subject to change during daylight saving time and certain public holidays.

Can I open or close positions when the markets are closed?

You cannot close existing positions or open new positions when the markets are closed. You can, however, place or modify entry, stop-loss, take-profit and trailing stop orders.

What should I keep in mind during market closing hours ?

Spreads (the difference between the bid price and ask price) typically widen daily for non-FX CFDs and before the markets close for all other asset classes, and could trigger orders and/or margin closeouts.

Ensure that you maintain sufficient margin in your OANDATradeThe actual execution of buying or selling an asset, resulting in a completed transaction. account at all times to avoid a margin closeout. To reduce the risk of a margin closeout, you could consider various options, such as to reduce or close your overall positions or add more funds to your OANDA Trade account. To reduce the likelihood of unwanted order execution, you could also consider options such as widening your take-profits, stop-losses or trailing-stops prior to the market close.

What should I keep in mind when the market resumes trading?

Market prices may significantly change or “gap” when the market resumes trading. This change could trigger orders, which would be executed at the prevailing market rate. For example, if a price moves against you, a stop-loss order may be triggered at a different price to the price specified on your order. This could result in additional losses.

Why can’t I trade some instruments during the hours of operation?

We do not guarantee to quote all instruments during our hours of operation. There are numerous market reasons which may cause an instrument to either temporarily or permanently be withdrawn, hence preventing the opening of new positions.
