    • Postponed: Market Depth Change to 10-deep for NYMEX and COMEX Futures

      The production launch and mock trading session for the NYMEX and COMEX futures change to 10-deep market depth has been postponed. The move to 10-deep was previously scheduled for this weekend.

      The mock trading session will be held Saturday, April 18.

      The production launch date will be announced in the CME Globex Notices.
