NYSE Liffe

NYSE Liffe’s Derivatives markets will be open Monday to Friday throughout 2012 except on the following days:

  2012
Good Friday Friday 6 April
Easter Monday Monday 9 April
Labour Day1 Tuesday 1 May
Christmas Day Tuesday 25 December
Boxing Day Wednesday 26 December

2012
On Monday 24 December 2012 and Monday 31 December 2012 trading will be aligned with those of the underlying and, where appropriate, related markets. On these days the Exchange will commence the closing of its derivatives contracts from midday GMT (13.00 hours CET).

On 2 January, 7 May, 4 June, 5 June and 27 August 2012, which are public holidays in the United Kingdom, it is anticipated that NYSE Liffe’s sterling-based products and London-based Commodity contracts will not be made available for trading, but other products will be available.
