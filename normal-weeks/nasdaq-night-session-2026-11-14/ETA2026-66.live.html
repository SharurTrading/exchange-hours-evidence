Friday, November 6, 2026

Equity Trader Alert #2026 - 66
Nasdaq Stock Market: Global Trading Hours Pre-Production Period Now Available

Category:

  • New Functionality

Markets Impacted:

  • The Nasdaq Stock Market

Contact Information:

  • U.S. Market Operations at +1 212 231 5180
  • Nasdaq Trading Services at +1 212 231 5100
  • U.S. Market Sales
    +1 800 846 0477

Resources:

    • Nasdaq's Global Trading Hours Hub

Nasdaq reminds members and vendors that the Global Trading Hours pre-production period is now underway. Participants are encouraged to begin certification and testing at their earliest convenience.

Pre-Production Period — Ongoing as of November 1, 2026

  • Requires new production Nasdaq order entry and market data ports
  • Test symbols are available for order entry and trading
  • Trading is available Sunday through Thursday, from 9:00 p.m. ET until 3:55 a.m. ET* the following day
  • Market data will be broadcast for test symbols throughout the pre-production period

Daily System Availability Schedule (all times ET)

  • 8:35 p.m. ET - 9:00 p.m. ET (Variable) — Ports become available for connection; market data multicast feeds begin broadcasting system status & reference data messages
  • 9:00 p.m. ET — Trading opens for test symbols
  • 3:55 a.m. ET* — Trading ends and all open orders are cancelled

Additional Resources

For additional information, please visit Nasdaq's Global Trading Hours Hub.

 

* Time may be subject to revision and if so, will be indicated in a future ETA.

Please follow Nasdaq on Facebook RSS and Twitter.

Nasdaq (Nasdaq: NDAQ) is a leading global provider of trading, clearing, exchange technology, listing, information and public company services. Through its diverse portfolio of solutions, Nasdaq enables customers to plan, optimize and execute their business vision with confidence, using proven technologies that provide transparency and insight for navigating today's global capital markets. As the creator of the world's first electronic stock market, its technology powers more than 90 marketplaces in 50 countries, and 1 in 10 of the world's securities transactions. Nasdaq is home to approximately 3,900 total listings with a market value of approximately $13 trillion. To learn more, visit: business.nasdaq.com.
