## Margin and history counterexamples

- Dec14 2021 and June5 2023 captured UK hours: four indices NewYork18:00–17:00, four Treasuries Chicago17:00–16:00 (same17:00ETclose). Feb21 2024: all eight Chicago17:01–15:59 (=18:01–16:59ET). The old/new close endpoints differ. Their sourced intersection can certify an executable interior, but cannot certify which endpoint ended these six2024sessions. The captures do not state an unconditional effective day.
- OANDA2024MLK/Presidents and2025equivalent holiday statements close13:00ET and reopen18:00ET, matching CME12CT/17CT exactly. OANDA2026MLK/Presidents closes12:59ET and reopens18:01ET. Thus minus1close/plus1open is a dated observation, not a timeless broker law.
- OANDA2024indexChristmasEve13:14 differs from CME13:15ET. OANDA2026Memorial12:59 likewise differs from CME13:00ET. Both defeat an exact universal underlying==broker claim.
- Queue/preopen events are not executable opens: CME PDF legend explicitly says no order matching in PREOPEN/HALT. Use the17:00open event, not16:00preopen; Treasury family is not cash-equity trading.
