OANDA holiday hours and maintenance times

Scheduled maintenance on FX instrument pricing

Over the next few weeks, we will be upgrading all our FX instrument pricing on a phased basis.

To accomplish this there is a possibility of prices being unavailable for a maximum of 30 minutes at the end of certain trading days (we hope to minimise disruption to 3 trading days, however, 5 occasions would be the most).

We are doing this to improve our FX product. These few maintenance windows will help us provide a better FX product service to our clients in the future. We anticipate that the downtime for the specific instruments will be approximately 5 minutes, only the whole 30 minutes in the worst case scenario. This will take place over the course of the next 3 weeks or so.

We will continually update our website with details of the impacted instruments and the relevant dates, to ensure that we provide adequate notice. And, we will notify you as soon as this upgrade has been completed. If you have any questions, contact our Client Experience Team whenever markets are open.
