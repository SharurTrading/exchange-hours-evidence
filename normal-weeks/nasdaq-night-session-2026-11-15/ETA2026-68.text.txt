Trading Specifications
To prepare for the launch of nearly continuous 23-hour, 5-day trading on the FINRA/Nasdaq TRFs beginning December 6, 2026, Nasdaq is offering the following testing opportunities.
Nasdaq Test Facility (NTF)
Beginning September 28, 2026
Trade Reporting and Clearing is available on FINRA/Nasdaq TRF Carteret and FINRA/Nasdaq TRF Chicago from Sunday to Friday from 9:00 p.m. to 8:00 p.m. via TRF FIX or WorkX.
Starting on November 16, 2026, Post-Trade Risk will be available for testing from Sunday to Friday from 9:00 p.m. to 8:00 p.m.
Firms may use existing NTF TRF FIX Ports and WorkX logins. To request
NTF TRF FIX ports
or
WorkX logins
, pl
ease submit a request form.
Firms may test with production and test symbols
Weekend UAT Production Dates
Remaining Industry Test Dates: November 20, 2026 and December 4, 2026
Firms may use their existing Production TRF FIX Ports and/or WorkX services to test Trade Reporting, Clearing, and Post-Trade Risk services
Firms may test with production and test symbols
Testing is available from 11:00 p.m. Friday until 12:00 p.m. (noon) Saturday.
To receive dedicated support from our TRF OPS team, please register for weekend testing by contacting Nasdaq Trading Services by 12:00 p.m. (ET), on the Friday
one week prior
to the scheduled weekend test in which the firm would like to participate.
For example, please register by Friday, November 13th at 12:00 PM for testing on November 20th.
NTF Fee
Waiver
NTF metered testing 30-day fee waivers are available to customers for 23/5 TRF testing pursuant to Nasdaq rules (see Equity Rule 7, Section 130(d)(3) for eligibility). Customers may test with TRF FIX free of charge for a 30 consecutive day period in the NTF by registering their firm MPID and start date with
NTFbilling@nasdaq.com
in advance of testing. Please indicate the testing purpose (23/5 TRF testing) when submitting the fee waiver request. Customers who register for the testing fee waiver will be charged for such testing at the expiration of the 30 consecutive day fee waiver period. Customers who do not register for the TRF FIX testing fee waiver will be charged for all testing. Customers can test WorkX free of charge in the NTF.
