Holiday Trading Calendar
U.S. Equities and Options
Nasdaq U.S. Equities and Options markets will be closed on
Thursday, November 26, 2026
, and will
close early on Friday, November 27
, in observance of the Thanksgiving holiday.
U.S. Data Feed Products
The Nasdaq Global Index Data Service (GIDS) data feed product will be up and available at the Nasdaq U.S. data centers on
Thursday, November 26.
In conjunction with the early U.S. market close on
Friday, November 27,
Nasdaq will adjust the closing transmission schedules for its U.S. proprietary equity and derivative data feed products. For the modified closing times for Nasdaq proprietary feeds on November 27, please refer to the
Proprietary Data Feed Transmission Schedule
.
While most of its U.S. products will close early on November 27, Nasdaq will observe a normal schedule for the Nasdaq Fund Network (NFN) Data Service.
