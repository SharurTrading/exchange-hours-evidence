Nasdaq global trading hours
Frequently Asked Questions


What are global trading hours?
To meet rising demand for U.S. equities across the globe, Nasdaq is engaging with regulators to offer nearly continuous trading 23
hours a day, 5 days a week, on the Nasdaq Stock Market. This expanded global trading hours schedule will include a new trading
session from 9 p.m. to 4 a.m., together with Nasdaq’s current extended trading hours from 4 a.m. to 9:30 a.m. and 4 p.m. to 8 p.m.,
enabling issuers to more efficiently attract capital from across the globe while enabling investors to broaden access and expand
wealth-building opportunities.

The U.S. trading hours, from 9:30 a.m. - 4:00 p.m. and marked by the Opening and Closing Crosses, will continue to set the prices
to drive investor transparency across all hours of market operation.

All times throughout this document are in Eastern Time (ET).


What changes can I expect as an investor?
Subject to SEC approval, Nasdaq will introduce a new trading session on the Nasdaq Stock Market from 9 p.m. to 4 a.m. ET.

Trading will commence at 9 p.m. on Sunday evening and conclude at 8 p.m. on Friday evening with a 1-hour industry-wide trading
pause from 8 p.m. to 9 p.m. daily to allow for processing and transition to the next trade date.


When will the new session be available on Nasdaq?
The industry’s transition to a 23-hour trading day is currently expected to occur on Sunday, December 6, 2026, pending SIP
readiness as well as any applicable SEC rule changes.


How will the existing Nasdaq Stock Market trading hours be impacted?
Currently the Nasdaq stock market operates from 4:00 a.m. to 8:00 p.m.. There will be no substantive change to current
functionality or operations during this time frame.


Will other Nasdaq exchanges support the adjusted operating schedule?
No, Nasdaq Texas (NTX) and PSX equity exchanges will continue to operate on their current schedules; NTX: 7 a.m. to 7 p.m. and
PSX: 8 a.m. to 5 p.m.. Nasdaq options exchanges will also continue to operate on their current schedules.

What about correspondent clearing arrangements?
Subject to SEC approval, if a member who seeks to trade in the new overnight session clears and settles trades through another
member that is a clearing member, the designated clearing member must complete and provide Nasdaq with a Clearing Letter of
Guarantee before the designating member’s MPID may be associated with an order-entry port enabled for trading between 9 p.m.
and 4 a.m.


Will Nasdaq be available for trading on US exchange holidays?
Yes, if a holiday falls between Monday and Thursday, trading will commence at 9 p.m. ET on the holiday evening. For example, if
Monday is a US exchange holiday, trading will begin at 9 p.m. on Monday and continue until 8 p.m. on Tuesday.

Order Handling from 9 p.m. to 4 a.m.
How will Members participate?
In order to participate, members will need to order new ports for order entry between 9 p.m. to 4 a.m.. There will be no changes to
current operations between 4:00 a.m. and 8:00 p.m.. The following protocols will be available between 9 p.m. and 4 a.m.; OUCH 5,
Core FIX, and FIX. Existing order entry and market data specifications are not changing to accommodate the extension of trading
hours.


What happens to open orders at 4 a.m.?
All orders entered between 9 p.m. and 4 a.m. that are outstanding in the system at 4 a.m. will be cancelled at that time. Orders can
be re-entered at 4 a.m. in the subsequent session.


Will Nasdaq accept routable orders during the new trading hours?

What TIFs will be accepted?
All TIFs will be accepted with the exception of After Hours, On Open and On Close. All orders entered between 9 p.m. and 4 a.m.
will be cancelled at the conclusion of the session and may be re-entered at 4 a.m.

Market Protections from 9 p.m.to 4 a.m.
Will Equities Risk Checks (EQRC) be available?
Yes, Nasdaq will offer Equities Risk Checks through both the API and WorkX. Clients can establish separate rules for the period
between 9:00 p.m. and 4:00 a.m. Gross exposure calculations will not carryover and be reset when orders are canceled at 4:00
a.m.


What market protections will be in place?
Subject to SEC approval, Nasdaq will reject orders that exceed static price bands. Listing markets will calculate the static price
bands that will be disseminated by the SIP.


What reference price will be used for the bands?
Subject to SEC approval, the proposed bands will be based on the listing market’s adjusted Official Closing Price (“OCP”) and the
adjusted last Round Lot sale prior to 7:30 p.m.


How will bands be calculated?
Subject to SEC approval, the proposed upper band value will be 20% higher than the greater of the reference prices while the lower
band value will be 20% lower than the lesser of the reference prices. Band values will be a minimum of $3.00 from the reference
price or $1.00 for stocks with an OCP <$1.00. A leverage factor will be applied to leverage ETPs.


How will corporate actions and dividends be handled?
Pending SEC approval, certain corporate actions and dividends will be effective when trading commences at 9 p.m.. Certain
corporate actions and distributions as described in the rule will be halted before 9 p.m. on the day immediately preceding the
effective date and resumed at 8 a.m. on the effective date. Nasdaq will send a Trade Halt Code of ‘M1’ to denote Corporate Action.

Corporate Actions and Distributions that would remain halted until 8 a.m.:

թ      Symbol and/or CUSIP Change
թ      Dividends equal to or greater than 25% of the prior Nasdaq Official Closing Price
թ      Forward and Reverse Splits
թ      De-SPACs
թ      Spin-offs
թ      Security type change (e.g. preferred to common)
թ      Merger/Mandatory exchange
թ      Any other corporate action/event where the Exchange determines a halt is necessary for fair/orderly markets, investor
       protection, or the public interest

Market Data
How will market data be affected?
Trading activity between 9 p.m. and 4 a.m. will be disseminated on separate ITCH market data feed. Additionally, Nasdaq is
introducing new “Plus” versions of its flagship market data products, designed to deliver broader coverage today and scale
naturally over time as trading hours expand. These products will incorporate data from additional Nasdaq owned exchanges,
specifically Nasdaq PSX and Nasdaq Texas, and are designed to support the future 23 hour trading environment. All products are
pending SEC review.


What about reference data messages?
Since the 9 p.m. to 4 a.m. session is independent from the 4 a.m. to 8 p.m. session, reference data, including stock locate code
assignments, can change between sessions.

Nasdaq Basic Plus delivers best bid and offer visibility across all trading hours, allowing clients to maintain a consistent BBO view
