SYCOM Trading calendar - 2010

  • SFE Trading calendar
    • 2010
    • 2009
    • 2008

The following information details the Trading Hours as they relate to the SYCOM markets during domestic and international Public Holidays. The policy and process regarding Trading Hours and Public Holidays is detailed in the tables immediately below.

Public Holidays SYCOM Australian, US and NZ Dollar Market Status
Australian Day Session Closed
Night Session Normal Trading Hours
New Zealand Day Session Closed
Night Session Normal Trading Hours
US Day Session Normal Trading Hours
Night Session to cease trading at midnight
International Closed Day and Night Sessions

Public Holidays refer to holidays observed by the respective Debt and/ or Equity financial markets.

Overnight Options (ONO) on Australian Dollar Products before a Day Session Holiday

The Three and Ten Year Commonwealth Government Bond ONOs will not be available for trading on the Night Session on the dates detailed below due to the unavailability of the following Day Session to calculate a settlement price with integrity.

Calendar Date Public Holiday - Trade Date

Intra Day Options (IDO) on Australian Dollar Debt Products

The Three and Ten Year Commonwealth Government Bond IDOs will not be available for trading on dates where the Day Session for SFE Australian products is not available or closes early.
