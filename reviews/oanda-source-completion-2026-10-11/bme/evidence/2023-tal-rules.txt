              Sociedad de Bolsas approved Circular 1/2021 on March 17, outlining the Operating Rules of
              the Stock Exchange Interconnection System, consolidating the regulatory rules for trading
              in the Stock Exchange Interconnection System into a single regulatory text. This
              consolidation was done in alignment with the standards of Directive 2014/65/EU for greater
              clarity.
              With the aim of enhancing the efficiency of trading in the Stock Exchange Interconnection
              System, Sociedad de Bolsas has decided to introduce two new functionalities related to
              trading at the closing auction: At-the-Close condition (AtC) orders and the "Trading at Last"
              (TAL) trading phase.

              Thus, through this Circular, the applicable rules for these new functionalities are
              incorporated into the Operating Rules of the Stock Exchange Interconnection System, which
              remain fully in force.

              In pursuit of these objectives, the Board of Directors of Sociedad de Bolsas, in its session on
              September 20, 2023, within its scope of authority, approved this modification Circular to
              Circular 1/2021 on the operating rules of the Stock Exchange Interconnection System.


              First. Modification of subsection "3.2.1 General Trading Hours" of Circular 1/2021,
              dated March 17, on Operating Rules of the Stock Exchange Interconnection System.
              A fifth section is included in subsection "3.2.1 General Trading Hours" of Circular 1/2021, as
              follows:

                        "3.2.1.5 Trading at Last
                                Trading period that begins after the closing auction of the session, lasting for 10
                        minutes until 17:45, for executing operations at the closing price published at the end of
                        the trading session.
                               For those securities that enter in closing auction extension, the duration of the
                        Trading at Last (TAL) trading phase will have a maximum duration of 8 minutes."


              Second. Modification of subsection "6.2.3 Order Conditions" of Circular 1/2021, dated
              March 17, on Operating Rules of the Stock Exchange Interconnection System.

              Two additional paragraphs are included at the end of subsection "6.2.3 Order Conditions" of
              Circular 1/2021, as follows:
                            "Market orders, limit orders, and market to limit orders can be entered into the System
                      with the At-the-Close (AtC) condition. Orders with this condition will not be displayed in the
                      order book until the start of the closing auction.
                            For these types of orders, a new priority will be assigned when they become visible in
                      the order book. In the closing auction, the priority will be based on visibility, price, and time.
                            Orders entered into the system with the At-the-Close (AtC) condition that are not
                      executed, either totally or partially, in the closing auction will automatically participate in
                      the Trading at Last (TAL) phase, except for "valid for a day" orders, for which the market
                      member can choose whether they move to this trading phase or not."

              Fourth. Inclusion of a new subsection within the "6. GENERAL TRADING" section of
              Circular 1/2021, dated March 17, on Operating Rules of the Stock Exchange
              Interconnection System.

              A fourth subsection is included within the "6. GENERAL TRADING" section of Circular 1/2021,
              with the following wording:
                        "6.4 TRADING AT LAST (TAL) PHASE
                        6.4.1 Definition
                      Trading period that starts after the closing auction and is exclusively available for
              securities whose closing price matches the closing auction price, and for which at least one (1)
              share has been traded in the closing auction.
                      If the security intended for trading in the Trading at Last (TAL) phase does not meet both
              requirements, the trading session ends for that instrument, and trading in this phase is not
              possible.
                        6.4.2 Operation
                     During the Trading at Last (TAL) phase, Market members can enter, modify, or cancel
              orders after the completion of the closing auction, for securities for which this trading phase is
              open.
                      Orders will be executed based on the time priority of the orders and at the closing price of
              the traded security determined in the closing auction.
                      If a good-till-date (GTD) order cannot be executed or can only be partially executed during
              this trading phase, it will remain in the order book. If they are "valid for a day" orders, the
              unexecuted or partially executed order will be removed from the System at the close of the Trading
              at Last (TAL) phase.
                        Trades during the Trading at Last (TAL) phase do not contribute to price formation.
                     During the Trading at Last (TAL) phase, the System will make visible the aggregated volume
              that can be executed at that moment and the closing price of the session.
