                                                                    Circular no. 1/2001



           THE OPERATING RULES OF THE SPANISH STOCK EXCHANGE
                     INTERCONNECTION SYSTEM (SIBE)



       Sociedad de Bolsa’s Circular 6/2000 revised and reorganised the Operating
Rules of the Spanish Stock Exchange Interconnection System (SIBE).

        These Rules represent the next step in the harmonisation of the Spanish
electronic stock exchange trading market model to bring this into line with the
established standards of the main European stock markets.

        The main innovation lies in the management, by individual securities, of price
fluctuations. A double price fluctuation mechanism is established via the determination
of so-called static and dynamic ranges. Basically, static ranges are based on auction
prices and dynamic ranges on the last traded prices. This double mechanism is aimed
at individually managing the very diverse situations which arise during security trading.
When the aforementioned ranges are exceeded, so-called volatility auctions are held.

       These auctions will help these significant price fluctuations to occur with the
involvement of a large number of market participants. They will also make it easier for
market participants to react to the various situations, reducing operator errors.

       These innovations have been included in the full draft of the Rules with certain
sections being reorganised and new ones being introduced in order to make the Rules
clearer and easier to apply, with this Circular 1/2001 being presented as the new
revised draft of the Operating Rules.

       The innovations affect the following aspects:

       -    Section 3, referring to trading hours, now includes the various auctions and
            possible extensions to these, referring with regard to specific aspects of
            these to other sections of the Rules. In the section on general trading the
            resolution of each auction has been explained.

       -    In Section 5, references to maximum price fluctuations have been replaced
            by a definition of static and dynamic prices and ranges, and the
            circumstances which result in the holding of a volatility auction and the
            procedure for this have been included.

       -    Section 9 of the Rules, which refers to trading in the special trading segment
            known as the “Nuevo Mercado” includes the innovations arising from the
            new prices and ranges system.
       - Finally, the innovations caused by the static and dynamic prices and ranges
system to the Trading and Control Committee’s attributes have been included in
Circular 5/1998, which deals with this Committee.

       In pursuit of the objectives outlined above, the Board of Directors of the
Sociedad de Bolsas has approved the following Circular, which reorganises the
Operating Rules of SIBE, replacing and cancelling the first section to the previous
Circular of the Sociedad de Bolsas 6/2000, of 5 December 2000.

           (SIBE).

1. SCOPE OF APPLICATION

 SIBE shall be governed by these Operating Rules without prejudice to the
 outcome of the application of Stock Market Regulations and other general
 provisions. The rules included in the Traders’ Manual and the Circulars and
 Operating Instructions approved by the board of directors, the Trading and
 Control Committee and the various governing bodies shall also be applied.

 These Rules have a general nature as regards the operating of SIBE, shall be
 directly applicable to main trading and, as regards other trading types and
 segments, shall be applicable in all cases which are not the object of specific
 regulations governing each of these.


2. OPERATING REGIME
 Trades shall be deemed valid when they take place as a result of the use of
 the technical instruments or media registered for each member, who will
 assume full and exclusive responsibility for all trades carried out in this way,
 without exception.

 Trades carried out in this way shall be definitively confirmed with full obligatory
 enforceability. Exceptionally, an operation may be cancelled with the consent
 of the contracting parties always provided the appropriate authorisation has
 been granted by the Supervision Department or the Trading and Control
 Committee.

 Once the trades have been carried out, each of these shall be assigned to the
 stock market to which the buying member belongs.


3. SESSIONS AND TRADING HOURS
      3.1 Sessions

           SIBE shall be open on all business days from Monday to Friday
           which figure in the legally established calendar and at the times
           given below.

       3.2 Trading hours


              3.2.1 Trading hours for main trading and for the “Nuevo
              Mercado”

           Securities included under main trading and in the special “Nuevo
           Mercado” trading segment shall have the following trading hours:

                      3.2.1.1 Opening auction

                      From 8:30 to 9am, when orders can be entered, modified
                      and cancelled but no trades can be made.

                      The auction shall end at any time, within a maximum 30-
                      second period with a random ending, from 9am.



                                                                                   5
              On special occasions, as indicated in section 6.2.4.2, the
              opening auction may be extended for two minutes, ending
              at random within a maximum additional period of thirty
              seconds.

       3.2.1.2 Open session.

       From 9am to 5.30pm when continuous trading is carried out. This
       may be interrupted by volatility auctions.

       3.2.1.3 Volatility auctions

       When the circumstances described in section 5.5 arise during an
       open session, a volatility auction will be activated with a duration
       of five minutes, ending at random within an additional period of at
       most thirty seconds.

       During this period, orders can be entered, altered and cancelled
       but no trades can be made. Once the auction has ended, the
       open session will restart.

       If a security’s volatility auction overlaps the closing auction, the
       security will remain under auction with the conditions of the
       closing auction.

       3.2.1.4 Closing auction

       From 5.30pm to 5.35pm, when orders can be entered, altered
       and cancelled but no trades can be made. The auction shall end
       at any time within a random period of at most 30 seconds from
       5.35pm.


       On special occasions, as indicated in section 6.2.4.4, the closing
       auction may be extended for two minutes, ending at        random
       period within a maximum additional of thirty seconds.


       Subsequent auction periods may take place under the terms
       specified in these Rules.

       The duration of the auction periods may be altered when
       circumstances make this advisable and these alterations may
       affect a particular security, group of securities or all the trading
       activity.



       3.2.2. Trading hours for the Fixing Market

The fixing market shall have the following trading hours:

        - First auction period              8.30am to 12.00 noon.
        - First assignment of prices        12.00 noon.
        - Second auction period             12.00 noon to 4.00pm.

               - Second assignment of prices      4.00pm.


             Fixing market auctions will end within a maximum additional period of 30
             seconds with a random ending.


             3.2.3. Trading hours for the Blocks Market

      These shall be from 9:00am to 5:30pm except in auction periods for
      securities included in Main Trading and the “Nuevo Mercado”.



             3.2.4. Exceptional trading hours

      In the event of important news or events or particularly significant
      incidents arising during SIBE sessions, the usual duration of trading
      hours may exceptionally be altered in accordance with the provisions of
      section 12 of these Operating Rules.

      For the same reasons, the start of a session may be brought forward.
      This shall be announced via an Operating Instruction which shall be

4. ORDERS, CROSS OPPOSITE-SIDE ORDERS AND TRADING UNITS

 For purposes of the System, an order is defined as each position entered by a
 member indicating the security to be traded, whether the operation is buy or
 sell, the amount and the price in accordance with the provisions of the
 Traders’ Handbook.

 Given the exclusively buy or sell nature of each order, cross opposite-side
 orders will only take place if a member’s opposite-side orders are matched,
 due to these being among the best ask and bid prices at the time the orders
 are entered. No preference shall be given to these over those of other
 operators.

 Orders admitted in any market periods and market types shall be valid for the
 length of time indicated for these, with a maximum limit of 90 calendar days
 from their entry in the System. Those for which no period is indicated shall
 only be valid for the session in which they were entered.

 As a general rule, the trading unit shall be one unit of the corresponding
 security included in the System. However, via an Operating Instruction, a
 different trading unit may be established in the case of specific securities
 where this is considered necessary.


5. PRICES AND PRICE VARIATIONS




                                                                             7
      5.1. Reference price for the session

      At the start of each session, the reference price shall be taken as the
      closing price of the previous session, established in accordance with
      section 5.6 of these Rules.

      When a security is included in SIBE for the first time, the reference price
      shall be the official closing price of the Stock Exchange on which it has
      most recently been traded, and in the case of different prices among
      various Stock Exchanges, the price of the market in which the highest
      trading volume has been recorded in the preceding fifteen sessions.

      When a security is included in the System for the first time without
      having been traded previously on the Spanish Stock Exchanges, the
      reference price shall be that which corresponds to certain ask or bid
      positions.


      When it is difficult to arrive at a reference price, either for the reasons
      indicated in the two preceding paragraphs or because other exceptional
      circumstances arise, the reference price may be fixed on the basis of
      other parameters, with the CNMV (Spanish Securities Markets
      Commission) being notified of the measure adopted.


      When a capital increase begins, the theoretical value of the subscription
      right shall be deducted from the closing price of the previous session.

      In the event of financial tradeson a security which could affect the
      reference price, this may be altered as considered necessary.


      5.2. Static price and range

The static price is the price arising from the last auction or the price limiting the
static range if a volatility auction is held because of a breakout from the static
range. At the beginning of the session, the static price is the same as the
reference price.

The static range is the maximum variation permitted in the static price at any
given moment.

The static range is calculated on the basis of the historical volatility of each
security. The static range will be in force throughout the session.

      5.3. Dynamic price and range


The dynamic price is the last traded price of a security either from an auction
or from the execution of an incoming order in an open session.

This price is continually updated during the session.




                                                                                   8
The dynamic range is the maximum variation permitted in the dynamic price. It
is calculated on the basis of the historical series of price fluctuations of each
security during the sesion.

The dynamic range will be in force during both the open session and the
closing auction. However, it will not be in force during the opening auction, the
volatility auctions, nor during the extensions to the opening and closing
auctions.


5.4. Ranges and their diffusion

      The static and dynamic ranges for each security will be published in
      Operating Instructions and revised on a regular basis. They may
      exceptionally be modified at other times. Appropriate information will be
      provided on these measures and displayed on SIBE screens as soon as
      circumstances allow.

5.5. Volatility auctions

      During an open session, immediately before a trade at the limit price of
      the static range (breakout from the static range) or at the limit price of
      the dynamic range or outside this (breakout from the dynamic range), a
      volatility auction will automatically be generated on the security in
      question.

      If a volatility auction is activated due to a breakout from the dynamic
      range, the static price prevailing at that moment will be maintained
      throughout the auction. However, if a volatility auction is activated due to
      a breakout from the static range, the price responsible for the breakout
      will become the new static price.

      Volatility auctions will last for five minutes and end at any time within a
      maximum additional 30-second period with a random ending. During
      these auctions, orders can be entered, altered and cancelled but no
      trades can be made until the auction has ended, in accordance with the
      stipulations of section 6.2.4.3 to these Rules. The price resulting from
      these auctions will be the new static and dynamic price of the
      corresponding security.

      5.6. Closing price

      The session ends with a 5-minute auction, between 17:30 and 17:35,
      which will end at any time within a maximum additional 30-second period
      with a random ending. In accordance with the stipulations of section
      6.2.4.4, the price resulting from this auction shall be the closing price of
      the session. If there is no auction price or if fewer than 500 trading units
      are traded during the auction, the closing price shall be the price of the
      last 500 traded units closest to the weighted average. If two prices have
      the same difference with respect to this weighted price, the price will be
      the last executed. If 500 units have not been traded, the closing price will
      be the price of the previous session.

      In the Fixing Market, closing prices shall be based on 200 trading units.



                                                                                    9
            5.7 Minimum price variation

            The minimum variation shall be €0.01 if the price is equal to or
            lower than €50 and €0.05 if the price is over €50.

            If the amount resulting from the application of the static or
            dynamic range to a security is equal to or lower than a price unit,
            the provisions of section 5.8 to these Rules regarding the trading
            of warrants and subscription rights shall be applied.



            5.8 Special rules for warrants and subscription rights

            The minimum variation shall be €0.01 whatever the price of the
            warrant or subscription right at any given time.

            Warrants and subscription rights shall be subject to the maximum
            price variation limits established.




6. MAIN TRADING

     6.1. Definition

     In SIBE this is the type of trading for which priority in executing trades is
     determined by price and, if prices are equal, by the timing of order entry.

     6.2. Auction periods


            6.2.1. Definition

            Periods of time during which orders can be entered, altered and
            cancelled but no trades can be executed.

            During this period, the System will show the indicative prices that
            would be determined if, at that moment, the auction were to end.



            6.2.2. Types of order

            During auction periods, the following types of order shall be
            admitted according to price:




                                                                               10
           6.2.2.1. Limit order: order executed at the maximum
           purchase price or minimum sale price.

           6.2.2.2. Market order: order introduced without a price
           limit so it is executed at the auction price. If a market
           order is partially traded or not traded at the auction, it
           remains a market order.

           6.2.2.3. Market to limit order: order introduced without a
           price limit so it is executed at the auction price. Once the
           open session starts, the order is limited to the auction
           price. If there is no auction price, the order is rejected.


    6.2.3. Order conditions

    Orders may have hidden volumes (iceberg orders). These are
    entered in the System displaying only part of the volume to be
    traded, but take part in the auctions for the total volume. Only the
    displayed volume has priority in terms of time whilst both the
    displayed and hidden volumes have priority in terms of price.


    6.2.4. Determination of the auction price

           6.2.4.1 General rules for determining the auction price

           The price for orders introduced during the auction period
           shall be the one which allows the maximum volume of
           orders to be executed.

           If there are two or more prices at which the same order
           volume can be executed, the auction price shall be the
           one which leaves the smallest surplus (the difference
           between bid and ask volumes that could be traded at the
           same price).

           If there is no surplus or if the surpluses are the same, the
           price of the side with the larger volume shall be taken.

           If the three conditions stipulated above are the same, the
           auction price, of the two possibles, shall be that which is
           closer to the last executed price with the following
           exceptions:

-          If the last executed price is within the range of the two
           possible auction prices, then this shall be the auction
           price.

-          If there is no last executed price or this is outside the
           static range of prices, the auction price shall be that which
           is closest to the static price.




                                                                     11
If, at the end of the auction period, ask and bid volumes
are unequal, the security may be traded during the
session as soon as buy and sell prices coincide.


6.2.4.2. Resolution of the Opening Auction



The auction shall be resolved in accordance with the rules
developed in section 6.2.4.1.

However, if, once the auction period has ended, the
resulting price for a certain security is within the static
range limit or the volume of Market and Market to Limit
orders is not covered by the total volume to be traded, the
system will not make an allocation for this security and the
auction period will be extended.

If at the end of the auction extension period, regardless of
the price fixed, the volume of Market and Market to Limit
orders has been covered by the total volume to be traded,
trading units will be allocated in accordance with section
6.2.5, and the security will go into open session. If the
volume of Market and Market to Limit orders exceeds the
total volume to be traded, the security will remain under
auction, which will end as established in section 6.2.4.5.2.
of these Rules.

6.2.4.3. Resolution of Volatility Auctions

The auctions shall be resolved in accordance with the
rules developed in section 6.2.4.1.

However, if, at the end of the volatility auction, regardless
of the price fixed, the volume of Market and Market to
Limit orders has been covered by the total volume to be
traded, trading units will be allocated in accordance with
section 6.2.5, and the security will go into open session. If
the volume of Market and Market to Limit orders exceeds
the total volume to be traded, the security will remain
under auction, which will end as established in section
6.2.4.5.2. of these Rules.



6.2.4.4. Resolution of the Closing Auction

The auction shall be resolved in accordance with the rules
developed in section 6.2.4.1.

However, if, once the auction period has ended, the
resulting price for a certain security reaches or exceeds
the dynamic range, or is at the static range limit, the



                                                          12
system will not make the automatic allocation and the
security enters the closing auction extension period.

Once the two-minute closing auction extension period has
elapsed and the corresponding random ending has taken
place, the system will make the allocation in accordance
with section 6.2.5.




6.2.4.5 Other special circumstances.

      6.2.4.5.1 Incorporation of securities to the
Spanish Stock Exchange Interconnection System

If, during the auction period, special circumstances arise
concerning a particular class of security, because this is
being included in SIBE for the first time without having
been previously listed on the national stock markets, the
following measures may be taken, singly or cumulatively,
with the CNMV (Spanish Securities Markets Commission)
being notified of the measures adopted:

1ª A reference price may be chosen which seems
reasonable in view of the circumstances, so that, on the
basis of this reference price, it is real supply and demand
factors which establish, in accordance with the
stipulations of section 6.2.4.1. of these Rules, the first
price.

2ª Static ranges for the reference price may be altered as
required.

6.2.4.5.2 Exceptional market situations.

When special circumstances affect a security, either
because trading in it has been suspended or because the
volume of Market and Market to Limit orders is not
covered by the total volume to be traded or due to any
other circumstance in which the reference price or the
static price prevent normal trading, the following
measures may be taken, singly or cumulatively, with the
CNMV (Spanish Securities Markets Commission) being
notified of the measures adopted:

1ª The security may be provisionally maintained in auction
until the situation normalises, with the possibility of this
being extended until the closing auction.

2ª Static ranges for the static price may be altered as
required.


                                                         13
             Appropriate information regarding the application of these
             measures shall be provided and displayed on the screens
             of SIBE as soon as circumstances allow.


      6.2.5. Allocation of trading units in the auction

      Once the auction price has been fixed, shares shall first be
      allocated to market and market to limit orders (without distinction,
      on the basis of when they entered the market), then to limit
      orders with higher prices than the auction price and, finally, to
      other limit orders at the auction price, as far as possible,
      according to the order in which these were introduced in the
      System.


6.3. Open session

      6.3.1. Definition

      Period of time during which orders may be traded, as well as
      entered, altered and cancelled.

      6.3.2. Types of order

             6.3.2.1. Limit order: order executed at the maximum bid
             price or minimum ask price.

             6.3.2.2. Market order: order introduced without a price
             limit and executed at the best opposite-side price at the
             time of introduction. If a market order is partially traded or
             not traded, the order or the non-executed part remains a
             market order.

             6.3.2.3. Market to limit order: order introduced without a
             price limit so it is executed at the best opposite-side price
             at the time of introduction. If, at the best price, there is no
             sufficient volume to satisfy the order, this shall be partly
             satisfied, with the rest being limited to that price. If there is
             no tradeable price on the opposite side of the market, the
             order is eliminated from the System.


      6.3.3. Restrictions on the execution of orders

             6.3.3.1. Minimum execution: these are orders which
             specify a minimum trading volume to be executed when
             they are introduced in the System. After this, the rest is
             treated as an order without restrictions. If there is
             insufficient volume to meet the minimum amount
             specified, these orders shall be automatically excluded by
             the System without any amount being traded.


                                                                           14
                         6.3.3.2. Fill or Kill: these are orders which have to be
                         totally executed immediately or rejected. This is a special
                         type of minimum execution order for which the said
                         minimum execution is equal to the total of the order.

                         6.3.3.3. Immediate or Cancel (Execute or Eliminate):
                         these are orders which are executed immediately with the
                         non-executed part being eliminated from the System.


                  6.3.4. Conditions of the order

                  Orders may have hidden volumes, i.e. only part of the trading
                  volume is displayed in the System. Once the displayed volume
                  has been executed, the rest is considered, for all intents and
                  purposes, as newly introduced hidden volume (iceberg) orders.


                  6.3.5. Allocation of trading units in the open session

                  In the open session, orders executing tradesshall be matched to
                  orders on the opposite side of the market according to price
                  ranges and, if prices are equal, by timing priority.


                  6.3.6. Trading in securities at times of high volatility during
                  the open session

                  If imbalances were to arise between supply and demand which
                  prevented usual trading in a security, trading in the said security
                  could be temporarily suspended giving rise to an auction at which
                  the provisions of section 6.2.4.5.2 would come into effect.

                  Appropriate information regarding the application of these
                  measures shall be provided and displayed on the screens of
                  SIBE as soon as circumstances allow.


7. BLOCK MARKET

          7.1. Definition

          This is designed to allow members to apply cross opposite-side orders
          or carry out trades, provided that they meet the volume and price
          requirements established for gaining access to block trading conditions.


          7.2. Order validity

          Only orders valid for one day may be entered, and they must have
          issued by just one person, understood to be an individual or legal entity
          with decision-making power in respect of the whole order. The grouping
          of orders proceeding from various persons in this respect is expressly
          prohibited.



                                                                                  15
7.3. Announcement of operations

Before the start of the auction period on the day following the day of
execution of the trades, details of the trades executed shall be provided
to the various Spanish stock markets and entered on the System’s
screens.


7.4. Trading of Agreed Blocks

Market members may apply opposite-side orders or execute trades,
through SIBE and within the established time scale, provided that they
meet the following requirements:


       7.4.1. Price

    A ± 1% fluctuation from the mid point of the range between the best
    bid and ask positions in main trading or trading in the special trading
    segment known as the “Nuevo Mercado”, shall be permitted,
    depending on the security in question.

    The SIBE screens shall show the price range within which trades
    can be executed.



       7.4.2. Minimum amount

    The effective amount of the trade must total a minimum of €600,000
    and represent at least 2.5% of the average daily trading volume of
    the security in question over the last complete calendar quarter. To
    this effect, only trades executed through main trading and trading in
    the special trading segment known as the “Nuevo Mercado” shall be
    taken into account, all special tradesand trading on the block market
    being excluded.

    The SIBE screens shall reflect the minimum volumes required and
    the dates these requirements come into force.


       7.4.3. Securities included

    All stocks that form part of the IBEX 35 at any given time shall be
    included, together with any other securities that it is decided to
    include, due to their characteristics or the prevailing circumstances.

    The inclusion of a security in or its exclusion from the block market
    shall, at the very latest, be announced in the session immediately
    preceding the one in which it is required to take effect.

    No trades may be made on securities that cannot, for any reason,
    be traded on SIBE.

           7.5. Parametrised Block Market

           Market members may apply opposite-side orders or execute trades,
           through SIBE and within the established time scale, provided that they
           meet the following requirements:



                  7.5.1. Price

                  The permitted maximum divergence from the static price is 15%
                  for Main Trading and fixing securities and 25% for “Nuevo
                  Mercado” securities, depending on the security in question.



                  7.5.2. Minimum amount

               The effective amount of each order shall total a minimum of
               €1,200,000 and represent at least 5% of the average daily trading
               volume in the security in question over the last complete calendar
               quarter. To this effect, only trades executed through main trading,
               trading in the special trading segment known as the “Nuevo

               taken into account, all special tradesand trading on the block market

               The SIBE screens shall reflect the minimum volumes required and
               the date these requirements come into force.


                  7.5.3. Securities included

                  All securities traded on SIBE may be traded under this system.


8. TRADING OF SECURITIES ON THE FIXING MARKET

           8.1. Operating procedures

           Auction periods, order entry and the fixing of prices shall be carried out
           in accordance with the general rules established in section 6.2.4.1, with
           a single price for each security traded on this market being fixed at the
           end of each auction period, assigning to each price the corresponding
           trading units in each case.

           Following the first price fixing stage, and the corresponding assignment
           of trading units, another auction period shall be held until the second
           price fixing stage, when a new assignment of trading units shall take
           place and the session shall be closed for the securities traded on this
           market. Both auctions shall end within a random 30-second period.



                                                                                   17
            This market segment does not have dynamic ranges but it does have
            static ranges. Auctions for this market segment do not have extensions.

            8.2. Selection of securities

            The main criterion taken into account in assigning securities to this
            market shall be liquidity. To measure this, the size and stability of the
            following variables shall be taken into account:

            −   Effective daily total traded in ordinary operations, excluding those
                that are large in volume relative to the total and do not increase the
                market’s liquidity.

            −   Number of daily operations.


            −   Range of best bid and ask prices.


            −   Annualised rotation index: Number of trading units traded in ordinary
                tradesdivided by the number of listed shares.


            −   Trading frequency: Percentage of sessions in which the security has
                traded.


            Other variables can also be used as necessary to establish the liquidity
            of each security.

            In the case of securities traded through SIBE, their inclusion in the fixing
            market shall depend on the market capitalisation, the size of the free
            float and other distinctive features of the securities in question and of
            their issuers’ standing on the stock market.

            8.3. Review of securities

            The decision regarding which securities are to be traded on the fixing
            market shall be taken every six months, other than in the case of new
            securities added to trading via SIBE or in cases where market conditions
            call for a special review before the six-month period has elapsed.



9. TRADING IN THE SPECIAL TRADING SEGMENT KNOWN AS THE “NUEVO
MERCADO”

     The trading of securities included in this special trading segment shall meet the
     following special requirements:

            9.1 Fixing of the first price when the securities in question are first
            included for trading via SIBE.




                                                                                         18
     This first price shall be established on the basis of the real supply and
     demand introduced into the System, in accordance with section 6.2.4.1,
     and shall function as a reference price in all respects.



     9.2 General rule governing price variations for each session.


     Variations in price of the securities traded on this segment shall be
     governed by the provisions of sections 5 and 6 to these Rules with the
     securities of this segment having a single static range.



     9.3 Special rule governing static range variations for the segment’s
     securities.


     In exceptional situations, the static ranges of one, several or all the
     securities of this segment may be altered, taking into account the
     circumstances of each case.

     The application of these measures shall begin with an auction and
     appropriate information in this respect shall be provided and displayed
     on the screens of SIBE as soon as circumstances allow.

     This modification of the static range of the prices of all or some of the
     segment’s securities may be carried out as many times as is necessary.

     9.4 Exceptional extension of sessions.


     In cases where the exceptional circumstances referred to in section 9.3
     of this Rule arise in the final moments of a session, a decision may be
     taken exceptionally to extend the same. This extension shall be
     governed by the criteria set out in the previous section of this Rule with
     regard to auction periods.



     9.5 Actions of members obliged to promote the liquidity of the
     securities included in the segment.

     The actions of these members shall be regulated by a special rule,
     establishing both the limits of their actions and the transparency
     requirements with which they must comply in performing this role.


10. SPECIAL OPERATIONS

     10.1. TradesCommunicated

     The members of the stock markets may apply opposite-side orders, or
     carry out trades without publicly communicating them, through SIBE,

outside established trading     hours,   provided    that   the   following
requirements are met:


      10.1.1 Price

      May not be more than 5% higher than the highest, or 5% lower
      than the lowest, of the following of the day’s prices:

      a) Weighted Average Price
      b) Closing Price

      Trades may be made at any price in the range resulting from the
      application of the percentages indicated above.

      After the close of the session, SIBE will publish the closing
      prices, weighted average price and the price range at which
      trades may be made, on its screens.

      In the event that no trades have been matched in the day, the
      average weighted price and closing price will correspond to the
      nearest preceding session in which prices have been
      established.



      10.1.2. Minimum amount

      The effective amount of each order shall total a minimum of
      €300,000 and represent at least 20% of the average daily trading
      volume for the security in question in the last complete calendar
      quarter. The calculation of this volume shall take into account the
      trades executed in SIBE within the established hours of trading
      and those binding positions dealt with in accordance with point
      10.1.4 of these Rules.

      The Sociedad de Bolsas Supervision Department shall keep the
      Market informed of the volumes required and the date these
      requirements come into force.



      10.1.3. Individualised orders

      Both purchases and sales shall be executed in one single order,
      i.e. issued by just one person, an individual or a legal entity, with
      the capacity to take a decision in respect of the whole order. In
      this respect, the criteria set out in Arts. 4 and 7 of Royal Decree
      377/1991, of 15 March, on the communication of major
      shareholdings in listed Companies, and their acquisition of own
      shares, shall be applicable. The grouping together of orders is
      expressly prohibited.

10.1.4. Prior coverage of binding cash or paper positions


Market members may record binding bid or ask positions in a
certain security, the sole aim of this being that these positions be
dealt with in special tradesoutside normal trading hours, always
provided the said positions meet the following requirements:

a) They have been communicated to the Supervision
   Department in the form established in the Traders’ Manual.

b) They are for an effective amount of at least €30,000

c) They are not rejected by the Sociedad de Bolsas Supervision

   admitted may not be more than the greater of the following

    - An effective total of €60,000.

    - 5% of the average daily trading volume for the last complete

A single binding position shall be deemed to be that placed by a
single person, with the breakdown of orders therefore being

In the event that the Supervision Department rejects a binding

exceed the amounts indicated above. This specification shall be
made by means of fax model O.E./004, sent to the Sociedad de
Bolsas Supervision Department, before the close of the market.

d) does not exceed the price variation limit permitted.

The positions that meet the conditions indicated above shall be
classed as binding positions at the close of the session and up to
8pm on the same day, bearing in mind the following specific
details:

• in converting an order into a binding order, the total volume
shall be taken into account (hidden volume, if it exists, as well as
displayed volume).

• an order shall only be considered binding for one day, whatever
the validity of the order itself.

11. ALTERNATIVE PROCEDURES

In the case of partial System failure, resulting in the exclusion of a limited group
of traders from the System, the Stock Market Governing Bodies shall have in
place the corresponding alternative systems, such that these traders are able to
enter orders on the System.

If the System failure affects all traders and is on a scale such that trading might
not be expected to begin or be resumed within a reasonable period, the Board
of Directors shall immediately be notified of this fact, with a view to it taking
appropriate measures with the corresponding stock markets, so as to introduce
the necessary procedures for ongoing trading in the affected securities on the
markets where they are listed, until System tradesreturn fully to normal.



12. EXCEPTIONAL PRECAUTIONARY INTERRUPTION OF TRADING,
EXTENSION OR RESUMPTION OF THE TRADING SESSION AND SYSTEM
SHUTDOWNS.

       12.1 Precautionary interruption of trading

       If important news or events or particularly significant incidents arise in
       the course of the SIBE session and could affect the normal performance
       of the same or the satisfactory operation of the System’s technical
       support, trading may interrupted as a precautionary measure for the time
       required to assess the full implications of the news, event or incident.

       Notification of the interruption of trading shall be given through the SIBE
       terminals and shall immediately be brought to the attention of the stock
       markets and the CNMV.

       During the precautionary interruption period, orders may not be entered
       or executed, although the System may be consulted.

                                                                                 24
            Following the end of the precautionary interruption period, an auction
            period shall be held, with subsequent resumption of trading, to which all
            other sections of these Rules shall be applicable.

            If such news, events or incidents arises in the final moments of the
            session, and as a result trading cannot be interrupted on a precautionary
            basis, the session may be exceptionally resumed even when it would
            previously have been concluded in accordance with the normal timetable
            established in section 3 of these Rules.

            This resumption or the possibility of resumption shall be announced via
            the SIBE terminals in the period between the publication of the end of
            session message and the publication of the closing price announcement.
            The communication of the possible resumption of the session shall
            expressly indicate that the last prices fixed at the end of the session are
            provisional.

            In the event that the session is in fact resumed following the above-
            mentioned announcement, this resumption may be preceded by a
            special auction period or comprise a period of this type, with the setting
            of prices that this entails.

            The length of time for which the session is resumed or extended in the
            context of a precautionary interruption of trading shall be determined on
            the basis of the prevailing circumstances in each case.

            Both the exceptional resumption of a session previously concluded and
            the extension of a session due to a precautionary interruption shall be
            restricted to the absolute minimum length of time required. The resulting
            prices shall be subject to the general rules relating to closing prices.


            12.2 System shutdowns.

            If five or more members are unable to trade, SIBE’s tradesshall be shut
            down until the incident is resolved. This shutdown shall not in any event
            exceed 30 minutes, after which period trading shall be resumed.

            If all members of a stock market are unable to trade, SIBE’s tradesshall
            be shut down until the incident is resolved. This shutdown shall not in
            any event exceed 45 minutes, after which period trading shall be
            resumed.

            In the event that the market shutdown affects the auction period or the
            last 30 minutes of the open session, the Supervision Department may
            extend the auction period or the open session period, for as long as it
            deems necessary, though the extension may not exceed the interruption
            that justifies it, nor be for a period of less than 5 minutes.
