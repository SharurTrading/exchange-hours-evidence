                 Operating Instruction Nº 47/2023



                 APPLICATION OF TRADING AT LAST (TAL) PHASE IN THE FIXING SYSTEM
                 (Fixing)

                 In light of the entry into force of Circular 1/2023, on September 20, 2023, amending the
                 Market Rules of the Stock Exchange Interconnection System, the regulation of two new
                 functionalities has been incorporated into the Stock Exchange Interconnection System:
                 At-the-Close (AtC) orders and the Trading at Last (TAL) phase.

                 Regarding these new rules, the Trading and Supervisory Committee of Sociedad de
                 Bolsas has approved, in the exercise of its functions, this Operating Instruction about
                 the implementation of the Trading at Last (TAL) phase functionality for securities traded
                 in the Fixing System.



                 First. Implementation of the Trading at Last (TAL) phase in Fixing.

                 Circular 1/2023, on September 20, amending the Market Rules of the Stock Exchange
                 Interconnection System, with effect from December 4, 2023, includes in its Fifth section
                 new rules for trading securities in Fixing, introducing the possibility of trading these
                 securities in the new Trading at Last (TAL) phase.

                 The Trading and Supervisory Committee of Sociedad de Bolsas has decided not to apply
                 the Trading at Last (TAL) phase to securities traded in Fixing.

                 In line with the above, orders entered into the system with the At-the-Close (AtC) validity
                 condition that are not executed, either totally or partially, in the second fixing auction
                 will be automatically canceled.


                 Second. Entry in force

                 This Operating Instruction shall apply from the day following its publication.




                 Notice is hereby given for the appropriate purposes.
