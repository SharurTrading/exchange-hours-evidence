       Deutsche Börse Readiness Newsflash | MiFID II/MiFIR order flagging requirements: Final reminder of Algo ID solution and CUE pre-validation tool - Changed requirements with T7 Release 15.0 
       https://www.cashmarket.deutsche-boerse.com/cash-en/Stay-Informed/circulars-newsletters/deutsche-boerse-circulars/Deutsche-B-rse-Readiness-Newsflash-MiFID-II-MiFIR-order-flagging-requirements-Final-reminder-of-Algo-ID-solution-and-CUE-pre-validation-tool-Changed-requirements-with-T7-Release-15.0-5665664 
       As announced with Deutsche Börse Circular 027/26 from 16 June 2026, we kindly remind you of the changes to the Algo ID solution, the Common Upload Engine (CUE) validations for Short Code ID and Algo ID uploads, and an enhancement of the Short Code ID and Algo ID error reports, which will be introduced with T7 Release 15.0 on 9 November 2026.

Production start: 9 November 2026

Please be reminded that Trading Participants are required to familiarize themselves with the new requirements for the Algo ID solution, including the submission of information on substantial updates to their trading algorithms and the annual submission of test documentation explaining the means of testing. This information is also required for new trading algorithms. 

In addition, the CUE pre-validation tool for Short Code ID and Algo ID upload files will be decommissioned, and upload validations will be limited to checks of file type, file structure, and naming convention. For intraday uploads, comprehensive validation results of the file content are provided to Trading Participants via intraday reports TR160 - TR163 at 10:00 CET, 14:00 CET, and 18:00 CET, in addition to the end-of-day reports.

Furthermore, the existing field “processingTime” in Short Code ID and Algo ID reports TR160, TR162, TR169 will be enhanced for error 1 “ShortCodeID registration / AlgoID registration is missing”. The timestamp of the Short Code ID or Algo ID missing identification will be added. Therefore, the format of the field changes and Trading Participants shall adhere to the new field format.

For further regulatory and functional information, please refer to the Deutsche Börse Circular published on 16 June 2026, the new “sample upload file Algo ID”, the dedicated “Reporting handbook for audit trail and other regulatory reporting under the MiFID II / MiFIR regime”, the “Mini Reporting handbook Short Code and AlgoIDs”, the “CUE validation and file specification Short Code ID and Algo ID” and the “User guide GUI Short Code ID and Algo ID Upload” under the following path: MiFID II > Reference data reporting.

For further technical information, please refer to the T7 final release notes published on 2 October 2026 under the following path: T7 Release 15.0.

If you have any questions or comments, please send an e-mail to: mifid.reporting@deutsche-boerse.com.

Kind regards,
Your Client Services Team

       Introduction of tk accelis Group AG & Co. KGaA in Deutsche Börse Xetra trading on 28 October 2026 
       https://www.cashmarket.deutsche-boerse.com/cash-en/Stay-Informed/circulars-newsletters/deutsche-boerse-circulars/Introduction-of-tk-accelis-Group-AG-Co.-KGaA-in-Deutsche-B-rse-Xetra-trading-on-28-October-2026-5660818 
       1. Introduction 

As part of the spin-off of tk accelis Group AG & Co. KGaA from thyssenkrupp AG, subject to the issuer's admission to the Regulated Market (Prime Standard), we hereby inform you about the procedure for the first price determination on trading venue Deutsche Börse Xetra (MIC: XETR) for shares of tk accelis Group AG & Co. KGaA on 28 October 2026.

First trading day: 28 October 2026

2. Required action

Trading Participants have to make sure that the Product Assignment Group of the new instrument is assigned to their trader groups.

3. Details of the first price determination 

The following trading parameters will apply:

Please note that the market opening for the tk accelis Group AG & Co. KGaA share will take place in parallel to the thyssenkrupp AG share. For details, please refer to the paragraph below or the table with the schedule for the first price determination.

Pre-trading on the first trading day will start from 07:00 CE(S)T. The call phase for the first auction (Opening Auction - Opening Auction Call; display in Deutsche Börse Xetra: “OpnA”) will start at 08:55 CE(S)T and will last at least five minutes. Consequently, the first price determination in Deutsche Börse Xetra trading will take place not earlier than 09:00 CE(S)T.

The following will apply to the Opening Auction:

During the Opening Auction Call Phase, if the order book is crossed, the indicative auction price, auction volume and auction surplus (volume and side) will be displayed. In addition, the first ten price levels will be displayed via GUI, EMDI and MDI and the full market depth via EOBI.

Once the Opening Auction concludes, Continuous Trading will commence.

Please note the following special procedures:

Orders entered with trading restriction “OAO” (Opening Auction only) will only be considered in the first auction of the exchange trading day, i.e. in the first price determination for the shares. Orders entered with trading restriction “AOO” (Auction only) will be considered in the opening auction, the intraday auction and the closing auction.
The usual safeguards in Deutsche Börse Xetra (MIC: XETR) will also be applied in the Opening Auction. Therefore, a volatility interruption may occur following the Auction Call Phase.
The intraday auction starts at 13:00 CE(S)T and takes at least two minutes.
If an exceptional number of orders is executed in any one auction, it may lead to delays in processing confirmations.
Moreover, the "Continuous trading with auctions" market model applies.

Treatment regarding the MDAX® index:

Following the spin-off of tk accelis Group AG & Co. KGaA from thyssenkrupp AG, an adjustment to the index is required. On 28 October 2026, tk accelis Group AG & Co. KGaA will be included in the MDAX® index for one day. This adjustment ensures the trackability of the index for investors.

On 28 October 2026, after close of trading, the tk accelis Group AG & Co. KGaA share will be removed from the MDAX® index and the weighting of the thyssenkrupp AG share will be adjusted accordingly.

       T7 Release 15.0: Important information for production start 
       https://www.cashmarket.deutsche-boerse.com/cash-en/Stay-Informed/circulars-newsletters/deutsche-boerse-circulars/T7-Release-15.0-Important-information-for-production-start-5650620 
       1. Introduction 

Deutsche Börse Group will launch Release 15.0 of the T7 trading system on Monday, 9 November 2026. 

The support window for T7 Release 15.0 will take place on Saturday, 7 November 2026 between 15:00 and 18:00 CET. Connectivity testing during the support window is strongly recommended for all participants and Multi-Member Service Providers. 

This circular outlines the key topics relevant for the production launch of T7 Release 15.0 and provides details regarding the connection test.  

Connection test: 7 November 2026 
Production start: 9 November 2026 

2. Required action

Participants are requested  

to complete and return the production readiness statement (see section 3.H.) and  
to register a Technical Contact for the support window (see section 3.G.)  

no later than Friday, 30 October 2026. 

3. Details of the initiative 

Deutsche Börse Group will launch Release 15.0 of the T7 trading system on Monday, 9 November 2026. Please refer to the dedicated document “T7 Release 15.0 - Functional Technical Release Notes Deutsche Börse Xetra and Deutsche Börse Frankfurt, Version 2” for a full overview of both the new and changed functional scope.

The document is available on the Deutsche Börse Cash Market website www.cashmarket.deutsche-boerse.com via the following path: T7 Release 15.0 > Overview and Functionality. 

A. Backward compatibility

T7 Release 15.0 will not provide backward compatibility with the T7 Release 14.1 interface versions of T7 ETI, FIX LF and EDCI. Participants must use the new interface versions and will no longer be able to connect to T7 using the T7 Release 14.1 interface layouts following the production launch of T7 Release 15.0. 

The public market and reference data interfaces, including EOBI, EMDI, MDI and RDI/RDF, as well as reports and data files, will also not provide backward compatibility. 

B. Decommissioning of deprecated ETI Requests

The following deprecated ETI requests will be permanently decommissioned with the production introduction of T7 Release 15.0 and will no longer be available: 

New Order Single 
New Order Single (short layout) 
Replace Order Single 
Replace Order Single (short layout) 

Participants must ensure that they have completed the migration to the replacement ETI requests prior to the production launch. 

For a detailed overview of the new ETI order entry requests to be implemented, please refer to the Cash Market Deutsche Börse Readiness Newsflash from 4 September 2026: Mandatory Implementation of New ETI Order Entry Requests with T7 Release 15.0. 

C. Decommissioning of T7 Java Admin GUI 

The T7 Java Admin GUI will be decommissioned with the production launch of T7 Release 15.0, as is already the case for the T7 Release 15.0 Simulation.  

Participants must ensure that their migration to the T7 Web Admin GUI is completed before the production launch on 9 November 2026. Following the deployment of T7 Release 15.0, access to the T7 Java Admin GUI will no longer be supported.  

The new T7 Web Admin GUI is fully operational and available to all T7 users via Internet and N7 leased-line access.

For further information, please refer to: New T7 Web GUIs  

D. Preparations for Friday, 6 November 2026 and Saturday, 7 November 2026 

On Friday, 6 November 2026, T7 Release 14.1 will be in production for the last time. Reports for the business day 6 November 2026 can be expected at the usual time.  

On Saturday, 7 November 2026, Deutsche Börse will convert the T7 production systems to Release 15.0 and initial reference data for business day 9 November 2026 will be available via RDI and RDF following the start of the system in preparation for the support window. 

E. Support Window and Important Information for T7 Release 15.0 

This section details the upcoming support window and essential information for the T7 Release 15.0 production launch on Monday, 9 November 2026. 

Support Window on Saturday, 7 November 2026 (15:00 – 18:00 CET) 

Connectivity testing during the support window is strongly recommended for all participants and Multi-Member Service Providers to ensure connectivity at the start of trading on Monday, 9 November 2026. Please ensure that a staff member is available to log on to the T7 production system (e.g., via the T7 Java Trader GUI, T7 Web Admin GUI and/or third-party applications). 

At this time, the T7 production system will be available for connectivity testing on the ETI, FIX LF, FAGSE Gateways, RDI, MDI, EMDI, EOBI and EDCI interfaces and via the T7 Web Admin GUI, the T7 Web Clearer GUI and the T7 Java Trader GUI. No order and quote entries will be possible. 

Start of Day: during the support window, at approximately 15:30 CET, all products in the T7 production systems will be set to “Start of Day” providing participants the opportunity to perform their regular start of day activities:   Area Availability/special processes 

Market data: 

After the start of day, product and instrument state change information messages will be sent out. Thereafter, snapshots are sent out periodically (MDI, EMDI, EOBI) in addition to functional beacons or heartbeats via the incremental stream in EMDI and EOBI respectively.
Traders will not have access to the order book.
Please refer to the document "T7 Release 15.0 Market and Reference Data Interfaces Manual" (Section 9.11.1) for further details. 

ETI and FIX LF Gateways: 

During the production introduction weekend, the T7 ETI, FIX LF, and FAGSE Production Gateways will be available for participant connectivity.
In general, session and user logons will be possible.
ETI: It will be possible to send retransmission requests.
FIX LF and FAGSE Gateways: It will be possible to send resend requests.
Orderbook management will not be possible.
Order book restatement messages will be sent during start of day switch.
Please refer to the documents "T7 Release 15.0 Enhanced Trading Interface Manual" (Section 4.7.11) and "T7 Release 15.0 FIX LF Manual" (Section 3.8.11) for further details. 

Connection Test Message: 

During the support window a message “Connection Test Deutsche Börse Xetra. <hh:mm:ss>”, “Connection Test Deutsche Börse Frankfurt <hh:mm:ss>” will appear with an increasing sequence number in the market news view (news board) of the T7 Java Trader GUI and the T7 Web Admin GUI. 

FIX LF MsgSeqNum's: 

Please note that after the connection test, the inbound and outbound MsgSeqNum's will not be reset upon the next start of day or restart of the FIX Gateway. This is documented in Section 5.10.2, “Outage on T7 FIX LF Side”, of the “T7 Release 15.0 FIX LF Manual”.

Support Window Updates: Deutsche Börse will inform participants about the general progress of the connection test via implementation news on the Cash Market website available under the path: Implementation News

Following the end of the support window, the T7 15.0 production system will shut down on Saturday, 7 November 2026 at 18:00 CET and will remain unavailable until Sunday evening. 

F. Monday, 9 November 2026 – Production Start 

The first trading day with T7 Release 15.0 will start as usual. 

Please note: As the system will be restarted on Sunday evening after the connection test on Saturday, the initial reference data file for the business day on 9 November 2026 will be regenerated. 

As the reference data that will be provided on 7 November 2026 via RDI and RDF prior to the support window may potentially contain some changes, participants are required to use the initial reference data provided following the system start for 9 November 2026. 

Persistent orders entered until the end of trading on 6 November 2026 but not executed will remain available on 9 November 2026, unless other validity restrictions apply. 

A list of any items relevant prior to the production start of T7 Release 15.0 will be provided as of 6 November 2026. This “Known Limitations” list will be available on the Cash Market website under the following path: T7 Release 15.0 > Production

G. Technical Contact for the Support Window 

Please enter details of the contact person who will be available during the support window in the Member Section of Deutsche Börse Group not later than Friday, 30 October 2026. The path is:  

Member Section > Company Contacts > [+] Create Contact > Contact Types > Release

Please choose the category “Connection Test Responsible” and make sure to check the box “Cash Market T7”. This will ensure that Deutsche Börse will be able to reach you in any emergency between Saturday, 7 November 2026 and production start on Monday, 9 November 2026. 

H. Provision of the Readiness Statement 

We kindly ask all Trading Participants, ISVs and TPIPs to submit the Readiness Statement for T7 Release 15.0 by Friday, 30 October 2026 at the latest. Please note that the provision of the Readiness Statement is mandatory.  

The online Readiness Statement for T7 Release 15.0 is available here: Readiness Statement. 

Please enter your dedicated Trading PIN in the online questionnaire. It is available for Central Coordinators and their Deputies in the Member Section under: My Profile > PIN.

       With this Newsflash, we announce the introduction of the next Member Section enhancement with Member Section Release October 2026, which will be available as of Wednesday, 7 October 2026.

A new regional jurisdiction field has been added to the Trading Location form. This enhancement ensures full alignment with local regulatory parameters while streamlining the registration and verification process.
 

New regional jurisdiction field in Trading Location form for local regulatory alignment

To reflect distinct regional regulatory frameworks across various jurisdictions, a new mandatory field has been introduced in the application form for the registration of a new Trading Location.

What is changing?

When submitting an application from an applicable country subject to regional regulatory oversight, applicants will now be required to select their specific region from a dedicated dropdown menu.
The dropdown list presents regions that meet the regulatory prerequisites for the respective application.
If your specific region is not listed in the available options, please select the indicated option. Once you proceed with the application, you will be asked to contact your Key Account Manager, who will assist you in reviewing the regulatory requirements for your jurisdiction.

If you have any questions or require assistance with an ongoing application, please contact your dedicated Key Account Manager or client.services@deutsche-boerse.com.
