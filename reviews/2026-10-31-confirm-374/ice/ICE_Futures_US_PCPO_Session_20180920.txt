NOTICE
SEPTEMBER 20, 2018


EXTENSION OF THE PRE-OPEN ORDER ENTRY SESSION FOR COFFEE “C”®,
COTTON NO. 2®, COCOA, FCOJ, SUGAR NO. 11® AND SUGAR NO. 16 FUTURES
CONTRACTS

ICE Futures U.S. Rule 4.22 provides for a pre-open order entry session during which only Limit orders (including
Stop-Limit orders and Stop Orders with Protection) may be entered and during which resting Good ‘Til Cancelled
(“GTC”), Good ‘Til Date (“GTD”) and Good ‘Til Date and Time (“GTD&T”) orders that remain active may be cancelled
or revised.

Commencing for trade date Monday, October 8, 2018, the pre-open order entry session for Coffee “C”, Cotton No. 2,
Cocoa, FCOJ, Sugar No. 11 and Sugar No. 16 futures contracts will be enhanced by the addition of a new post-close
pre-open (“PCPO”) session that will start at 30 minutes after the end of trading for the contract and end at 6:00 pm on
the Exchange business day prior to each trading day. The addition of this new PCPO session does not impact the
regular pre-open order entry session, which will continue to be held at the current times for each of these contracts.

The new PCPO session is for the entry and amendment of GTD, GTC and GTD&T orders only. Market participants
seeking to enter new Day orders are advised that while the system will permit the creation of new Day orders during
the PCPO order entry session, any such Day orders entered during the PCPO session will be automatically
killed at the end of the PCPO session and will not remain live at the end of the PCPO session. Market
participants seeking to enter new Day orders must wait until the start of the regular pre-open order entry session to
enter such orders.

As an example, for trade date Monday, October 8, 2018 the PCPO for each of these futures contracts will start 30
minutes after the close of trading on Friday, October 5 and end at 6:00 pm on Friday, October 5, 2018, and the pre-
open order entry session will start and end at its regular time, as shown below.

                   Cotton No. 2                       2:50 PM                 6:00 PM                   7:30 PM                   9:00 PM
