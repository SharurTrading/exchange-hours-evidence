                 Mock session on eOFS platform from Disaster Recovery (DR) site

The Exchange shall be conducting mock bidding session for eOFS from its Disaster Recovery (DR) site.
The schedule for the same is given below:

 Date                          Sessions       Location & Configuration       Schedule & Instructions
 October 31, 2026              Mock Session   Disaster Recovery (DR)         Annexure 1

Members may note that no changes are required to be made at their end for connecting to
Primary/DR site and the same URL as mentioned below shall be used for login.

URL: https://eofs.nseindia.com

Important instructions for members:
      • Kindly participate actively in the mock bidding session to check the connectivity.
      • New/revised announcements with respect to mock bidding sessions on October 31,2026, if
         any, shall be available on the website www.nseindia.com
      • Bids from mock bidding session on October 31,2026 shall not attract any obligation in terms
         of funds and securities.
