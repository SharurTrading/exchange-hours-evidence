 To All Members,

 Sub: Muhurat Trading Session on Account of Diwali

 This is further to circulars NSE/CMTR/76941 dated October 19, 2026, NSE/CD/76972 dated
 October 19, 2026, NSE/COM/76978 dated October 19, 2026, NSE/EGR/76928 dated
 October 19, 2026, NSE/FAOP/76940 dated October 19, 2026, NCL/CMPT/77030 dated
 October 21, 2026, NSE/DS/77072 dated October 22, 2026, providing the schedule for special
 trading session on Sunday, November 08, 2026 on account of ‘Muhurat Trading on Diwali’.

 In view of the above, Members are required to note that the UCCs identified as compliant by
 the Exchange by 22:00 hrs on Saturday, November 07, 2026 shall be considered as
 Permitted to Trade on Sunday, November 08, 2026 for Muhurat trading on Diwali.

 Trading Members are required to refer the report mentioned below downloaded by Exchange
 on daily basis for the UCCs permitted to trade on the next trading date.
 Report Name: “SEG_TMCODE_UCC_TRADING_STATUS_DDMMYYYY_nn”

 Trading Members are requested to take a note of the above.
