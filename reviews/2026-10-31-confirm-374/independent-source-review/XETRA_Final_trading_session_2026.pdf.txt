Final trading session on 30 December 2026

The final trading session in the Regulated Market of Frankfurt Stock Exchange (FWB) and in
the Open Market of Deutsche Börse AG at FWB will take place on Wednesday, 30 December
2026. The following trading and service times apply to this trading day. All times are stated in
Central European Time (CET).


1. Market Deutsche Börse Frankfurt (electronic trading system T7 - MIC: XFRA)

from 7.00 a.m.        Start of pre-trading
from 8.00 a.m.        Continuous auctions
from 2.00 p.m.        Start of post-trading
from 3.00 p.m.        End of post-trading

The start and end times of post-trading deviate from those on a regular trading day. The start
of end-of-day processing of the electronic trading system T7 for the Deutsche Börse Frankfurt
market remains unchanged. Reports will be distributed following end-of-day processing.

2. Market Deutsche Börse Xetra (electronic trading system T7 - MIC: XETR)

from 7.00 a.m.        Start of pre-trading
from 8.00 a.m.        Start of retail early
from 8.55 a.m.        Opening auction call
from 1.00 p.m.        Intraday auction call
from 2.00 p.m.        Closing auction call
from 3.00 p.m.        End of post-trading

Price determination in the closing auction will take place no earlier than 2.05 p.m. For
DAX®, MDAX® and SDAX® instruments, the subsequent Trade-at-Close period will end
at 2.10 p.m. The start of end-of-day processing of the electronic trading system T7 for the
Deutsche Börse Xetra market remains unchanged. Reports will be distributed following end-
of-day processing.

3. Availability of off-book services

   a) Market Deutsche Boerse Frankfurt (electronic trading system – MIC: XFRA):
      TES OTC and TES LIS trades in combination with clearing and settlement may be
      entered until 2.59 p.m. on 30 December 2026.

   b) Market Deutsche Boerse Xetra (electronic trading system T7 – MIC: XETR):
      TES OTC and TES LIS trades in combination with clearing and settlement may be
      entered, and Xetra EnLight may be used, until 2.59 p.m. on 30 December 2026.
