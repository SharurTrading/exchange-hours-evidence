  CIRCULAR                            Temporary change to trading hours and designated
  10/070                              settlement periods due to US Daylight Saving Time in 2010
                                      and notification of resulting changes to the UK or US expiry
                                      times of specific Contracts

  Category:                           The purpose of this circular is to remind Members of the contents of
                                      circular 10/013 dated 26 February 2010 and to notify Members of the
  Trading Calendar                    resulting changes to the UK or US expiry times of specific contracts.

                                      The Circular advised Members that the period of Daylight Saving Time
                                      (“DST”) in the U.S will be different to British Summertime (“BST”).
  Attachments:                        As a consequence, the Exchange is making temporary changes to
                                      the trading hours and designated settlement periods of some of its
  None                                Contracts for the period between the end of BST and the end of DST.

                                      The temporary changes set out below will therefore apply during the
                                      period of Sunday 31 October 2010 to Friday 5 November 2010
  Summary of content:                 (inclusive). After this period the trading hours and designated
                                      settlement periods will revert to the normal times.
