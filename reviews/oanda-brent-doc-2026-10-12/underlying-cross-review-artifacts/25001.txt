Circular 25/001
Early Close of certain ICE Futures Europe Contracts on 09 January 2025
Category
Trading

Attachments
None


The purpose of this Circular is to advise Members that the Settlement Periods and Trading Hours of the below ICE
Futures Europe Contracts on the U.S. National Day of Mourning on Thursday 09 January 2025 will be amended as
follows:


  Contract                                                                          Time of Designated                               Time Trading Ceases
                                                                                     Settlement Period
  IFEU Two, Five, Ten and Thirty Year SOFR
  Swapnote Futures®                                                                   17:58-18:00 (GMT)                                     18:15 (GMT)
  IFEU One Month SOFR Futures                                                          12:58-13:00 (ET)                                      13:15 (ET)
  IFEU Three Month SOFR Futures



For the avoidance of doubt, there are no changes to the regular pre-open or open times of the aforementioned Contracts.
Full details of all ICE Futures Europe Trading Holidays can be found here.


Please ensure that the appropriate members of staff within your organisation and customers are advised of the content of
this Circular.
