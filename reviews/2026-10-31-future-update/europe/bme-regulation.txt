                    Learn about stock market regulation, which is key to ensuring transparency and the proper functioning of the market 
                   
                  
                  
                   
                    Financial market regulation establishes the rules that protect investors, ensure equal opportunities, and foster confidence in the system. Thanks to a solid regulatory framework, trading activities are supervised, abusive practices are prevented, and a fair and efficient trading environment is promoted — one that supports economic growth and market stability.
