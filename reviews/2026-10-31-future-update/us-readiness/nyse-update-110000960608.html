Effective December 6, 2026, FINRA/NYSE TRF® ("NYSE TRF") will extend its operating hours in alignment with the recently approved CTA and UTP Plan amendments to extend the operating hours of the Securities Information Processors pursuant to the proposed rule change to amend the FINRA Rule 6300 Series, as announced in a May FINRA Technical Notice and in a FINRA/NYSE TRF ® Trader Update in July 2026. 

NYSE TRF will operate from 9:00 p.m. ET Sunday through 8:00 p.m. ET Friday, with a one-hour pause between 8:00 p.m. and 9:00 p.m. ET Monday through Thursday, excluding holidays. This expands the current operating hours of 4:00 a.m. - 8:00 p.m. ET, Monday through Friday, excluding holidays. 

Extended Hours Trading Testing Schedule 

Certification testing will be available beginning October 2, 2026. 

FINRA/NYSE TRF® will conduct six tests ahead of the December 6 Production launch, in conjunction with the Securities Information Processors. Tests will begin at 11:00 p.m. ET on Friday and conclude at 12:00 p.m. ET on Saturday. 

The scheduled industry test dates are: 

  • October 2 | October 16 | October 30 | November 6 | November 20 | December 4 

Beginning November 8, 2026, test symbols will be available for Production testing during the Overnight Session.
