23×5 FAQ
The following information is current as of 08/21/26 and will be updated on a best-efforts basis. For the most current information, please contact your MemberX representative. All times outlined in this document are ET.
Q: What are MEMX’s plans for 23×5 trading?
A: Pending filing with the SEC, MEMX will operate nearly continuously, offering trading 23 hours a day from 9:00 PM through 8:00 PM, five days a week. Trading will begin at 9:00 PM on Sunday with a daily one-hour break occurring Monday through Thursday evenings between 8:00 PM and 9:00 PM.
Q: How will MEMX support 23×5 trading?
A: MEMX will add a new Trading Session to cover the 9:00 PM to 4:00 AM time period.

Q: When will MEMX support 23×5 trading?
A: Subject to SEC approvals,
MEMX Equities is targeting December 6, 2026,
provided the following conditions are met.
MEMX has received SEC approval for its Overnight Trading Session;
DTCC can receive and process trades on a real-time basis throughout the entire Overnight Trading Session;
The SIPs can receive and disseminate all exchange quotes and trades on a real-time basis;
The primary listing markets can produce corporate action information prior to 9:00 p.m. which will be provided to the industry by the SIPs.
MEMX Equities does not plan on making test symbols available in production for the overnight session prior to go live on December 6, 2026.
Q: Who is eligible to participate in 23×5 Trading?
A: All registered MEMX members may participate in any trading session, including the Overnight session.
Q: Are there additional membership documents needed to participate in 23×5 trading?
A: No. MEMX will not require additional Membership documents to participate in the Overnight session.
Q: Will I need new physical connections or MEMO ports to participate in 23×5 trading?
A: No. All connections and MEMO ports may be used in the Overnight trading session.
Q: How will MEMX’s protocols change to support 23×5 trading?
Order Entry: There are no protocol changes required to participate in the Overnight trading session. MEMX will require a limit price on pegged orders in the Pre, Post, and Overnight sessions.
Market Data:
A new ‘Overnight’ value will be added to the TradingSessionType field.
Although MEMX Equities recently announced new version, 2.0, of MEMO order entry and MEMOIR market data specifications, upgrade to the new version is not required to participate in overnight trading. Current MEMO and MEMOIR versions will continue to be supported. For further details on v2.0 can be found
here
.
Q: When will MEMX start accepting orders for the extended hours?
A: MEMX will begin accepting orders for the overnight session starting at 9:00 PM.
Q: What order types are supported?
A: All orders that are currently accepted in the Pre-Market session will be accepted in the overnight session.
Q: How will the system restart be impacted in a 23×5 trading environment?
A: The targeted time for the system shutdown will be communicated in a subsequent update. Once initiated members will lose connectivity to all order entry and market data systems.  MEMX will perform all necessary daily maintenance activities and communicate the system restart time in a subsequent update, at which point members may reconnect.  During the daily maintenance window, Market Data and Order Entry sequence numbers will be reset.  MEMX may need to delay the restart time without notice, and members should be prepared to handle variable restart times.
Q: How will the Risk Settings be supported in 23×5 trading?
A: All existing risk settings will be applied to the Overnight trading session.  Additionally, MEMX will introduce a new setting allowing clients to prevent orders in the Overnight session.
Q: Will orders automatically be cancelled after the Overnight session ends at 4:00 AM?
A: No, any open orders that are outstanding in the system at 4:00 AM will remain on the MEMX book eligible for execution.
Q: Will MEMX support routing during MEMX’s during the Overnight session?
A: Yes, MEMX will support routing for Overnight trading to eligible NMS exchanges, applying the same execution logic as it does during non-Overnight trading hours.
Q: What sale conditions apply to trades executed in the Overnight session?
A: Trades executed outside the Regular Market session will be assigned the ‘T’ sale condition.  These trades count toward the Consolidated Volume and are not Last Sale eligible.
Q: How will MEMX support LULD bands in the Overnight session?
A: Subject to SEC approval, Static LULD Price Bands will be in effect overnight from 9:00 PM to 4:00 AM. They will be fixed values provided by listing exchanges and remain constant throughout the Overnight session.
Q: How will MEMX handle corporate actions before and during the Overnight session?
A: MEMX will apply symbol changes and corporate actions from the information received from the listing exchanges and/or the SIP.
Q: How will trades executed in the Overnight session clear and settle?
A: Trades in the Overnight Session after 9:00 PM and before 12:00 AM (midnight) will be sent to DTCC with a Clearing Date of the next calendar date, for settlement one day thereafter.

Q: Will additional paperwork be required for clearing in the overnight session?
The MEMX Equities Clearing Letter of Guarantee is applicable to the overnight session. Clearing firms that do not plan to participate in clearing in the overnight session may submit a
MEMX Equities Overnight Trading Session Opt-Out Notice
.
Q: How will trades in the Overnight Session on the last day of the month be counted for trading tiers?
A: Trades will be counted on the trade date in which they occur. Trades in the Overnight Session on the last calendar day of the month will be included in the following month’s trading tiers, based on the trade date.
Q: Will MEMX be available for trading on US exchange holidays?
A: Yes, if a holiday falls between Monday and Thursday, trading will commence at 9:00 PM on the holiday evening. For example, if Monday is a US exchange holiday, trading will begin at 9:00 PM on Monday and continue until 8:00 PM on Tuesday.
When there is an Early Close, the Regular Market session ends at 1:00 PM and the Post Market session ends at 5:00 PM, the market will be closed with no Overnight session for that calendar day.
