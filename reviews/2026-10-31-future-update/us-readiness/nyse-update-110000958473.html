Beginning Sunday, December 6, 2026, NYSE Arca will operate 23 hours a day. An Overnight Session will begin at 9:00 PM each evening, Sunday through Thursday, and end at 8:00 PM the following day. If a US exchange holiday falls between Monday and Friday, the Exchange will conclude its Late Session trading the evening before the holiday. The next Overnight Session will begin at 9:00 PM the evening of the holiday, if the following day is a trading day. All times are ET.

NYSE Arca Extended Trading Hours Functionality

  • New Trading Session: An Overnight Session will be introduced as a fourth distinct session on the existing NYSE Arca platform, covering all NMS securities from 9:00 PM to 4:00 AM. The Overnight Session will open with continuous trading at 9:00 PM with no opening auction; order entry will open at 8:59 PM. The existing Early, Core and Late Sessions will not change.
  • Connectivity: Firms will use their existing NYSE Pillar Gateway ports and submit a new TradingSessionID value to participate in the Overnight Session. 
  • Risk Controls: Existing risk limits (e.g., Gross Credit) will be available in the Overnight Session as in all other sessions. In addition, Entering and Clearing firms may opt clients in or out of the Overnight Session at the MPID or MPID + SubID level. 
  • Order Protection: Clearly Erroneous Execution (CEE) protections will be extended to the Overnight Session with doubled numerical guidelines. Limit Order Price Protection will apply as in the Early and Late Sessions.
  • Market Data: Consolidated and NYSE Arca proprietary data feeds will be available throughout the extended operating schedule on existing publication channels. A new Security Status message value of 'n' will be added for the Overnight Session in the Security Status and Market State fields. 
  • Specifications: Updated system specifications will be distributed via Trader Update in August.

Testing

In conjunction with the Securities Information Processors, the Exchange will conduct six tests ahead of the December 6 Production launch. Tests will begin at 11:00 PM on Friday and conclude at 12:00 PM on Saturday. The scheduled industry test dates are:

  • October 2 | October 16 | October 30 | November 6 | November 20 | December 4

Beginning November 8, 2026, NYSE Arca will support trading of test symbols in Production during the Overnight Session.

Regulatory Updates

  • LULD Plan Amendment: The LULD Plan has filed an amendment with the SEC to create Overnight Price Bands, which would be in effect from 9:00 PM to 4:00 AM, calculated using two reference prices: the primary listing market's official closing price and the consolidated last round lot sale as of 7:45 PM. NYSE Arca is prepared to implement these changes if the SEC approves the LULD Plan amendment.
    • The lower band would be set 20% below the lower reference price; the upper band would be set 20% above the higher reference price. 
    • Unlike LULD Bands in the Core Session, limit states would not be calculated by the SIPs and there would be no automatic Trading Pauses. Primary Listing Markets would instead have discretion to declare a Regulatory Halt for the remainder of the Overnight Session. 
  • Corporate Actions: The primary listing markets have agreed on harmonized rules for mandatory Regulatory Halts affecting securities subject to certain corporate actions. These rules will provide exchanges and market participants adequate time to process complex corporate actions and make adjustments to systems, orders, quotes, and reference data. For details, see the Corporate Action Halt Rules FAQ.
