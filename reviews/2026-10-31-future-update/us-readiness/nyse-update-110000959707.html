Testing

Certification testing will be available beginning September 27, 2026.

The Exchange will conduct six tests ahead of the December 6 Production launch, in conjunction with the Securities Information Processors. Tests will begin at 11:00 PM on Friday and conclude at 12:00 PM on Saturday.

The scheduled industry test dates are:

  • October 2 | October 16 | October 30 | November 6 | November 20 | December 4

Beginning November 8, 2026, test symbols will be available for Production testing during the Overnight Session.
