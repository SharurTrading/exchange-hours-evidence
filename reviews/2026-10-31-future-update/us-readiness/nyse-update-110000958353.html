Effective December 6, 2026, FINRA/NYSE TRF® ("NYSE TRF") will extend its operating hours in alignment with the recently approved CTA and UTP Plan amendments to extend the operating hours of the Securities Information Processors, pursuant to the proposed rule change to amend the FINRA Rule 6300 Series. 

NYSE TRF will operate from 9:00 p.m. ET Sunday through 8:00 p.m. ET Friday, with a one-hour pause between 8:00 p.m. and 9:00 p.m. ET Monday through Thursday, excluding holidays. This expands on the current operating hours of 4:00 a.m. - 8:00 p.m. ET, Monday through Friday, excluding holidays. 

The NYSE TRF Pillar FIX system specifications have been updated to reflect the upcoming extended operating hours. 

Please contact rmteam@nyse.com with any questions about this change.
