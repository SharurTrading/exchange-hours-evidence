Industry Test Reminder

On October 2, 2026, NYSE Arca will conduct the first Extended Hours Trading industry test in conjunction with the Securities Information Processors. The test will begin at 11:00 PM ET on Friday and conclude at 12:00 PM ET on Saturday. The test script can be found here.

The full schedule of industry test dates is as follows:

October 2 | October 16 | October 30 | November 6 | November 20 | December 4

  • Certification testing will be available beginning Sunday, September 27, 2026.  
  • Test symbols will be available for Production testing during the Overnight Session beginning November 8, 2026.
