Beginning December 6, 2026, subject to SEC approvals, NYSE Arca will extend its trading hours to operate from 9:00 PM  ET Sunday through 8:00 PM ET Friday, with a one-hour pause between 8:00 PM and 9:00 PM ET Monday through Thursday, excluding holidays.  

Highlights 

New Trading Session - NYSE Arca will introduce the Overnight Trading Session as a fourth distinct session, running from 9:00 PM to 4:00 AM and trading all NMS securities. Trading begins continuously at 9:00 PM with no opening auction. Order entry will begin one minute earlier, at 8:59 PM.  

Existing Connectivity - Firms will use their existing NYSE Pillar Gateway ports and send a new TradingSessionID value to participate in the Overnight Session. Clearing firms can opt clients in or out of the new Overnight Session at the MPID level.    

Order Protection - NYSE Arca will extend Clearly Erroneous Execution (CEE) protections to the Overnight Session, with doubled numerical guidelines. Limit Order Price Protection will apply in the Overnight Session as in all other sessions. Additionally, the LULD committee has filed a proposed amendment to extend market-wide volatility controls to the Overnight Session. 

Corporate Action Processing - NYSE Arca and other listing exchanges will file rules to standardize how certain corporate actions are handled overnight, including the ability to declare regulatory halts uniformly across all NMS securities 

Market Data - Consolidated and NYSE Arca Proprietary Data Feeds will be available 23 hours a day. 

Updated system specifications will be distributed via Trader Update. 

For additional information, please visit NYSE Extended Hours Webpage or review the Extended Hours Trading Frequently Asked Questions.
