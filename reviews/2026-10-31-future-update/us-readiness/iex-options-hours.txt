Orders may be executed on IEX or routed away from IEX during the Pre-Market, Regular Market, and Post-Market Sessions, collectively known as "System Hours." All times are in Eastern Time (“ET”).

System Startup Completion
Upon startup, heartbeats followed by the Sequence Number Reset, Underlying Ref Data and Symbol Mapping messages are published on multicast feeds. Retrasmission and snapshot services are available.

Business Open
Logical session connectivity is available.

Begin Order Entry Acceptance
Orders and quotes eligible for entry prior to Continuous Trading may be submitted for participation in the Opening Process and will be reflected on IEX Options DEEP.
8:00 a.m.
Begin Opening Process
Following the Opening Trigger (22.160(a)(4)), IEX Options will initiate the Opening Process. Upon completion of the Opening Process, the applicable symbol will transition to Continuous Trading.
9:30 a.m.*
Regular Market Session (i.e. Continuous Trading Session)
Trading and acceptance of orders and quotes conclude at 4:00 p.m. ET, except for eligible Late Close symbols, for which trading and acceptance of orders and quotes conclude at 4:15 p.m. ET.
The list of Late Close symbols subject to extended trading eligibility are listed in the IEX Options System Settings Specification.

System Close
