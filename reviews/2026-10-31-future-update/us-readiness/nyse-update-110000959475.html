Overview

NYSE American Options will introduce Extended Trading Hours ("ETH") for certain eligible equity options, as previously announced.

Two new sessions will be added before and after the Core Trading Session: an Early Trading Session from 7:30 AM to 9:25 AM ET and a Late Trading Session from 4:00 PM to 4:15 PM ET. The Core Trading Session will remain unchanged.

Updates

  • Certification Testing will be available beginning Tuesday, August 18, 2026
    • Available Symbols: AAPL, AMD, AMZN, AVGO, BABA, BAC, F, GLD, GOOG, GOOGL, HOOD, INTC, IWM, META, MRVL, MSFT, MU, NFLX, NOK, NVDA, ORCL, PFE, PLTR, QQQ, SNDK, SPY, TSLA, TSM, XLF. 
      • GLD, IWM, QQQ, SPY, XLF will be available for the Core Trading Session (Session 2) until 4:15 PM, not in the Late Trading Session (Session 3).
    • Including Early and Late Trading Sessions
  • Test Symbols available in Production beginning Monday, September 21, 2026 will be NTEST (enabled for Early, Core, Late) and PTEST (enabled for Early, Core).
  • The NYSE American Options Extended Trading Hours Fact Sheet has been updated.
