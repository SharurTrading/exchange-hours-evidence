Overview

NYSE American Options will introduce Extended Trading Hours ("ETH") for certain eligible equity options. Two new sessions will be added before and after the Core Trading Session: an Early Trading Session from 7:30 AM to 9:25 AM ET and a Late Trading Session from 4:00 PM to 4:15 PM ET. The Core Trading Session will remain unchanged.

Implementation Schedule

  • Certification Testing Available: Friday, August 14, 2026 
  • Pre-Production with Test Symbols: Monday, September 21, 2026 
  • Production Symbols: To be announced via a subsequent Trader Update, pending effectiveness of regulatory filings (will go live no earlier than September 21, 2026)

Participation Requirements

To participate in ETH, firms must have a Letter of Guarantee from a Clearing Member authorized by the OCC and provide appropriate risk disclosures to customers before accepting orders. Firms may use their existing NYSE Pillar gateway ports with a new TradingSessionID value to participate.
