Complex QCC and Complex Customer-to-Customer Cross Orders - Leg Price Support

Effective September 21, 2026 for NYSE American Options and September 24, 2026 for NYSE Arca Options, NYSE will support the entry of individual component leg prices for Complex QCC and Complex Customer-to-Customer cross orders submitted via FIX. When a Complex cross order passes validation, the legs will execute at the prices provided on the order.
