Equity Trader Alert #2026 - 61
UPDATED: FINRA/Nasdaq TRF Software Change Scheduled for November 2, 2026

Markets Impacted:

+1 212 231 5180

Trading Specifications
Post Trade 23/5 FAQ
Nasdaq previously announced two FINRA/Nasdaq TRF software changes in
ETA #2026-55
to prepare for the TRF expanded system hours change effective December 6, 2026. The TRF shutdown change will be effective November 2, 202
6.
The TRF clearing processing change will be effective December 6, 2026, in alignment with the TRF expanded hours (instead of November 2, 2026, as noted in
ETA #2026-55
).
For more information about the TRF expanded system hours change, please refer to
ETA #2026-39
.
What is changing?
TRF Shutdown at 8:00 p.m. ET
As described in Section 3.1 of the
Nasdaq FIX Trading Reporting Specifications
, the FINRA/Nasdaq TRFs will shut down at 8:00 p.m. ET each day the TRFs are open,
a change from the current shutdown time of 8:20 p.m. New trade reports and trade actions will be rejected beginning at 8:00 p.m. and while the system remains closed. Any
match-eligible and trade compare trade reports
will be carried over and remain available for trade matching during the next Trade Date that the TRFs are open. Firms using Post-Trade Risk services should note that the clearing firm’s default action will apply to all trade reports with a Held status at the 8:00 p.m. system close. Firms should note that if they report a trade after 7:45 p.m., clearing firms will have less time than the standard 15-minute period to action the held trade before the Post-Trade Risk system closes. This shutdown schedule change will occur on
November 2, 2026.
TRF Clearing Processing Change
Effective December 6, 2026, the FINRA/Nasdaq TRFs will send real-time clearing reports to the NSCC during expanded system hours, from 9:00 p.m. Eastern Time (ET) Sunday through 8:00 p.m. ET Friday, with a one-hour pause from 8:00 p.m. to 9:00 p.m. ET on Monday through Thursday.
As outlined in
ETA #2026-48
and
in previous industry communications, the NSCC has developed new 24/5 requirements, includi
ng a requirement for clearing firms to establish
separate 9A/9B trading relationships
for the overnight session
. If firms do not adhere to the NSCC requirement, there could be a situation where QSR clearing trades with an execution timestamp in the Overnight Trading Session (8:00 p.m. - 4:00 a.m.) are reported to and accepted at a TRF, but those same trades would be soft rejected at NSCC if firms have not established their 9A/9B agreements for the overnight session, as required by NSCC. Upon a soft NSCC reject, firms will be notified by the Nasdaq TRF Operations team and will have until 6:00 p.m.
on
the same business day to establish new NSCC paperwork and remediate these trades with the NSCC. If no action is taken, these trades will be
hard rejected
by the NSCC.
Note
: DTCC’s 9A/9B Trading Relationship validations do not apply to AGU clearing trades. Nasdaq encourages firms that wish to trade overnight to update their QSR agreements in DTCC’s Automated Special Representative Facility through the DTCC Portal.
Client Testing Availability
The FINRA/Nasdaq TRFs currently offer Expanded Hours in the Nasdaq Testing Facility (NTF)
.
Weekend Production User Acceptance Testing opportunities are also available on dates aligned with SIP industry testing and Nasdaq on-exchange testing. For the testing schedule and additional information, please refer to
ETA #2026-50
.
Additional Information
For more information regarding the Nasdaq Testing Facility (NTF), including billing and registration information, please refer to our
NTF overview
,
FAQ
, and
pricing
pages. For FINRA/Nasdaq TRF technical questions about the expanded system hours, firms may review the
Trading Specifications
, the
Post-Trade 23x5 FAQ
, or
contact
Nasdaq Trading Services
or
TRF Ops
at +1 212 231 5180. For regulatory questions regarding your trade reporting responsibilities, please refer to the listed contacts in
FINRA’s Technical Notice
.
“FINRA/Nasdaq TRF” means the FINRA/Nasdaq TRF Carteret and the FINRA/Nasdaq TRF Chicago, depending on the facility to which the member firm reports.
