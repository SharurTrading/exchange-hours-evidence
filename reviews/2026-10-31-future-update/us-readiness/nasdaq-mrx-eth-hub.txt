MRX Extended Trading Hours (ETH)
Nasdaq MRX (MRX) has amended its rules with the Securities and Exchange Commission (SEC) to expand trading hours for certain multi-listed Equity Options, Exchange-Traded Products (ETP), and Index Options outside of Regular Trading Hours (RTH).  MRX’s proposed rule filing will introduce the following trading sessions for permissible securities:
Early ETH Session – 7:30 a.m. – 9:25 a.m. EST
Extended Close Session – 4:00 p.m. – 4:15 p.m. EST
MRX’s filing permits MRX to designate the following options as eligible for the ETH:
Index Options: NDX, NDXP, and XND
Equity and ETP Options
Equity and ETP Options Eligibility Criteria: MRX may designate as eligible for trading during ETH up to 100 multiply listed Equity and ETP option  classes that satisfy the following criteria for the preceding six calendar months:
The option has an average daily volume of 150,000 contracts;
The underlying equity to the option has a $50 billion market capitalization; and
The underlying equity to the option has an average daily trading volume of 10 million shares.
The exchange may also designate as eligible for trading during ETH any equity option that is traded on another exchange during  ETH or any other trading session that is not RTH, and any equity option designated in this manner will not be subject to the 100 multiply listed option class limit.
