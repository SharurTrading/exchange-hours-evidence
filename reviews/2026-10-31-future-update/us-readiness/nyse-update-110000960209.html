Effective Monday, October 19, 2026, NYSE American Options will introduce Extended Trading Hours ("ETH") for eligible Production symbols, pending regulatory effectiveness.

For additional details about ETH, including eligible symbols and session information, please refer to the Extended Trading Hours Fact Sheet.

Extended Trading Hours - Industry Tests

On Saturday, September 19, 2026, NYSE American Options will participate in an ETH industry test between 7:30 a.m. and 12:00 p.m. ET. Please register to test by emailing trading@nyse.com. The test script can be found here.

A second industry test will take place on Saturday, October 17, 2026.

Test Symbols in Production Environment

Beginning Monday, September 21, 2026, NTEST will be enabled as an equity option class with an Early, Core, and Late Trading Session, and PTEST will be enabled as an ETF option class with an Early and Core Trading Session only, as previously announced.
