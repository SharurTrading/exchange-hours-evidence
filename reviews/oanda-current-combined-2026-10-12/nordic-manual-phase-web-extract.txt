L475@P12: 3.4 Concept of calls
L476@P12: Opening, closing and intraday calls are formed with two sub phases; order
L477@P12: management and uncross.
L478@P12: 1. Order management
L479@P12: a. During pre-open there is market transparency of all orders with displayed
L480@P12: volume. Orders only eligible for the opening call are not displayed.
L481@P12: b. Before the closing call, only displayed orders are visible. Orders only
L482@P12: eligible for the closing call are not displayed.
L483@P12: 2. Price determination and share allocation takes place in uncross.
L484@P12: The opening call procedure is conducted to open all Order Books at virtually the same
L485@P12-13: time. 14(76)

L491@P14: 4 Sessions during the trading day
L492@P14: 4.1 Pre-open session
L493@P14: During the pre-open session, order and trade management including order entry for
L494@P14: opening and closing calls is allowed.
L495@P14: In the examples below Stockholm and Helsinki schedules are described.
L496@P14: 4.2 Calls
L497@P14: The Call procedure starts in all Order Books of the Market segment at virtually the
L498@P14: same time. A Call consists of two phases: order management and uncross. The uncross
L499@P14: lasts a short time, usually a fraction of a second. A random uncross sequence for the
L500@P14: Order Books will be applied.
L501@P14: To facilitate the price formation process in calls, the order book information is
L502@P14: supplemented with tentative matching information. It includes indicative matching price
L503@P14: based on the prevailing order information, and how much total volume (including nondisplayed volume) would be matched at the indicated price.
L504@P14: Uncross phase includes opening/closing price determination, share allocation and order
L505@P14: information delivery.
L506@P14: 4.2.1 Opening call
L507@P14: During the opening call there is Market by Order dissemination of the order book. Order
L508@P14: entry and full order management is available through the 9:00 opening auction uncross
L509@P14: (and after). Orders with time-in-force conditions Day, Good-Till-Cancel, Immediate-OrCancel and Good-Till-Time and On-open orders (Market-on-open (MOO), Limit-on-open
L510@P14: LOO) and Imbalance on-open orders (IOOP)) become eligible interest for the opening
L511@P14: auction. An IOC order is eligible for execution in the opening auction and will be
L512@P14: cancelled after the completion of the opening auction if it is not fully executed. On-close
L513@P14: orders (Market-on-close orders (MOC), Limit-on-close orders (LOC) and Imbalance onclose orders (IOOC)) can be entered, but are effective for closing auction only.
L514@P14: Orders are displayed in the market by order feed at their entered limit prices. The book
L515@P14: will show crossed prices if the highest bids are at higher price levels than the lowest
L516@P14: offers. The Market by Order does not include On-open, On-close, Non-displayed, Market
L517@P14: and Hidden iceberg volumes.
L518@P14: Orders entered during pre-open are assigned time priority. No matching (continuous
L519@P14: market) until 9:00. Imbalance information dissemination begins exactly 15 minutes
L520@P14: before opening call for all issues, and is updated once a second if information is
L521@P14: changed. Unexecuted orders (non-IOC and non-on open orders) remaining after the
L522@P14-15: uncross will transition into the continuous market with retained time-priority.16(76)

L545@P15: 4.2.2 Order entry during call
L546@P15: Time priority for orders entered prior to the uncross and during continuous trading is
L547@P15: based on the order entry time. Orders (with time-in-force condition GTC) entered prior
L548@P15: to the current trading day will keep their time priority.
L549@P15: 4.2.3 Closing call
L550@P15: Leading up to the closing call uncross there is Market by Order dissemination of the
L551@P15: continuous order book. The Market by Order does not include MOC, LOC, IOOC, nondisplayed, Market and Hidden iceberg volumes.
L552@P15: Continuous trading is halted 17:25 followed by a Pre-close period with no auto
L553@P15: matching. The Pre-close period lasts approximately for 5 minutes and ends with the
L554@P15: closing call uncross that randomly among order books takes place between 17:29:30
L555@P15: and 17:30. Order entry and full order management is available during the Pre-close
L556@P15: with the exception for pegged orders that cannot be entered.
L557@P15: Orders with time-in-force conditions Day, Good-Till-Cancel and Good-Till-Time are
L558@P15: transitioned automatically into the Pre-close and are eligible interest for the closing
L559@P15: auction. Pegged orders are transitioned at their last limit price. On-close orders, i.e.
L560@P15: Market-on-close orders (MOC), Limit-on-close orders (LOC) and Imbalance on-close
L561@P15: orders (IOOC) can be entered until the closing call uncross. An IOC order entered
L562@P15: during Pre-close is eligible for execution in the closing call uncross.
L563@P15: Orders entered are assigned time priority. The Imbalance dissemination begins exactly
L564@P15-16: 17:25 and is updated once a second if information is changed.17(76)
