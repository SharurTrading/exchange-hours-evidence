# mini-grains — ONE family. CBOT "Mini-Sized" grain futures (1,000-bushel contracts) have moved as a single block in every CME document I retrieved: the 2012 expansion notice names Mini-Sized Corn/Soybeans/Wheat together; the 22-Mar-2013 Global Command Center notice lists XC/XW/XK together with a single mini-specific close; the 2022 amendment table lists XC/MKC/XK/XW with identical current and new hours. MKC (Mini-Sized KC HRW Wheat) DOES belong here despite its KC-wheat lineage: SER-7044 launched it with "the same contract specifications - ... trading hours ... - as the existing CBOT Mini-Sized Corn, Soybean, and Chicago SRW Wheat futures", and the decisive test is 2015 - on captures taken the same day, KC HRW Wheat (KE) reads "Monday – Friday, 8:30 a.m. – 1:20 p.m. CT" while Mini-Sized KC HRW Wheat (MKC) reads "Monday - Friday, 8:30 a.m. - 1:45 p.m. CT". MKC follows the MINI grid, not the KC grid. No sub-splitting is warranted: I found no document in 2010-2026 in which XC, XK, XW and (post-2014) MKC carry different hours from one another.

**Products:** Mini-Sized Corn futures — CME Globex XC (clearing/ClearPort YC), CBOT Rulebook Ch. 10B, 1,000 bu.
Mini-Sized Soybean futures — CME Globex XK, 1,000 bu.
Mini-Sized Wheat (Chicago SRW) futures — CME Globex XW, 1,000 bu.
Mini-Sized KC HRW Wheat futures — CME Globex MKC (CBOT Rulebook Ch. 10N; trading-floor code MKC; the 2022 Globex notice gives MDP tag 6937-Asset = MKC and iLink tag 55-Symbol / tag 1151-SecurityGroup = MH). Listed 2014-03-23; not in the family before that date.
Explicitly EXCLUDED: standard-size ZC/ZS/ZW/KE (the crate's globex_grains key), Micro Ag futures MZC/MZS/MZW (launched Feb 2022, on the standard grid), and mini-sized OPTIONS (no mini options exist).

**Timezone:** America/Chicago (US Central). Every CME source quotes these products in "Central Time (CT)"; e.g. Globex Notice 20220905: "Sunday - Friday, 7:00 p.m. - 7:45 a.m. CT and Monday - Friday, 8:30 a.m. - 1:20 p.m. CT".

**Confidence:** high

## Reuse verdict

NEEDS ITS OWN KEY. Do not reuse globex_grains, and do not be fooled by the fact that the two envelopes are identical today.

The trap: as of 2022-10-02 the mini grid and the standard grain grid are byte-identical - 19:00-07:45 overnight, 08:30-13:20 day, same four queues. A reviewer looking only at a current envelope would conclude the key is redundant. It is not.

The product-family semantics are the opposite of redundant. CME built the mini contracts with a deliberate, permanent 30-minute-later close so that traders could offset mini against standard positions AFTER the standard settlement. CME says this in its own words twice: CFTC filing CBOT 13-092 footnote 1, "To maintain the traditional mini- to full-sized trading hours, both electronic and floor hours for CBOT Mini-Sized Corn, Soybean, and Wheat futures will close daily at 1:45 p.m. CT"; and SER-7044, "because mini-sized products have slightly longer trading hours and because mini- and standard-sized products allow for offsets, the mini-sized products continue to be a popular tool for adjusting positions at the end of each trading day for all categories of traders." That is a design property of the family, not an accident of one envelope.

The history bears it out. Over the roughly sixteen years from the January-2010 floor to today, the mini grid differs from the standard grain grid for about twelve of them:
  2010-01-01 .. 2012-05-19  DIFFERS  day close 13:45 vs 13:15; and from 2010-04-19 the PCP starts 13:45:30 vs 13:15:30
  2012-05-20 .. 2012-09-15  IDENTICAL  both 17:00-14:00 (the only overlap window before 2022)
  2012-09-16 .. 2013-04-06  DIFFERS  17:00-14:30 vs 17:00-14:00; PCP 14:40 vs 14:30
  2013-04-07 .. 2015-07-04  DIFFERS  08:30-13:45 vs 08:30-13:15
  2015-07-05 .. 2022-10-01  DIFFERS  08:30-13:45 vs 08:30-13:20 (the minis skipped SER-7395R entirely)
  2022-10-02 .. today       IDENTICAL
A caller asking for 2016, 2019 or 2021 through globex_grains would get a close 25 minutes too early on every trading day. That is precisely the failure the crate's existing grains comment - "Mini-sized Corn, Soybean, and Wheat diverged on 2012-09-16 and are not represented by this key" - was written to prevent, and the research shows the divergence is in fact older and longer than that comment implies: it predates the January-2010 floor and it survived until October 2022.

Two consequences for the model that follow from the same reasoning. First, the revision list is NOT a copy of the grains list: the minis have a 2012-09-16 revision the standard grains do not, they have NO 2015-07-05 revision, and they have a 2022-10-02 revision the standard grains do not. Second, MKC must live on this key and not on any KC-wheat key: on identically dated 2015 captures MKC closes 13:45 while KE closes 13:20. The KC lineage is a naming fact; the hours follow the mini design.
