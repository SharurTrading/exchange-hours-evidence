SUBJECT:  Reminder:  Initial  Listing  of  the  Southern  Yellow  Pine  (Fastmarkets)  Futures 

and Options on Southern Yellow Pine (Fastmarkets) Futures Contracts 

Please  be  reminded  that  effective  Sunday,  March  30,  2025  for  trade  date  Monday,  March  31,  2025,  Chicago 

Mercantile  Exchange,  Inc.  will  list  the  Southern  Yellow  Pine  (Fastmarkets)  Futures  and  Options  on  Southern 

Yellow  Pine  (Fastmarkets)  Futures  contracts  for  trading  on  the  CME  Globex  electronic  trading  platform 

(“CME  Globex”)  and  for  submission  for  clearing  via  CME  ClearPort.  SER  9524R  supersedes  SER  9524 

dated  March  4,  2025  and  is  being  issued  to  correct  the  CME  Globex  trading  hours  noted  below  in  blackline 

format. No other changes have been made to the original SER. 

Effective  Sunday,  March  30,  2025  for  trade  date  Monday,  March  31,  2025,  and  pending  all  relevant  CFTC 

regulatory  review  periods,  Chicago  Mercantile  Exchange,  Inc.  (“CME”  or  “Exchange”)  will  list  the  Southern 

Yellow  Pine  (Fastmarkets)  Futures  and  Options  on  Southern  Yellow  Pine  (Fastmarkets)  Futures  contracts 

(the  “Contracts”)  for  trading  on  the  CME  Globex  electronic  trading  platform  (“CME  Globex”)  and  for 

submission for clearing via CME ClearPort as set forth below.
