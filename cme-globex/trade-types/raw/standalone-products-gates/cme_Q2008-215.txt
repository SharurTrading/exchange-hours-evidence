*   # Extended Friday Close for Electronic Livestock and Dairy Products - Effective Friday, October 24, 2008

*       *           *   **To**
        *   Quote Vendors

    *           *   **From**
        *   Market Data Operations

    *           *   **#**
        *   Q2008-215

    *           *   **Notice Date**
        *   09 October 2008

    *           *   **Effective Date**
        *   24 October 2008

    *   Effective October 24, 2008, the close of electronic trading on Fridays for Live Cattle, Feeder Cattle, Lean Hog, Frozen Pork Belly and all Dairy futures and options on CME Globex will be extended from **1:30 p.m. CT to 1:55 p.m. CT**.

For more information, please call:

John Harangody, Director, Commodity Products 312-466-4437

Tom Clark, Associate Director, Commodity Products 312-930-4595

Tom Sandy, Associate Director, Commodity Products 312-930-3116

Please contact Market Data Operations (MDO) at [mdo@cmegroup.com](mailto:mdo@cmegroup.com), if you have any questions concerning this notice.
