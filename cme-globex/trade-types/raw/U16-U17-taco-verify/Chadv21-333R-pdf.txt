SUBJECT: Product Modification Summary for Clearing Firms, Bookkeeping Software Providers, ISVs 

Effective Sunday, October 3, 2021 for trade date Monday, October 4, 2021, and pending all relevant CFTC regulatory review periods, please be advised that the New York Mercantile Exchange, Inc. (NYMEX or EXCHANGE) will amend the Decimal Price Locator (DPL) for Micro WTI Crude Oil Futures. The DPL is changing from 2 to 4 to accommodate inter-commodity spreads on Globex at 4 DPL. 

In addition, a price of 0.0025 minimum price increment can be assigned when trading MCL Inter-commodity spreads.
