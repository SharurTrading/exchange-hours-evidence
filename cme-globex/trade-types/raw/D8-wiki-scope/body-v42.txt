Starting at 4 p.m. Central Time on Friday, May 29, 2026,
CME Group will expand
cryptocurrency futures and options
to 24/7 trading.
Cryptocurrency futures and options will trade continuously on CME Globex with at least a two-hour weekly maintenance period
occurring on the weekend. All holiday or weekend trading from Friday evening through Sunday evening will have a trade date of the following business day, with clearing, settlement and regulatory reporting processed the following business day.
This topic outlines the necessary information for processing cryptocurrency 24/7 trading.

### Saturday Maintenance Window Processing Overview
The following section outlines Saturday maintenance processing for 24/7 trading.
### Saturday Shutdown and Startup
### Shutdown
At the beginning of the maintenance
p
eriod, client systems will receive a
MDP 3.0 - Security Status
. Then CME Globex will send the iLink messages to
iLink Execution Report - Elimination
orders. Next, client systems will experience MDP multicast, TCP iLink and Drop Copy disconnections. At this point, clients should reset their books. CME Group recommends that clients and vendors restart their applications at this time and go through their own restart processes.
Startup
iLink order entry, Drop Copy and market data sequence numbers reset to 1 during the Saturday startup
cycle. Client systems will re-establish connectivity to the MDP multicast groups and receive the following startup messaging:
The
MDP 3.0 - Market Data Snapshot
(tag 35-MsgType=W) messages on the Market Recovery feed are deleted for the impacted 24/7 channel. Tag 83-RptSeq for Market by Price (MBP) book updates resets to 1 for each instrument repeating group.
CME Globex disseminates incremental real-time market data for that channel and client systems can process the data. A
MDP 3.0 - Market Data Incremental Refresh
(tag 35-MsgType=X) message will be sent with an empty End of Event indicator if no other information is available. If session statistics are available, they will be republished via the normal Incremental Refresh messaging:
Current session trade volume
Price levels in the order books
Highest and lowest trade
MDP 3.0 - Security Status
messages containing market states (per instrument group) will be sent for the 24/7 channel. Then any instrument states that are different from the instrument group state are sent (e.g., Forbidden/Halted instruments).
Pre-registered administrative information will be removed during the Saturday maintenance window. Clients must re-submit their
iLink Party Details Definition Request
following the pre-open on Saturday.

### 24/7 Client System Impacts
This section outlines 24/7 client system impacts.
### iLink Order Entry
At the beginning of the Saturday extended maintenance window, CME Globex will disconnect all clients' sessions; reset sequence numbers; and cancel resting day orders and mass quotes. Pre-registered administrative information will be removed during the Saturday maintenance window. Clients must re-submit their Party Details Definition
following the pre-open on Saturday.
### Market Segment Gateway (MSGW)
All existing iLink Order Entry functionality is supported. Client systems may leverage existing iLink MSGW sessions.
### Convenience Gateway (CGW)
With this launch, iLink CGW sessions will support seven day markets.

CGW sessions must be created
to support 24/7 trading. Newly created CGW sessions will support all Globex futures and options markets.
Existing
CGW sessions can only submit cryptocurrency futures and options orders during the weekday.

With launch, CME Globex Credit Controls (GC2) will support unique GC2 weekend Limits
. Client specified limits may affect weekend GT order cancellation
. The GC2 weekday schedule for 5 day clients begins Sunday at 4:05 p.m. CT. At this time, 5 day client systems can start trading. For weekday limits, 5 day clients can trade during the expanded weekday schedule.
