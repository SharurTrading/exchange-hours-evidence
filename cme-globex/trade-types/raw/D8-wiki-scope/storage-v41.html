Starting at 4 p.m. Central Time on Friday, May 29, 2026, CME Group will expand cryptocurrency futures and options to 24/7 trading.

Cryptocurrency futures and options will trade continuously on CME Globex with at least a two-hour weekly maintenance period occurring on the weekend. All holiday or weekend trading from Friday evening through Sunday evening will have a trade date of the following business day, with clearing, settlement and regulatory reporting processed the following business day.

This topic outlines the necessary information for processing cryptocurrency 24/7 trading.

Saturday Maintenance Window Processing Overview

The following section outlines Saturday maintenance processing for 24/7 trading.

Saturday Shutdown and Startup

Shutdown

At the beginning of the maintenance period, client systems will receive a MDP 3.0 - Security Status. Then CME Globex will send the iLink messages to iLink Execution Report - Elimination orders. Next, client systems will experience MDP multicast, TCP iLink and Drop Copy disconnections. At this point, clients should reset their books. CME Group recommends that clients and vendors restart their applications at this time and go through their own restart processes.

Startup

iLink order entry, Drop Copy and market data sequence numbers reset to 1 during the Saturday startup cycle. Client systems will re-establish connectivity to the MDP multicast groups and receive the following startup messaging: 

  1. The MDP 3.0 - Market Data Snapshot (tag 35-MsgType=W) messages on the Market Recovery feed are deleted for the impacted 24/7 channel. Tag 83-RptSeq for Market by Price (MBP) book updates resets to 1 for each instrument repeating group.

  2. CME Globex disseminates incremental real-time market data for that channel and client systems can process the data. A MDP 3.0 - Market Data Incremental Refresh (tag 35-MsgType=X) message will be sent with an empty End of Event indicator if no other information is available.  If session statistics are available, they will be republished via the normal Incremental Refresh messaging:

    1. Current session trade volume

    2. Price levels in the order books

    3. Highest and lowest trade

  3. MDP 3.0 - Security Status messages containing market states (per instrument group) will be sent for the 24/7 channel. Then any instrument states that are different from the instrument group state are sent (e.g., Forbidden/Halted instruments).

Pre-registered administrative information will be removed during the Saturday maintenance window. Clients must re-submit their iLink Party Details Definition Request following the pre-open on Saturday.

24/7 Client System Impacts

This section outlines 24/7 client system impacts.

iLink Order Entry

At the beginning of the Saturday extended maintenance window, CME Globex will disconnect all clients' sessions; reset sequence numbers; and cancel resting day orders and mass quotes. Pre-registered administrative information will be removed during the Saturday maintenance window. Clients must re-submit their Party Details Definition following the pre-open on Saturday.

Market Segment Gateway (MSGW)

All existing iLink Order Entry functionality is supported. Client systems may leverage existing iLink MSGW sessions.

Convenience Gateway (CGW)

With this launch, iLink CGW sessions will support seven day markets.

  • New CGW sessions must be created to support 24/7 trading. Newly created CGW sessions will support all Globex futures and options markets.

  • Existing CGW sessions can only submit cryptocurrency futures and options orders during the weekday.

iLink Session Support Summary

Session Summary

Impact

Existing MSGW Session

No impact. Existing MSGW sessions can send orders 24/7 on CME Globex.

Existing CGW sessions can only send orders between Sunday 4:00 p.m. and Friday 4:00 p.m. CT.

New CGW sessions can send orders 24/7 on CME Globex.

To trade during the weekend, create a new CGW session.

Drop Copy

All existing Drop Copy 4.0 Service for iLink functionality is supported. At the beginning of the Saturday extended maintenance window, CME Globex will disconnect all clients' sessions and reset sequence numbers.

Drop Copy MSGW Session

With this launch, client systems can leverage existing Drop Copy MSGW sessions.

Drop Copy CGW Session

With this launch, Drop Copy CGW target sessions will support seven day markets.

  • New CGW target SessionID must be created to support 24/7 trading. Newly created CGW target sessions will support all futures and options markets on Globex.

  • Existing CGW target sessions will only be active during the weekday.

New CME CF Rates and Indexes Channel

A new SBE - Streamlined Market Data for CME CF Cryptocurrency Indices (Channel 214) will launch with this migration, offering 24/7 availability. The new channel will provide parallel data to the existing Streamline CME CF Rates and Indexes channel (213) during weekday operations.

  • View connection information (channel, subnet, multicast, port, IP). CME Group Login is required.

New Streamlined Block Trades Channel

A new 7 day enabled SBE - Streamlined Market Data for Block Trades (Channel 235) for 24/7 cryptocurrency futures and options will launch with this release. Availability will support the same 24/7 maintenance windows as Globex.

  • View connection information (channel, subnet, multicast, port, IP); new IPs and ports (CME Group Login is required).

Credit and Risk Management

With launch, CME Globex Credit Controls (GC2) will support unique GC2 weekend Limits. Client specified limits may affect weekend GT order cancellation. The GC2 weekday schedule for 5 day clients begins Sunday at 4:05 p.m. CT. At this time, 5 day client systems can start trading. For weekday limits, 5 day clients can trade during the expanded weekday schedule.

7-day clients should follow the standard product schedule.

For more information see the CME Clearing & Global Operations Summary of Requirements and Guidelines for Cryptocurrency Trading and 24x7 Clearing.

Summary of Impacts for 5 Day Cryptocurrency Trading

The following table summarizes impacts for clients who choose not to support 24/7 trading.

Type

Impact

Schedule update

The GC2 weekday schedule for 5 day clients begins Sunday at 4:05 p.m. CT. At this time, 5 day client systems can start trading. For weekday limits, 5 day clients can trade during the expanded weekday schedule.

7-day clients should follow the standard product schedule.

CME Globex Sunday startup

Currently 5 day cryptocurrency futures and options clients can utilize CME Globex Start-up Processing. However, due to the new 7 day trading schedule there is no longer a Globex Sunday startup processing cycle.

Convenience Gateway (CGW) Support

Clients can continue to use 5 day CGW sessions, however, order submission is not available until 4:05 p.m. CT on Sunday. If a client wishes to trade between Friday 4:00 p.m. CT - Sunday 4:00 p.m. CT, a new CGW session must be created.

Good Till (GT) Order Elimination

If weekend trading is not enabled in CME Globex Credit Controls (GC2), GT orders will be eliminated on Friday at 4:00 p.m. CT. All client systems are encouraged to cancel all cryptocurrency futures and options GT orders before Friday 4:00 p.m. CT.
