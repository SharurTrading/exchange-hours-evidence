Title: Cryptocurrency Futures and Options Migration to 24-7 Trading - CME Group Client Systems Wiki

URL Source: https://cmegroupclientsite.atlassian.net/wiki/spaces/EPICSANDBOX/pages/1283194884/Cryptocurrency+Futures+and+Options+Migration+to+24-7+Trading

Markdown Content:
Starting at **4 p.m. Central Time on Friday, May 29, 2026,** CME Group will expand[cryptocurrency futures and options](https://www.cmegroup.com/markets/cryptocurrencies.html "https://www.cmegroup.com/markets/cryptocurrencies.html") to 24/7 trading.

Cryptocurrency futures and options will trade continuously on CME Globex with at least a two-hour weekly maintenance period occurring on the weekend. All holiday or weekend trading from Friday evening through Sunday evening will have a trade date of the following business day, with clearing, settlement and regulatory reporting processed the following business day.

This topic outlines the necessary information for processing cryptocurrency 24/7 trading.

### Saturday Maintenance Window Processing Overview

The following section outlines Saturday maintenance processing for 24/7 trading.

#### Saturday Shutdown and Startup

##### Shutdown

At the beginning of the[maintenance](https://cmegroupclientsite.atlassian.net/wiki/spaces/EPICSANDBOX/pages/1283194884/Cryptocurrency+Futures+and+Options+Migration+to+24-7+Trading#ex1 "#ex1")p eriod, client systems will receive a [MDP 3.0 - Security Status](https://cmegroupclientsite.atlassian.net/wiki/spaces/EPICSANDBOX/pages/457223126 "https://cmegroupclientsite.atlassian.net/wiki/spaces/EPICSANDBOX/pages/457223126"). Then CME Globex will send the iLink messages to [iLink Execution Report - Elimination](https://cmegroupclientsite.atlassian.net/wiki/spaces/EPICSANDBOX/pages/716177614 "https://cmegroupclientsite.atlassian.net/wiki/spaces/EPICSANDBOX/pages/716177614") orders. Next, client systems will experience MDP multicast, TCP iLink and Drop Copy disconnections. At this point, clients should reset their books. CME Group recommends that clients and vendors restart their applications at this time and go through their own restart processes.

##### **Startup**

iLink order entry, Drop Copy and market data sequence numbers reset to 1 during the [Saturday startup](https://cmegroupclientsite.atlassian.net/wiki/spaces/EPICSANDBOX/pages/1283194884/Cryptocurrency+Futures+and+Options+Migration+to+24-7+Trading#Startup "#Startup") cycle. Client systems will re-establish connectivity to the MDP multicast groups and receive the following startup messaging:

1.   The [MDP 3.0 - Market Data Snapshot](https://cmegroupclientsite.atlassian.net/wiki/spaces/EPICSANDBOX/pages/457574196 "https://cmegroupclientsite.atlassian.net/wiki/spaces/EPICSANDBOX/pages/457574196") (tag 35-MsgType=W) messages on the Market Recovery feed are deleted for the impacted 24/7 channel. Tag 83-RptSeq for Market by Price (MBP) book updates resets to 1 for each instrument repeating group.

2.   CME Globex disseminates incremental real-time market data for that channel and client systems can process the data. A [MDP 3.0 - Market Data Incremental Refresh](https://cmegroupclientsite.atlassian.net/wiki/spaces/EPICSANDBOX/pages/457226984 "https://cmegroupclientsite.atlassian.net/wiki/spaces/EPICSANDBOX/pages/457226984") (tag 35-MsgType=X) message will be sent with an empty End of Event indicator if no other information is available. If session statistics are available, they will be republished via the normal Incremental Refresh messaging:

    1.   Current session trade volume

    2.   Price levels in the order books

    3.   Highest and lowest trade

3.   [MDP 3.0 - Security Status](https://cmegroupclientsite.atlassian.net/wiki/spaces/EPICSANDBOX/pages/457223126 "https://cmegroupclientsite.atlassian.net/wiki/spaces/EPICSANDBOX/pages/457223126") messages containing market states (per instrument group) will be sent for the 24/7 channel. Then any instrument states that are different from the instrument group state are sent (e.g., Forbidden/Halted instruments).

Pre-registered administrative information will be removed during the Saturday maintenance window. Clients must re-submit their [iLink Party Details Definition Request](https://cmegroupclientsite.atlassian.net/wiki/spaces/EPICSANDBOX/pages/715587786 "https://cmegroupclientsite.atlassian.net/wiki/spaces/EPICSANDBOX/pages/715587786") following the pre-open on Saturday.

### New CME CF Rates and Indexes Channel

A new [SBE - Streamlined Market Data for CME CF Cryptocurrency Indices](https://cmegroupclientsite.atlassian.net/wiki/spaces/EPICSANDBOX/pages/457573131 "https://cmegroupclientsite.atlassian.net/wiki/spaces/EPICSANDBOX/pages/457573131") (Channel 214) will launch with this migration, offering 24/7 availability. The new channel will provide parallel data to the existing Streamline CME CF Rates and Indexes channel (213) during weekday operations.

*   View [connection information](https://www.cmegroup.com/solutions/market-access/globex/globex-resources.html#cfo247tmdmc "https://www.cmegroup.com/solutions/market-access/globex/globex-resources.html#cfo247tmdmc") (channel, subnet, multicast, port, IP). CME Group Login is required.

### New Streamlined Block Trades Channel

A new 7 day enabled [SBE - Streamlined Market Data for Block Trades](https://cmegroupclientsite.atlassian.net/wiki/spaces/EPICSANDBOX/pages/457094846 "https://cmegroupclientsite.atlassian.net/wiki/spaces/EPICSANDBOX/pages/457094846") (Channel 235) for 24/7 cryptocurrency futures and options will launch with this release. Availability will support the same 24/7 maintenance windows as Globex.

*   View [connection information](https://www.cmegroup.com/solutions/market-access/globex/globex-resources.html#cfo247tmdmc "https://www.cmegroup.com/solutions/market-access/globex/globex-resources.html#cfo247tmdmc") (channel, subnet, multicast, port, IP); new IPs and ports (CME Group Login is required).

## CME ClearPort

The [CME ClearPort](https://www.cmegroup.com/clearport.html "https://www.cmegroup.com/clearport.html") web-based user interface will support 24/7 trading.

**Block Trades** Entry is available through the ClearPort [platform](https://www.cmegroup.com/tools-information/webhelp/cme-platform/Content/Home.html "https://www.cmegroup.com/tools-information/webhelp/cme-platform/Content/Home.html")during the weekend. There are no specific weekend limits for ClearPort, however, a firm can adjust [RAV limits](https://www.cmegroup.com/tools-information/webhelp/account-manager-service/Content/manage-accounts.html#creditcontrols "https://www.cmegroup.com/tools-information/webhelp/account-manager-service/Content/manage-accounts.html#creditcontrols") prior to the start of the weekend session or over the weekend.

## Credit and Risk Management

With launch, CME Globex Credit Controls (GC2) will support unique GC2 [weekend Limits](https://www.cmegroup.com/tools-information/webhelp/globex-credit-controls/Content/CME-Globex-Credit-Controls-Management.html#weekend "https://www.cmegroup.com/tools-information/webhelp/globex-credit-controls/Content/CME-Globex-Credit-Controls-Management.html#weekend"). Client specified limits may affect weekend GT order cancellation. The GC2 weekday schedule for 5 day clients begins Sunday at 4:05 p.m. CT. At this time, 5 day client systems can start trading. For weekday limits, 5 day clients can trade during the expanded weekday schedule.

7-day clients should follow the standard product schedule.

For more information see the[CME Clearing & Global Operations Summary of Requirements and Guidelines for Cryptocurrency Trading and 24x7 Clearing](https://www.cmegroup.com/clearing/files/cryptocurrency-guidelines.pdf "https://www.cmegroup.com/clearing/files/cryptocurrency-guidelines.pdf").
