### Position Reporting Under CFTC Part 17

On April 30, 2024, the Commodity Futures Trading Commission ("CFTC") approved final rules to amend its [Large Trading Reporting Regulations for Futures and Options](https://www.cftc.gov/PressRoom/PressReleases/8902-24). The amendments were made to modernize CFTC's Large Trader Reporting process and align it with other reporting structures outlined in CFTC regulations. CFTC's final rules remove the 80-character submission standard, replacing it with a FIXML standard. The required FIXML position message includes a large set of instrument characteristics as well as unique contract identifiers, Unique Contract Identifiers must be provided to Reporting Firms by the exchange where the positions are held.

CME, CBOT, NYMEX, and COMEX (each an "Exchange" and together the "Exchanges") will be providing Reporting Firms files containing Unique Instrument Codes in compliance with Large Trader Reporting requirements. The Exchanges are offering two methods to download this instrument information, as described in the [CME Group Client Systems Wiki](https://cmegroupclientsite.atlassian.net/wiki/spaces/EPICSANDBOX/pages/259457031/Position+Reporting+Under+CFTC+Part+17).

The Market Regulation Department will be publishing further guidance regarding Large Trader Reporting in subsequent Special Executive Reports as solutions become available.

CFTC's final rules became effective upon publication in the Federal Register on June 3, 2024. Reporting Firms must comply with the CFTC's final rules regarding Large Trader Reporting Requirements two years after publication in the Federal Register.

### MDP 3.0 SBE Schema Update- November 24

To support future enhancements for futures and options markets, including CME FX Spot+, on CME Globex MDP 3.0 and CME Smart Stream on GCP SBE, the update to Globex MDP SBE core schema version 13 has began and will conclude **Sunday, January 12, 2025** (trade date Monday, January 13, 2025). The next scheduled schema update will occur on **Sunday, November 24, 2024**.

Futures and options channels on CME Globex are currently utilizing core schema version 9. To ease customer development efforts, the new SBE schema files will support [template extension](https://cmegroupclientsite.atlassian.net/wiki/spaces/EPICSANDBOX/pages/46108407/MDP+3.0+-+Message+Schema#Template-Extension) for MDP 3.0 messages. For client systems that do not support template extension, the schema update is not backward compatible, and the MDP 3.0 messages sent from CME Globex cannot be decoded.

In conjunction with the core schema futures and options update, the futures and options markets will leverage a new separate schema dedicated to [TCP recovery for UDP](https://cmegroupclientsite.atlassian.net/wiki/spaces/EPICSANDBOX/pages/46466060/MDP+3.0+-+TCP+Recovery) effective **Sunday, November 24, 2024.**

Please review the [client impact assessment](https://cmegroupclientsite.atlassian.net/wiki/spaces/EPICSANDBOX/pages/145489930/Futures+and+Options+SBE+Schema+Updates) for full technical details and launch schedule.

### Update CME FX Spot+- March 2025

† Denotes update to the article

Effective**March 2025**, CME Group will launch CME FX Spot Plus (CME FX Spot+), a new tradable spot FX instrument, leveraging CME FX Link liquidity to bring together OTC spot FX and FX futures.

CME FX Spot+ will enable spot FX market participants to tap into CME FX futures liquidity through an open, transparent central limit order book while giving FX futures users broader access to OTC liquidity. This unique liquidity connection will be made possible by implied matching through CME FX Link, a liquid, tradable spread between OTC spot FX and FX futures.

The FX Spot+ products will be available for the following currency pairs: EUR/USD, GBP/USD, AUD/USD, NZD/USD, USD/JPY, USD/CAD, USD/CHF, and USD/MXN.

All of the CME FX Spot+ products will be available in the New Release environment for customer testing on **Monday, November 11, 2024**.

More information on these new products and the associated messaging and functionality enhancements is available in the [CME FX Spot+ client impact assessment](https://cmegroupclientsite.atlassian.net/wiki/spaces/EPICSANDBOX/pages/132775953/CME+FX+Spot+Plus). A new [CME FX Spot+ Credit Overview](https://cmegroupclientsite.atlassian.net/wiki/spaces/EPICSANDBOX/pages/132251681/CME+FX+Link+and+FX+Spot+Plus+Credit+Overview) is also available with credit features and details for both FX Link and CME FX Spot+.

### Messaging Efficiency Program (MEP) Mass Quote Shift Revision for Select Equity Options- Starting This Friday

In an effort to better accommodate market participants during extended trading hours (ETH), starting **Thursday, October 31** (trade date Friday, November 1), CME Group will no longer enforce Mass Quote (MQ) scoring and product group benchmarks on Globex during ETH only. ETH represents all non RTH hours that the Globex platform is open for trading.

Mass Quote (MQ) scoring and product group benchmarks on Globex**will continue to be enforced during Regular Trading Hours (RTH)**. RTH represents the hours between 7 a.m. Central Time (CT) - 4 p.m. CT.

Please contact the [Global Command Center (GCC)](mailto:gcc@cmegroup.com) in the US at 1 800 438 8616, in Europe at +44 20 7623 4747 or in Asia at +65 6532 5010 or [Global Account Management](mailto:gam@cmegroup.com) with any questions.

### CME STP API on Google Cloud- This Week

CME Group is launching CME STP API services on Google Cloud. This API launch will happen in phases.

As part of phase 1, CME STP FIXML service is now available on Google Cloud and clients may target the new service URL.

| ENVIRONMENT | CURRENT SERVICE URL | NEW SERVICE URL |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Production | https://services.cmegroup.com/cmestp/query | https://posttrade.api.cmegroup.com/cmestp/query |

Both service URLs will be available in parallel until close of business this Friday, November 1. Effective this **Sunday, November 3** (trade date Monday, November 4) current service URL will be decommissioned. During the parallel period, same trade data will be available on both services and clients should take steps to avoid double booking trades within their systems.

With this launch,

*   Clients can use their currently entitled API IDs to target new service URLs.
*   There are no changes to CME STP FIXML API message specifications.

The new service URL for the New Release is now available for customer testing.

| ENVIRONMENT | CURRENT SERVICE URL | NEW SERVICE URL |
| --- | --- | --- |
| New Release | https://servicesnr.cmegroup.com/cmestp/query | https://posttrade.api.uat.cmegroup.com/cmestp/query |

Clients are highly encouraged to test their CME STP FIXML implementations against the new service URL.

Additional information is available in the [client impact assessment](https://www.cmegroup.com/confluence/display/EPICSANDBOX/CME+STP+on+Google+Cloud).

Launch dates for CME STP FIX API will be announced in future CME STP Notices.
