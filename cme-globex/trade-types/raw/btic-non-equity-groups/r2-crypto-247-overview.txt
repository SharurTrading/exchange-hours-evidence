## Good Till Cancel / Good Till Date Order Support 

For the first time CME Globex will support GTC / GTD orders during weekend trading! ● Key challenge: How can 5 day and 7 day clients manage resting GTC / GTD orders? 

Solution 

● New Functionality: CME Globex Credit Controls (GC2) will support automatic GT elimination functionality via a distinct weekend GC2 limit at the execution firm level. ● GC2 Weekend Trading Configuration 

○ Accounts default to a 5-day support schedule (zero weekend limit). 

Good till when? 

Weekend GFIDs NOT Enabled Weekend GFIDs Enabled 

Cancellation Time: GTC / GTD orders for these products will be canceled every Friday at 4:00 p.m. CT .

Scope: This cancellation applies only to products designated as "7-Day Products" and is enforced at the GFID level. 

iLink 7 day session: GT Orders submitted through 7 day GFIDs will remain active and manageable in the market. 

iLink 5 day Session: GT Orders submitted through 5 day session will remain working, although not accessible via an iLink session. © 2026 CME Group. All rights reserved. CME GROUP PUBLIC

# 5-Day vs. 7-Day Client Journey Summary 

5-Day Cryptocurrency Clients 7-Day Cryptocurrency Clients 

Weekday Schedule Update Daily maintenance window reduced from one hour to one minute. 

Weekend Schedule Expansion N/A Clients must successfully process the new Saturday startup. 

CME Globex Sunday Startup Decommission 

Sunday startup is no longer applicable, startup moves to Saturday. 

GTC) / GTD Order Support 

Default : GTC / GTD orders are eliminated on Friday. GC2 Opt-in : FCMs enable weekend support to retain GTC / GTD orders. 

iLink & Drop Copy CGW Session Support 

No Impact - clients can use existing CGW sessions to continue to trade cryptocurrency products during the weekday. New CGW session required to support weekend trading and all CME Globex futures and options markets (e.g. interest rates, energy, etc) during the weekday. 

iLink & Drop Copy MSGW Session Support 

No impact - clients can use existing MSGW sessions.
