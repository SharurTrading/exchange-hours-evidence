NewCME FX Tape+ Over WebSocket API - April 27

Effective Monday, April 27, CME Group will launch CME FX Tape+ over WebSocket API to provide centralized reference prices and a comprehensive view of FX market liquidity from its transparent central limit order book (CLOB) marketplaces, including FX futures, EBS Market, FX Spot+‌ and FX Link.

CME FX Tape+ over WebSocket API offers:

  • Both actual and derived data points to provide customers a comprehensive view of CME Group’s FX Markets in a single market data feed
  • Best bid/offer prices, mid price, trade price and quantity
  • Highs, low, open and close for each market and instrument

CME FX Tape+ will cover G10 currency pairs initially and will include streaming data approximately 250 milliseconds conflation rate distributed via WebSocket API.

7 Day iLink CGW Sessions - May 3

Effective Sunday, May 3 (trade date Monday, May 4), new 7 day (7D) sessions will be required for all iLink and Drop Copy Convenience Gateway (CGW) users to participate in CME Globex 24/7 weekend trading. These 7D CGW sessions can be used to trade all 5 day and 7 day products moving forward.

iLink CGW users must create new 7D CGW sessions, which are now available in the Request Center (ESS), but will not be available for trading until Sunday, May 3.

Drop Copy CGW users must update their configuration to include a new dedicated target session, which will be auto-created by the Drop Copy service to support 7D CGW sessions.

Existing 5 day (5D) CGW sessions may be used to trade 7 day products from Sunday at 5:00 p.m. Central Time (CT) through Friday at 4:00 p.m. CT, but will be restricted from extended weekend trading hours.

To facilitate the transition, CME Group will waive Port Activation Charges (PAC) for all 7D sessions through July 2026. Monthly Inactivity Charges (MIC) remain in effect; a minimum of 100 traded contracts per month is required to avoid the $100 service charge.

Please Note: 7D sessions will not be available for weekend testing (mock trading sessions). Firms interested in weekend testing should retain a small number of 5D CGW sessions for this purpose. CME Group will waive the MIC for up to five 5D CGW sessions, maintained specifically for testing, per CME Globex Firm ID (GFID).

iLink and Drop Copy Market Segment Gateway (MSGW) sessions are unaffected and can access all 24/7 products, provided the associated GFID is enabled by the responsible FCM.

Please contact Global Account Management with any questions.

Position Reporting Under CFTC Part 17

On April 30, 2024, the Commodity Futures Trading Commission ("CFTC") approved final rules to amend its Large Trading Reporting Regulations for Futures and Options. The amendments were made to modernize CFTC's Large Trader Reporting process and align it with other reporting structures outlined in CFTC regulations.

Since October 2024, CME Group has offered methods to retrieve its instrument listing containing the Unique Instrument Code (UIC) and other instrument data elements in compliance with the amended rule.

Starting March 10, 2025, CME Group began accepting test files in the new Position Message FIXML format via SFTP. For details regarding submitting the new FIXML Position Message, file management, field validations, and example scenarios, please refer to the information in the CME Group Client Systems Wiki.

The Market Regulation Department will be publishing further guidance regarding Large Trader Reporting in subsequent Special Executive Reports as solutions become available.

CFTC's final rules became effective upon publication in the Federal Register on June 3, 2024. Subject to the conditions of the January 27, 2026, no-action letter, reporting firms must comply with these CFTC regulations by July 26, 2027.

CME Globex Connectivity Upgrades and Changes for Listed Derivative Markets - June 27

In response to client feedback and to provide firms with additional time to prepare, CME Group extended the deadline for the previously announced network equipment upgrades.

Starting Saturday, June 27, CME Group plans to replace End-of-Life network equipment that supports listed derivatives connectivity at CME Globex Hubs. Customers will be notified in advance if they are impacted by an equipment upgrade.

CME Globex market data for listed derivatives now regularly exceeds 1Gbps, increasing the risk of packet loss, performance concerns, and quality of service degradations on 1Gbps connections.

Impact on 1Gbps Listed Derivatives Connections

  • Multicast Market Data: Effective June 27, 1Gbps services will no longer support MDP 3.0 multicast market data.
  • Existing 1Gbps Customers with Market Data: Customers with 1Gbps service currently listening to multicast market data are encouraged to upgrade connections to 10Gbps as soon as possible.
  • Existing 1Gbps Customers without Market Data: Unicast services such as iLink, Drop Copy, STP, MQ and others will continue to be supported on 1Gbps connections. Customers with 1Gbps connectivity who are not receiving Market Data will have their service migrated to the new equipment.
  • New 1Gbps Connections: New connections for 1Gbps service do not support access to listed derivatives multicast market data.

Copper Handoffs

  • When the CME Globex Connectivity upgrades are rolled out in 2026, CME Group will no longer support copper handoffs.

Additional details about the CME Globex Connectivity upgrades will be announced in future CME Globex Notices.

Please contact your Global Account Manager for additional information.

CME STP FIX on Google Cloud - Production Internet Connectivity Migration Deadline - July 31

As part of a phased migration of CME Straight Through Processing APIs to Google Cloud, Production internet connectivity options for CME STP FIX API are now available .

Close of business Friday, July 31, all clients must complete migration and connect to CME STP FIX API Production using only the Google Cloud hostnames.

CME STP FIX on Google Cloud is available in parallel to the current on-prem instance. When connecting in parallel to Google Cloud and on-prem instances, clients should ensure trades are not double-booked.

A mandatory supplemental certification for message recovery is now available for CME STP FIX on Google Cloud. 

Please contact Certification Support for Electronic Trading (CSET) with any questions in the U.S. at +1 312 930 2322, in Europe at +44 20 3379 3803 or in Asia at +65 6593 5593.
