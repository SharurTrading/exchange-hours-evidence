Starting at 4 p.m. Central Time on Friday, May 29, 2026, CME Group will expand cryptocurrency futures and options to 24/7 trading.Cryptocurrency futures and options will trade continuously on CME Globex with at least a two-hour weekly maintenance period occurring on the weekend. All holiday or weekend trading from Friday evening through Sunday evening will have a trade date of the following business day, with clearing, settlement and regulatory reporting processed the following business day.This topic outlines the necessary information for processing cryptocurrency 24/7 trading.Contents16falsedefaultlisttrue

## Saturday Maintenance Window Processing OverviewThe following section outlines Saturday maintenance processing for 24/7 trading.
## Saturday Shutdown and Startup
## ShutdownAt the beginning of the maintenance period, client systems will receive a MDP 3.0 - Security Status. Then CME Globex will send the iLink messages to iLink Execution Report - Elimination orders. Next, client systems will experience MDP multicast, TCP iLink and Drop Copy disconnections. At this point, clients should reset their books. CME Group recommends that clients and vendors restart their applications at this time and go through their own restart processes.
## StartupiLink order entry, Drop Copy and market data sequence numbers reset to 1 during the Saturday startup cycle. Client systems will re-establish connectivity to the MDP multicast groups and receive the following startup messaging: The MDP 3.0 - Market Data Snapshot (tag 35-MsgType=W) messages on the Market Recovery feed are deleted for the impacted 24/7 channel. Tag 83-RptSeq for Market by Price (MBP) book updates resets to 1 for each instrument repeating group.CME Globex disseminates incremental real-time market data for that channel and client systems can process the data. A MDP 3.0 - Market Data Incremental Refresh (tag 35-MsgType=X) message will be sent with an empty End of Event indicator if no other information is available.  If session statistics are available, they will be republished via the normal Incremental Refresh messaging:Current session trade volumePrice levels in the order booksHighest and lowest tradeMDP 3.0 - Security Status messages containing market states (per instrument group) will be sent for the 24/7 channel. Then any instrument states that are different from the instrument group state are sent (e.g., Forbidden/Halted instruments). Pre-registered administrative information will be removed during the Saturday maintenance window. Clients must re-submit their iLink Party Details Definition Request following the pre-open on Saturday.

## 24/7 Client System ImpactsThis section outlines 24/7 client system impacts. 
## iLink Order EntryAt the beginning of the Saturday extended maintenance window, CME Globex will disconnect all clients' sessions; reset sequence numbers; and cancel resting day orders and mass quotes. Pre-registered administrative information will be removed during the Saturday maintenance window. Clients must re-submit their Party Details Definition following the pre-open on Saturday. 
## Market Segment Gateway (MSGW)All existing iLink Order Entry functionality is supported. Client systems may leverage existing iLink MSGW sessions. 
## Convenience Gateway (CGW) With this launch, iLink CGW sessions will support seven day markets. New CGW sessions must be created to support 24/7 trading. Newly created CGW sessions will support all Globex futures and options markets. Existing CGW sessions can only submit cryptocurrency futures and options orders during the weekday.

## CME ClearPortThe CME ClearPort web-based user interface will support 24/7 trading. Block Trades Entry is available through the ClearPort platform during the weekend. There are no specific weekend limits for ClearPort, however, a firm can adjust RAV limits prior to the start of the weekend session or over the weekend.

## Credit and Risk ManagementWith launch, CME Globex Credit Controls (GC2) will support unique GC2 weekend Limits. Client specified limits may affect weekend GT order cancellation. The GC2 weekday schedule for 5 day clients begins Sunday at 4:05 p.m. CT. At this time, 5 day client systems can start trading. For weekday limits, 5 day clients can trade during the expanded weekday schedule.7-day clients should follow the standard product schedule.For more information see the CME Clearing & Global Operations Summary of Requirements and Guidelines for Cryptocurrency Trading and 24x7 Clearing.

## Summary of Impacts for 5 Day Cryptocurrency TradingThe following table summarizes impacts for clients who choose not to support 24/7 trading.Type | Impact | 
Schedule update | The GC2 weekday schedule for 5 day clients begins Sunday at 4:05 p.m. CT. At this time, 5 day client systems can start trading. For weekday limits, 5 day clients can trade during the expanded weekday schedule. 7-day clients should follow the standard product schedule. | 
CME Globex Sunday startup | Currently 5 day cryptocurrency futures and options clients can utilize CME Globex Start-up Processing. However, due to the new 7 day trading schedule there is no longer a Globex Sunday startup processing cycle. | 
Convenience Gateway (CGW) Support | Clients can continue to use 5 day CGW sessions, however, order submission is not available until 4:05 p.m. CT on Sunday. If a client wishes to trade between Friday 4:00 p.m. CT - Sunday 4:00 p.m. CT, a new CGW session must be created. | 
Good Till (GT) Order Elimination | If weekend trading is not enabled in CME Globex Credit Controls (GC2), GT orders will be eliminated on Friday at 4:00 p.m. CT. All client systems are encouraged to cancel all cryptocurrency futures and options GT orders before Friday 4:00 p.m. CT. |
