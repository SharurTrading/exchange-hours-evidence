# Special Executive Report 

## DATE: May 28, 2026 SER#: 9740R SUBJECT: Expansion of the Trading and Clearing Hours for all Cryptocurrency Futures and Options on Futures Contracts 

(SER 9740R supersedes SER 9740 dated May 13, 2026 and is being issued to include Event Contracts on Bitcoin Futures which is provided in blackline format in Table 1. below. No other changes have been made to the original SER.)
