Currently, when the spread trades at a positive price, the leg2 (the deferred expiration)acts as the anchor leg. In addition, a new BTIC on Adjusted Interest Rate FTSE-100 Total Return Index futures calendar spread will be listed for trading on CME Globex. Please review the [client impact assessment](https://www.cmegroup.com/confluence/display/EPICSANDBOX/EC+Calendar+Spread+Leg+Pricing+Enhancement)for additional details. These changes are currently available for customer testing in New Release.
