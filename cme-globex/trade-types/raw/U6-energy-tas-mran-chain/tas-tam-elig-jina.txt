The column headings represent the first day of the month. The cells display the TAS eligible contract months and spreads as of the first day of the month.

TAS eligibility terminates one day prior to the last Friday of the contract month. If that day is not both a U.K. and U.S. business day, TAS eligibilty terminates on the prior day that is a U.K. and U.S. business day.

Any TAS trades submitted during weekend sessions are marked to the settlement price of the next business day.
