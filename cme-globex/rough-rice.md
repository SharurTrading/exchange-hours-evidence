# CBOT Rough Rice (ZR) — a CBOT grain that tracked the standard grain/oilseed grid from the 2010 floor until 2018-01-21, then diverged permanently. It requires its own key.

**Products:** Rough Rice Futures — CME Globex code ZR, CME ClearPort/Clearing code 14, CBOT Rulebook Chapter 17. Rough Rice Options — CME Globex code OZR (iLink tag 55-Symbol "RR"), CBOT Rulebook Chapter 17A; the options carry the identical Globex envelope and were changed by every notice below alongside the futures. Historically also traded by open outcry (pit symbol RR) until the CBOT futures pits closed by 2015-07-02.

**Timezone:** America/Chicago

**Confidence:** high

## Reuse verdict

ZR NEEDS ITS OWN KEY. It must not reuse the standard CBOT grains key.

Why, from product-family semantics and history rather than from a coincidentally matching envelope:

1. The divergence is a deliberate, product-specific exchange action, not an artifact. CBOT filed a standalone CFTC certification for Rough Rice ALONE (Submission 18-001, "Reduction of the Extended Trading Hours for Rough Rice Futures and Options Contracts"), on the basis of a rough-rice-industry survey, to concentrate liquidity in a thin market. Nothing about it is shared with corn/wheat/soy/oats. A key that lumps ZR with the grains would be asserting a relationship the exchange explicitly severed.

2. The divergence is live and current, and it is structural, not a five-minute edge case. Read the same day (2026-09-05) from CME's own spec pages: ZR extended = Sun-Thu 19:00-21:00 CT; Oats and Corn extended = Sun-Fri 19:00-07:45 CT. ZR's evening leg is 2 hours where the grains' is 12h45m. ZR has NO midnight-wrapping session at all since 2018-01-22, whereas every other CBOT grain does — that is a different session topology, not a different boundary. A caller resolving "is ZR open at 02:00 CT" gets the wrong answer on every single overnight for eight years and counting.

3. Nothing suggests re-convergence. CME's annual CFTC rule-filing indexes for 2018-2026 contain exactly one Rough Rice hours filing (the 2018 one) and no grain/oilseed hours filing that would restore ZR. The only other Rough Rice filings in that period are a market-maker program (2021, 21-458), price-limit rules (2023, 23-083) and annual milling-yield schedules — none touch hours.

4. HOWEVER — the history is genuinely shared up to the divergence, and the crate should model it that way rather than duplicating a parallel timeline by hand. From the January-2010 floor through 2018-01-21, ZR tracked the standard CBOT grain/oilseed grid EXACTLY through all three reorganisations, and CME named Rough Rice in each source document: the 2012-05-20 expansion to 17:00-14:00 CT, the 2013-04-07 split into 19:00-07:45 + 08:30-13:15, and the 2015-07-05 move of the RTH close to 13:20. There is no ZR-specific deviation anywhere in that span. So the correct shape is a ZR key whose revision list is identical to the grains key through 2015-07-05 and then adds one further revision at 2018-01-21 that the grains key does not have.

5. Do NOT key ZR together with the four mini-sized CBOT ag futures (XC, XW, XK, MKC) either. Those were separately truncated and then RE-ALIGNED to the standard grains grid effective 2022-10-03 (SER-9049), so they are on the grains envelope today and would drag ZR back to the wrong overnight.

One caveat that bears on key design: ZR is currently the only CBOT grain with this envelope, so any "shared key" for it would have exactly one member. If the crate's convention is to name keys after the venue-plus-product-family rather than after the shape, a dedicated ZR key (e.g. CBOT rough rice) is the honest encoding.

## Dated revisions

### 2012-05-20 — Globex expands from the split 18:00-07:15 + 09:30-13:15 CT grid to one continuous Sun-Fri 17:00-14:00 CT session (21 hours). Venue-local cutover day is Sunday 2012-05-20 (the session that opens that evening carries trade date Monday 2012-05-21). In model terms the extended leg becomes [61200, 86400) wrapping into [0, 50400) with no intraday break; the pit (a separate venue) still ran 09:30-13:15 CT. Rough Rice is explicitly one of the affected products in the accompanying CBOT rule certification.
