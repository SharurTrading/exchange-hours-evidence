L52: All times are local.
L53:   1. Last five minutes: no order matching; final uncross takes place randomly during the last 30 seconds of this five-minute period.
L54:   2. A pre-trading session in Swedish index futures starts 30 minutes prior to the start of continuous trading and lasts for 25 minutes.
L55:   3. An opening call auction in Danish, Norwegian and Swedish index futures that starts five minutes prior to and ends in the transition to continuous trading.
L56:   4. After the end of continuous trading, there is a closing call auction in index futures that lasts for 90 to 120 seconds for Danish and Norwegian index futures and 150 to 180 seconds for Swedish index futures.
L57:   5. A post-trading session in Swedish index futures starts five minutes after end of continuous trading and lasts for 30 minutes on Swedish index futures.
L58:   6. Clearing house opening hours for all cleared markets. On a half-trading day, the clearing hours remain the same from 07:00-19:25 CET.
