*   ### Trade sports performance

Capture shifting performance by taking long or short positions on NHL and MLB teams, pivoting in real time as the game or season unfolds.  
*   ### 24/7 market access

React to draft picks, trades, lineup changes and news cycles instantly. Express your view on your schedule – day or night – without waiting for the end of a game or season.  
*   ### Capital-efficient exposure

Access larger market positions with less upfront capital by trading futures with margin.  

*   ### Deep bench for savvy traders

Strengthen your investment portfolio with Sports Index futures – a pure-play asset class that moves independently of inflation and interest rates.  
*   ### Level playing field

Trade and hedge with confidence in a CFTC-regulated marketplace, where you can enjoy the same financial safeguards and transparent pricing as the world's largest institutions.  
*   ### Track sports like a stock index

CME FutureSports Performance Indexes transform officially reported, live professional statistics into rules-based financial benchmarks that move with the season.

How can Sports Index futures be used?

Besides diversification, these contracts will offer multiple applications, including:

*   Hedge against the financial impact exposure to a team's performance can have on your bottom line.
*   Buy and sell contracts to capture live, in-game and in-season momentum shifts.
*   Trade contracts to invest in a franchise's broader, season-long narrative.

How will the futures market work?

Sports Index futures will be 24/7 markets financially settled to the index, offering versatile usage. For example, you can buy and go long if you anticipate a rise in team performance, or sell and go short if you expect a decline. Manage risk and capture real-time moves from the start of a game until time has expired, or between matchups.

Who is FutureSports?

[FutureSports](https://www.futuresports.com/) is an independent index administrator transforming professional and college sports statistics into rules-based, benchmark financial indexes that may be referenced by exchange-listed financial products.
