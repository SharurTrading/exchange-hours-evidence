# Special Executive Report 

DATE: October 23, 2026 SER#: 9832RR SUBJECT: Basis Trade at Cash Open (“TACO”) Transaction Eligibility for the Nikkei Stock Average Futures and Yen Denominated Nikkei Stock Average Futures Contracts 

(SER 9832RR supersedes SER 9832R dated October 16, 2026 and is being issued to replace Appendix C with the correct CME Globex trading hours. No other changes have been made to SER 9832R.) Effective Sunday, November 15, 2026, for trade date Monday, November 16, 2026, and pending all relevant Commodity Futures Trading Commission (“CFTC” or “Commission”) regulatory review periods, Chicago Mercantile Exchange Inc. (“CME” or “Exchange”) will permit the eligibility of Basis Trade at Cash Open (“TACO”) transactions for the Nikkei Stock Average Futures and Yen Denominated Nikkei Stock Average Futures contracts (the “Contracts”), for trading on the CME Globex electronic trading platform (“CME Globex”) as noted in Table 1. below 

Table 1. 

The Contracts are currently available for trading on CME Globex and for submission for clearing via CME ClearPort. TACO eligibility for the Contracts will only be available for trading and CME Globex. TACO transactions on the Contracts will not be block eligible. In order to accommodate the TACO eligibility for the Contracts, the Exchange will amend CME Rule 35206. and Rule 352B06. of the Nikkei Stock Average Futures and Yen Denominated Nikkei Stock Average Futures product chapters respectively to include language regarding TACO transactions (collectively, the “Rule Amendments”). TACO is an Exchange futures pricing mechanism in which the price of the futures contract, which has been designated by the Exchange as eligible for TACO transactions, is quoted and traded by reference to the next following regularly scheduled opening special quotation (“SQ”) of such futures contract’s underlying cash Index (the “Basis”) (CME Rule 524. Section C. “Basis Trade at Cash Open (“TACO”) Transactions”). Note that SOQ is called SQ in the context of Nikkei and SQ is typically published on Wednesdays and Fridays on the Japan Exchange Group.

## Chapter 352 Nikkei Stock Average Futures 

*** 35206. BASIS TRADE AT INDEX CLOSE (“BTIC”) AND BASIS TRADE AT CASH OPEN (“TACO”) TRANSACTIONS 

All BTIC transactions and TACO transactions must be executed in accordance with the requirements of Rules 524.B and 524.C. Notwithstanding the provisions in Rule 524.B.2 and Rule 524.C.1, BTIC and TACO transactions are permitted on the last day of trading for the contract. 

35206.A. BTIC Trade Requirements 1. BTIC block trades must be executed in accordance with the requirements of Rule 526. For a BTIC or BTIC block trade executed on a given Trading Day on or before the scheduled close of the Primary Listing Exchange, the corresponding futures price shall be made by reference to the Index closing value for the current Trading Day. 

2. For a TACO trade executed on a given Trading Day on or before the scheduled opening auction on the Primary Listing Exchange, the corresponding futures price shall be made by reference to the Index opening special quotation (“SQ”) for such Trading Day. For a TACO trade executed on a given Trading Day after the scheduled start of the opening auction on the Primary Listing Exchange, the corresponding futures price shall be made by reference to the SQ for the next Trading Day. TACO is not block eligible. 

35206.B. BTIC Price Assignment Procedures 1. The price assignment procedures for BTIC transactions shall follow Rule 524.B.3. 

2. The price assignment procedures for TACO transactions shall follow Rule 524.C.3. The futures price of a TACO transaction made by reference to the Index SQ for a given Trading Day shall be determined by the Exchange following the publication of the Index SQ. 

35206.C. BTIC Minimum Price Increments 

The minimum price increment shall be 0.1 Index point, equal to $0.5 per contract, for any basis adjustment that is applied to the Index closing level to establish the futures price of a BTIC transaction or to the Index SQ to establish the TACO futures price. 

35206.D. Market Disruption Events 

In the event of a market disruption in the Primary Listing Exchange, all pending and executed BTIC and TACO transactions in the corresponding futures contract shall be cancelled by the Exchange. Such disruption shall be declared by the Exchange in its sole discretion, and may include, without limitation, an unscheduled early close of the Primary Listing Exchange or a regulatory trading halt necessitating an early closure of the Primary Listing Exchange. (End Chapter 352) Chapter 352B Yen Denominated Nikkei Stock Average Futures 

*** 352B06. BASIS TRADE AT INDEX CLOSE (“BTIC”) AND BASIS TRADE AT CASH OPEN (“TACO”) TRANSACTIONS 

All BTIC transactions and TACO transactions must be executed in accordance with the requirements of Rule 524.B and 524.C. Notwithstanding the provisions in Rule 524.B.2. and Rule 524.C.1, BTIC and TACO transactions are permitted on the last day of trading for the contract. 

352B06.A. BTIC Trade Requirements 1. BTIC block trades must be executed in accordance with the requirements of Rule 526. For a BTIC or BTIC block trade executed on a given Trading Day on or before the scheduled close of the Primary Listing Exchange, the corresponding futures price shall be made by reference to the Index closing value for the current Trading Day. For a BTIC or BTIC block trade executed on a given Trading Day after the scheduled close of the Primary Listing Exchange, the corresponding futures price shall be made by reference to the Index closing value for the next Trading Day. 

2. For a TACO executed on a given Trading Day on or before the scheduled opening auction on the Primary Listing Exchange, the corresponding futures price shall be made by reference to the Index opening special quotation (“SQ”) for such Trading Day. For a TACO trade executed on a given Trading Day after the scheduled start of the opening auction on the Primary Listing Exchange, the corresponding futures price shall be made by reference to the SQ for the next Trading Day. TACO is not block eligible. 

352B06.B. BTIC Price Assignment Procedures 1. The price assignment procedures for BTIC transactions shall follow Rule 524.B.3. 

2. The price assignment procedures for TACO transactions shall follow Rule 524.C.3. The futures price of a TACO transaction made by reference to the Index SQ for a given Trading Day shall be determined by the Exchange following the publication of the Index SQ. 

352B06.C. BTIC Minimum Price Increments 

The minimum price increment shall be 0.1 Index point, equal to ¥50 per contract, for any basis adjustment that is applied to the Index closing level to establish the futures price of a BTIC transaction or to the Index SQ to establish the TACO futures price. 

352B06.D. Market Disruption Events 

In the event of a market disruption in the Primary Listing Exchange, all pending and executed BTIC and TACO transactions in the corresponding futures contract shall be cancelled by the Exchange. Such disruption shall be declared by the Exchange in its sole discretion, and may include, without limitation, an unscheduled early close of the Primary Listing Exchange or a regulatory trading halt necessitating an early closure of the Primary Listing Exchange.
